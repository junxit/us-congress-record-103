# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Mfume, Kweisi (M000687) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.Res. 336, H.R. 3759

```
[Congressional Record: February 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  (Mr. MFUME asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. MFUME. Mr. Speaker, earlier today, for several hours, I was 
unavoidably detained in my district and was thus unable to be present 
for several votes. Had I been here, I would have voted ``aye'' on 
rollcall vote No. 6, ordering the previous question; I would have voted 
``aye'' on rollcall vote No. 7, on passage of House Resolution 336, a 
resolution providing for the consideration of H.R. 3759, the earthquake 
emergency supplemental; I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall vote No. 
8, the amendment to H.R. 3759; and I would have voted ``no'' on 
rollcall vote No. 9, the Myers amendment to H.R. 3759.

                          ____________________
```
