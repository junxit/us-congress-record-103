# IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: February 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
            IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business yesterday, 
February 2, the Federal debt stood at $4,524,359,010,160.50, meaning 
that on a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$17,353.92 as his or her share of that debt.
```
