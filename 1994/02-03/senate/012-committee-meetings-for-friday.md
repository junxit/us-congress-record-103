                     COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR FRIDAY,

                            FEBRUARY 4, 1994

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 S E N A T E

  Committee on Environment and Public Works, to hold hearings on the 
nominations of Brig. Gen. Eugene S. Witherspoon, USA, to be a 
Member and President of the Mississippi River Commission, Richard 
Thomas Moore, of Massachusetts, to be Associate Director of the

Carolina, to be Federal Co-Chairman of the Appalachian Regional 
Commission, and William W. Ginsberg, of Connecticut, to be an 
Assistant Secretary of Commerce, 10 a.m., SD-406.
  Committee on Foreign Relations, to hold hearings on the 
nominations of David N. Merrill, of Maryland, to be Ambassador to 
the People's Republic of Bangladesh, and Sandra L. Vogelgesang, of 
Ohio, to be Ambassador to the Kingdom of Nepal, 10 a.m., SD-419.
  Subcommittee on Near Eastern and South Asian Affairs, to hold 
hearings to examine current developments in South Asia, 11 a.m., SD-
419.
  Committee on Labor and Human Resources, to resume hearings on 
provisions of S. 1757, to ensure individual and family security 
through health care coverage for all Americans that is affordable 
and comprehensive, focusing on guaranteed benefits (pending on 
Senate Calendar), 9:30 a.m., SD-430.

  Subcommittee on Legislative, on GAO, 9:30 a.m., H-301 Capitol.
  Committee on the Judiciary, Subcommittee on Civil and

  Committee on Ways and Means, Subcommittee on Health, to continue 
hearings on Health Care Reform: The Administration's Health Care 
Reform Proposals, 10 a.m., 1100 Longworth.


                             Joint Meetings

  Joint Economic Committee, to hold hearings to examine the

Rayburn Building.
