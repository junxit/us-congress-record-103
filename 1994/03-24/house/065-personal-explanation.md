# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Barrett, Bill (B000179) [R-NE], Kildee, Dale E. (K000172) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
[Congressional Record: March 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. BARRETT of Nebraska. Mr. Chairman, I was unavoidably detained and 
missed rollcall vote number 93. Had I been present, I would have voted 
``yes'' on the amendment offered by the gentleman from Florida [Mr. 
Miller].
  Mr. KILDEE. Mr. Chairman, I move that the Committee do now rise.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Accordingly, the Committee rose; and the Speaker pro tempore [Mr. 
Hughes] having assumed the chair, Mr. Price of North Carolina, Chairman 
of the Committee of the Whole House on the State of the Union, reported 
that that Committee, having had under consideration the bill (H.R. 6) 
to extend for 6 years the authorizations of appropriations for the 
programs under the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965, and 
for certain other purposes, had come to no resolution thereon.
```
