# PRESIDENT, BLAME THYSELF

**Speaking:** Ballenger, Cass (B000104) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: March 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        PRESIDENT, BLAME THYSELF

  (Mr. BALLENGER asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BALLENGER. Mr. President, blame thyself.
  Tonight, President Clinton will seek to blame Republicans for every 
ill that has stricken his administration.
  He will blame Republicans for the sad fact that the Democrats could 
not complete a crime bill before the Easter recess.
  He will blame Republicans for his Whitewater fiasco.
  He will blame Republicans for the fact his health care plan was 
supported by no Democrats in a vote yesterday.
  And he will blame Republicans because another member of his staff is 
under fire for ethics problems.
  But, Mr. Speaker, the President has no one to blame but himself.
  For this, Republicans are blameless. Mr. President, blame thyself.

                          ____________________
```
