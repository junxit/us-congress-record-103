# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 1636, H.R. 3345

```
[Congressional Record: March 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Hallen, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate agrees to the Report of the Committee of Conference on 
the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendment of the House 
to the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 3345) ``An act to 
provide temporary authority to Government agencies relating to 
voluntary separation incentive payments, and for other purposes.''.
  The message also announced that the Senate agrees to the amendment of 
the House to the bill (S. 1636) ``An act to authorize appropriations 
for the Marine Mammal Protection Act of 1972 and to improve the program 
to reduce the incidental taking of marine mammals during the course of 
commercial fishing operations, and for other purposes.''
```
