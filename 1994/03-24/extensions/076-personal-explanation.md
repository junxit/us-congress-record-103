# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Torres, Estaban Edward (T000316) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 394

```
[Congressional Record: March 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                       HON. ESTEBAN EDWARD TORRES

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 24, 1994

  Mr. TORRES. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent on official 
business on Tuesday, March 22, 1994, for rollcall vote No. 78. Had I 
been present on the House floor I would have cast my vote as follows: 
Roll No. 78: ``Yea'' on House Resolution 394, Mr. Gephardt's resolution 
to express the sense of the House that Congress has a constitutional 
obligation to conduct oversight matters related to the operation of the 
Government.
```
