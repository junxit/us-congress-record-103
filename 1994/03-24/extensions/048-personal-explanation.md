# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Ballenger, Cass (B000104) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: March 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                          HON. CASS BALLENGER

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 24, 1994

  Mr. BALLENGER. Mr. Speaker, I missed rollcall votes 81 and 82. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``yea'' in support of the Multifamily 
Housing Property Disposition Reform Act of 1994 and ``nay'' against the 
Urban Recreation and At-Risk Youth Act of 1994.
```
