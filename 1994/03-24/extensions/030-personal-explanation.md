# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Nussle, Jim (N000172) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
[Congressional Record: March 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                            HON. JIM NUSSLE

                                of iowa

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 24, 1994

  Mr. NUSSLE. Mr. Speaker, on Monday, March 21, 1994, I cast my vote in 
favor of the Johnson amendment to H.R. 6--rollcall vote No. 75. 
However, my vote was not recorded. I would like to take this 
opportunity to express my support for the Johnson amendment to H.R. 6, 
and had my vote been recorded, it would have been recorded as ``aye.''
```
