# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** H.R. 3345

```

                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the motion to invoke 
cloture on the conference report accompanying H.R. 3345 is again in 
order.

                          cloture motion no. 2

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of Rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate,

     Report accompanying H.R. 3345, the Federal Workforce 
     Restructuring Act of 1994.
```
