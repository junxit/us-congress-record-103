# CORRECTION OF RECORD

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: June 14, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          CORRECTION OF RECORD

  Mr. MITCHELL. Madam President, during debate on the pending matter, I 
referred to a provision dealing with immunity of witnesses which was 
contained in the resolution passed by the Senate on March 17. I 
inadvertently omitted the last clause of that provision. I was relying 
on statements on the subject which had been made on March 9 by Senators 
D'Amato and Cohen and Special Counsel Fiske to the effect that immunity 
would not be granted under any circumstances. Even though inadvertent, 
the omission was regrettable. I did not learn of it until much later, 
and I am pleased now to make this correction.
```
