```
[Congressional Record: June 14, 1994]

                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER. The Chair will ask the gentleman from Florida [Mr. 
Johnston] if he would kindly come forward and lead the House in the 
Pledge of Allegiance.
  Mr. JOHNSTON of Florida led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the <gr-thn-eq>United 
     States of America, and to the Republic for which it stands, 
     one nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice
```
