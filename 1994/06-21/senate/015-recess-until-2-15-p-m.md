# RECESS UNTIL 2:15 P.M.

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                         RECESS UNTIL 2:15 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 1 
o'clock having arrived, the Senate will stand in recess until 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 1 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:16 p.m.; whereupon, 
the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer 
(Mr. Kohl).
```
