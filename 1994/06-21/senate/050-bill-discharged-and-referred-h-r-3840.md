# BILL DISCHARGED AND REFERRED--H.R. 3840

**Speaking:** DeConcini, Dennis (D000185) [D-AZ], Bond, Christopher S. (B000611) [R-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 3840

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                BILL DISCHARGED AND REFERRED--H.R. 3840

  Mr. DeCONCINI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that H.R. 3840, 
a bill to designate the Sam B. Hall Federal Building and U.S. 
Courthouse, be discharged from the Governmental Affairs Committee and 
referred to the committee of jurisdiction, the Committee on Environment 
and Public Works.
  Mr. BOND. No objection.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
