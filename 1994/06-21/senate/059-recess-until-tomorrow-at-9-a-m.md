# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** DeConcini, Dennis (D000185) [D-AZ]

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. DeCONCINI. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, and I see no other Senator seeking recognition, I 
now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess as previously 
ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 9:25 p.m., recessed until 
Wednesday, June 22, 1994, at 9 a.m..
```
