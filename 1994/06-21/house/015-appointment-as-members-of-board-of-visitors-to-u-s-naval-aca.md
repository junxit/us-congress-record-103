# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF BOARD OF VISITORS TO U.S. NAVAL ACADEMY

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
   APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF BOARD OF VISITORS TO U.S. NAVAL ACADEMY

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the provisions of section 6968(a) of title 
10, United States Code, the Chair appoints as members of the Board of 
Visitors to the U.S. Naval Academy the following Members of the House: 
Mr. Hoyer of Maryland; Mr. Mfume of Maryland; Mrs. Bentley of Maryland; 
and Mr. Skeen of New Mexico.
```
