# APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 4278, SOCIAL SECURITY ACT AMENDMENTS OF 1994

**Speaking:** Jacobs, Andrew, Jr. (J000033) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4278

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 4278, SOCIAL SECURITY ACT AMENDMENTS 
                                OF 1994

  Mr. JACOBS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to take from the 
Speaker's table the bill (H.R. 4278) to make improvements in the Old-
Age, Survivors, and Disability Insurance Program under title II of the 
Social Security Act, with a Senate amendment thereto, disagree to the 
Senate amendment, and agree to the conference asked by the Senate.

                              {time}  1550

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Clement). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Indiana? The Chair hears none, and, 
without objection, appoints the following conferees: Messrs. Gibbons, 
Rostenkowski, Pickle, Jacobs, Ford of Tennessee, Archer, Bunning, and 
Santorum.
  There was no objection.
```
