# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF BOARD OF VISITORS TO U.S. AIR FORCE ACADEMY

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF BOARD OF VISITORS TO U.S. AIR FORCE ACADEMY

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the provisions of section 9355(a) of title 
10, United States Code, the Chair appoints as members of the Board of 
Visitors to the U.S. Air Force Academy the following Members of the 
House: Mr. Dicks of Washington; Mr. Hoagland of Nebraska; Mr. Hefley of 
Colorado; and Mr. Young of Florida.
```
