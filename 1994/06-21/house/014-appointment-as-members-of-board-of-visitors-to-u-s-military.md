# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF BOARD OF VISITORS TO U.S. MILITARY ACADEMY

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF BOARD OF VISITORS TO U.S. MILITARY ACADEMY

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the provisions of section 4355(a) of title 
10, United States Code, the Chair appoints as members of the Board of 
Visitors to the U.S. Military Academy the following Members of the 
House: Mr. Hefner of North Carolina; Mr. Laughlin of Texas; Mr. Fish of
```
