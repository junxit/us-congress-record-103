# REPORT ON H.R. 4603, DEPARTMENTS OF COMMERCE, JUSTICE, AND STATE, THE JUDICIARY, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS, 1995, AND SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 1994

**Measures:** H.R. 4603

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 REPORT ON H.R. 4603, DEPARTMENTS OF COMMERCE, JUSTICE, AND STATE, THE 
JUDICIARY, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS, 1995, AND SUPPLEMENTAL 
                  APPROPRIATIONS FOR FISCAL YEAR 1994

  Mr. MOLLOHAN, from the Committee on Appropriations, submitted a 
privileged report (103-552) on the bill (H.R. 4603) making 
appropriations for the Departments of Commerce, Justice, and State, the 
Judiciary, and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 1995, and making supplemental appropriations for these departments 
and agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1994, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the Union Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
  Mr. ROGERS reserved all points of order on the bill.
```
