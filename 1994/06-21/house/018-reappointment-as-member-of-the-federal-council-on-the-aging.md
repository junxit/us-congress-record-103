# REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF THE FEDERAL COUNCIL ON THE AGING

```
[Congressional Record: June 21, 1994]

                              {time}  1210
 
      REAPPOINTMENT AS MEMBER OF THE FEDERAL COUNCIL ON THE AGING

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the provisions of section 204(a) of the 
Older Americans Act of 1965 (42 U.S.C. 3015(a)), as amended by section 
205 of Public Law 102-375, the Chair reappoints to the Federal Council 
on the Aging for a 3-year term on the part of the House the following 
member from private life: Mrs. Josephine K. Oblinger of Williamsville,
```
