# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 11, 1994

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 4, H.R. 3345

```
[Congressional Record: March 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, MARCH 11, 1994

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
stand in recess until 9 a.m., Friday, March 11, that following the 
prayer, the Journal of Proceedings be approved to date and the time for 
the 2 leaders reserved for their use later in the day; that the Senate 
then proceed to the House message on H.R. 3345, as provided for under a 
previous unanimous-consent agreement; that upon disposition of the 
House message, the Senate then resume consideration of S. 4.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
