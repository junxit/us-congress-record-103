**Speaking:** Bumpers, Dale (B001057) [D-AR]

```

                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                      committee on small business

1994, at 2:30 p.m., in room 428A of the Russell Senate Office Building.

Small Business Committee at 224-5175.
```
