# NOTICE OF RESCHEDULING OF TIME FOR HEARING COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

**Speaking:** Johnston, J. Bennett (J000189) [D-LA]

```
[Congressional Record: March 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  NOTICE OF RESCHEDULING OF TIME FOR HEARING COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND 
                           NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. JOHNSTON. Mr. President, I would like to announce for my 
colleagues and the public that the hearing on the domestic and 
international implications of energy demand growth in China and the 
developing countries of the Pacific rim will take place on March 16, 
1994, in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building, First and C 
Streets, NE, Washington, DC, and will begin at 10 a.m. instead of 9:30 
a.m., as originally scheduled.
  For further information, please contact Shirley Neff of the committee 
staff at 202/224-7865.

                          ____________________
```
