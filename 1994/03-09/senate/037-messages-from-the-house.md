**Measures:** H.R. 3345

```

                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 5:55 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House agrees 
to the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 3345) to provide 
temporary authority to Government agencies relating to voluntary 
separation incentive payments, and for other purposes, with an 
amendment, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate.
```
