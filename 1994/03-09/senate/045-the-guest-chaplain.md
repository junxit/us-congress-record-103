# THE GUEST CHAPLAIN

**Speaking:** Dorgan, Byron L. (D000432) [D-ND], Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: March 9, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           THE GUEST CHAPLAIN

  Mr. DORGAN. Mr. President, I rise today to welcome a friend and a 
fellow North Dakotan, the Reverend Hensel Hendrickson, who is from the 
Trinity Lutheran Church in Bismarck, ND.
  Reverend Hendrickson is a distinguished and well-respected North 
Dakotan, which happens to be the home State, I might add, of the 
resident Chaplain in the U.S. Senate, Reverend Halverson.
  I might also point out to my colleagues that Reverend Hendrickson is 
a Scandinavian and a Lutheran, which is not a rarity in our part of the 
country.
  I am delighted to welcome him to the Senate today, and I am very 
pleased he was able to open the Senate with prayer.
  Thank you, Mr. President. I yield the floor.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senate majority leader.
  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I join my colleague in welcoming 
Reverend Hendrickson, and I thank him for his prayer this morning.
```
