# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON SCIENCE, SPACE, AND TECHNOLOGY, AND COMMITTEE ON PUBLIC WORKS AND TRANSPORTATION

**Speaking:** Michel, Robert H. (M000692) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 335

```
[Congressional Record: February 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
ELECTION OF MEMBER TO COMMITTEE ON SCIENCE, SPACE, AND TECHNOLOGY, AND 
              COMMITTEE ON PUBLIC WORKS AND TRANSPORTATION

  Mr. MICHEL. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 
335) and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution as follows:

                              H. Res. 335

       Resolved, That Representative Vernon J. Ehlers of Michigan 
     be and is hereby elected to the following standing committees 
     of the House of Representatives: The Committee on Science, 
     Space, and Technology, and the Committee on Public Works and 
     Transportation.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
