# IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOX SCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: February 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
           IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOX SCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, the Federal debt stood at 
$4,522,736,108,206.24 as of the close of business yesterday, February 
1. Averaged out, every man, woman, and child in America owes a part of 
this massive debt, and that per capita share is $17,347.70.
```
