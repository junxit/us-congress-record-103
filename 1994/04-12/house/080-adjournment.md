# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Gingrich, Newt (G000225) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 400

```
[Congressional Record: April 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GINGRICH. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 400, I move 
that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable William 
H. Natcher.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 6 o'clock and 13 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Resolution 400, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Wednesday, April 13, 1994, at 2 p.m., in memory of the late 
Honorable William H. Natcher of Kentucky.
```
