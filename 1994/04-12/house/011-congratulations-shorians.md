# CONGRATULATIONS, SHORIANS

**Speaking:** Bonior, David E. (B000619) [D-MI]

```
[Congressional Record: April 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                       CONGRATULATIONS, SHORIANS

  (Mr. BONIOR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. BONIOR. Mr. Speaker, of all the great basketball finishes we have 
seen the past few weeks, none was more exciting than the Michigan Class 
B Boys Basketball Championship.
  The lead seesawed back and forth between Macomb County's Lake Shore 
Shorians and the East Grand Rapids Pioneers.
  With 5 seconds left, East Grand Rapids went up by one.
  But with two ticks left on the clock Lake Shore's Travis Conlan took 
the inbounds pass, slid down the baseline, and put up an off-balance 
shot from the behind the backboard that rolled around the rim and 
dropped through--setting off bedlam in Macomb County.
  I want to congratulate Coach Greg Esler and Lake Shore High for 
winning the first boys basketball State title in Macomb County history.
  The Shorians finished with a perfect 28-to-0 record and capped a 2-
year record of 54-to-1.
  I think I speak for Macomb County when I say ``Congratulations, 
Shorians. We're all proud of you.''
```
