# RECESS UNTIL 2:15 P.M.

**Speaking:** Pell, Claiborne (P000193) [D-RI]

```
[Congressional Record: April 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                         RECESS UNTIL 2:15 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
having come and passed, the Senate will be in recess until the hour of 
2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:35 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:16 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mrs. Boxer).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The pending question is amendment No. 1620. 
The time between now and 2:45 p.m. is equally divided and controlled by 
the Senators from Wyoming and California.
  Mr. PELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
permitted to speak for 3 minutes, the time not being charged to either 
side.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
