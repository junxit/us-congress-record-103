# HOMICIDES BY GUNSHOT IN NEW YORK CITY

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY]

```
[Congressional Record: April 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                 HOMICIDES BY GUNSHOT IN NEW YORK CITY

  Mr. MOYNIHAN. Mr. President, I rise today to announce to my Senate 
colleagues, as has been my custom each week during this session of the 
103d Congress, that during the last 3 weeks, 60 people were killed by 
gunshots in New York City, bringing the 1994 total to 304.
```
