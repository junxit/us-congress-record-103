# MODIFICATION IN APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 3355, VIOLENT CRIME CONTROL AND PREVENTION ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 3355

```
[Congressional Record: June 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 MODIFICATION IN APPOINTMENT OF CONFEREES ON H.R. 3355, VIOLENT CRIME 
                       CONTROL AND PREVENTION ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Speaker makes the 
following modification in the appointment of conferees on the bill 
(H.R. 3355) to amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe Streets Act of 
1968 to allow grants to increase police presence, to expand and improve 
cooperative efforts between law enforcement agencies and members of the 
community to address crime and disorder problems, and otherwise to 
enhance public safety:

       In the paragraph naming additional conferees from the 
     Committee on Merchant Marine and Fisheries, add Mr. Bateman 
     in lieu of Mr. Young of Alaska.

  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
