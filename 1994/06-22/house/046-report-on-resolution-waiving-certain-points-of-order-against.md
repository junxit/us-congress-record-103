REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING CERTAIN POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST H.R. 4603, 
DEPARTMENTS OF COMMERCE, JUSTICE, AND STATE, THE JUDICIARY, AND RELATED 
AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1994, AND SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS FOR 
                            FISCAL YEAR 1994

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-556) on the resolution (H. Res. 461) waiving 
certain points of order against the bill (H.R. 4603) making 
appropriations for the Departments of Commerce, Justice, and State, the 
Judiciary, and related agencies programs for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 1995, and making supplemental appropriations for these 
departments and agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1994, 
and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
