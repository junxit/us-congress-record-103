              NORTHERN GREAT PLAINS RURAL DEVELOPMENT ACT

                       DASCHLE AMENDMENT NO. 1838

  Mr. NUNN (for Mr. Daschle) proposed an amendment to the bill (S.2099) 
to establish the Northern Great Plains Rural Development Commission, 
and for other purposes; as follows:

     paragraph:
       (2) 1 member shall be appointed from each of the States by 
     the Secretary of Agriculture.
