# DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

**Speaking:** Simon, Paul (S000423) [D-IL]

```
[Congressional Record: June 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           DOMESTIC VIOLENCE

  Mr. SIMON. Mr. President, Senator Wellstone talked about domestic 
violence.
  It could be that the events of the last week will change our culture 
in the same way that the Clarence Thomas-Anita Hill hearings changed 
our culture in making us sensitive to the whole question of sexual 
harassment. All of the sudden, we are talking about this problem more 
openly, and that is a healthy thing.
```
