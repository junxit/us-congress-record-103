# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD], Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

```
[Congressional Record: July 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 2 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for not to exceed 5 minutes.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DOLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Daschle). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
