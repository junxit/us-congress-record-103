Agriculture Appropriations, 1995: Senate began consideration of H.R. 
4554, making appropriations for Agriculture, Rural Development, Food 
and Drug Administration, and Related Agencies programs for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 1995, agreeing to committee amendments, with 
certain exceptions, and taking action on amendments proposed thereto, 
as follows:
  Pages S9140-68
Adopted:
  (1) Daschle/Kerrey Amendment No. 2302, to make funds available to 
carry out the Northern Great Plains Rural Development Act.
Pages S9161-62
  (2) Bumpers (for Leahy) Amendment No. 2304, to remove the cap on the 
amount of acreage allowed in the Wetlands Reserve Program.
Page S9167
Pending:
  Heflin Amendment No. 2303, to make funds available for emergency 
community water assistance grants, low-income housing repair grants, 
and the Agriculture Credit Insurance Fund Program account.
Pages S9165-68
  A unanimous-consent agreement was reached providing for consideration 
of the committee amendment beginning on page 86, line 9, with a vote on 
the amendment to occur at 2:30 p.m., on Tuesday, July 19.
Page S9162
  Senate will continue consideration of the bill and amendments to be 
proposed thereto, on Tuesday, July 19.

Relations. (PM-133).
