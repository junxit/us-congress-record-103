# LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Michel, Robert H. (M000692) [R-IL], Gephardt, Richard A. (G000132) [D-MO]

```
[Congressional Record: June 29, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

  (Mr. MICHEL asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. MICHEL. Mr. Speaker, I would like to inquire of the distinguished 
Majority Leader how he envisions the evening unfolding at this 
juncture.
  Mr. GEPHARDT. Mr. Speaker, if the gentleman will yield, because of 
all the good cooperation we made here this evening, the good progress 
made, we have one additional matter tonight, as I said earlier, which 
is a motion to instruct on the crime bill. That should come right away.
  Tomorrow we will meet at 10 o'clock, and we will try to proceed with 
the District of Columbia appropriations bill. Negotiations are still 
going on with regard to that. We hope it can be worked out.
  Mr. MICHEL. I thank the gentleman. Mr. Speaker, we have an 
adjournment resolution then to consider. Does the gentleman intend to 
do that?
  Mr. GEPHARDT. I intend to do that right now.
```
