REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
       ON H.R. 4454, LEGISLATIVE BRANCH APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 1995

report (Rept. No. 103-569) on the resolution (H. Res. 470) waiving
