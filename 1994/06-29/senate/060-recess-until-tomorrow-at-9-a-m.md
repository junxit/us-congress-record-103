# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
[Congressional Record: June 29, 1994]

                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. LEAHY. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come
```
