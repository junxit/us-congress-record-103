# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** H.R. 4454

```
[Congressional Record: June 29, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 5, 1993, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on June 29, 1994, during the recess of the 
Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives announcing 
that the House disagrees to the amendments of the Senate to the bill 
(H.R. 4454) making appropriations for the legislative branch for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 1995, and for other purposes, and 
agrees to the conference asked by the Senate on the disagreeing votes 
of the two Houses thereon; and appoints Mr. Fazio, Mr. Moran, Mr. Obey, 
Mr. Murtha, Mr. Carr, Mr. Chapman, Mr. Sabo, Mr. Young of Florida, Mr. 
Packard, Mr. Taylor of North Carolina, and Mr. McDade as managers of 
the conference on the part of the House.
```
