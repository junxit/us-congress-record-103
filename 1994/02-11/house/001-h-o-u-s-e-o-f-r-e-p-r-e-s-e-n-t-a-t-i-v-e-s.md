# H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S

```

             H O U S E  O F  R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S


Vol. 140


WASHINGTON, FRIDAY, FEBRUARY 11, 1994

No. 14


House of Representatives

  The House met at 2 p.m.
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Be with us, O God, in all the moments of life. From the rising of the 
Sun until the going down of the same, may Your word be with each of us 
and all we do. For all those who desire meaning and purpose in life, 
give the direction of the ancient commandments and the eternal values 
that bind us together. For all who wish to reflect more clearly the 
brightness of Your light, give us strength to know the truth and the 
power to live the truth. Bless us, we pray O God, that we will be Your 
people in good times and bad, and be faithful in all the days. In Your 
name, we pray. Amen.
```
