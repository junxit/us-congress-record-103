  MAKING IN ORDER ON TODAY CONSIDERATION OF CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 
 3759, EMERGENCY SUPPLEMENTAL APPROPRIATIONS FOR EARTHQUAKE ASSISTANCE

  Mr. FAZIO. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it be in order 
today to consider the conference report to accompany the bill (H.R. 
3759) making emergency supplemental appropriations for the fiscal year

order against the conference report and against its consideration be 
waived, and that the conference report be considered as read.

request of the gentleman from California?
