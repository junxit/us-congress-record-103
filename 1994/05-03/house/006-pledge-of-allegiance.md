# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
[Congressional Record: May 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentlewoman from Utah [Ms. 
Shepherd] please come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of 
Allegiance.
  Ms. SHEPHERD led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the United States of 
     America, and to the Republic for which it stands, one nation 
     under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice for all.
```
