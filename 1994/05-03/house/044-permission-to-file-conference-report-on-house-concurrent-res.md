# PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 218, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET--FISCAL YEAR 1995

**Speaking:** Sabo, Martin Olav (S000005) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 218

```
[Congressional Record: May 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  PERMISSION TO FILE CONFERENCE REPORT ON HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 
       218, CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET--FISCAL YEAR 1995

  Mr. SABO. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the conferees may 
have until midnight tonight to file a conference report on the 
concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 218) setting forth the 
congressional budget for the U.S. Government for the fiscal years 1995, 
1996, 1997, 1998, and 1999.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Coppersmith). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Minnesota?
  There was no objection.
```
