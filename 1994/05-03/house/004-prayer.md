# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: May 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Gracious God, from whom comes every good and perfect gift, may Your 
grace be with all people who turn to You for blessing and assurance. 
May Your words of hope lift every heart; may Your healing presence 
restore us in mind, body, and spirit; may Your vision allow us to see 
opportunities of service to others; may Your gift of this day allow us 
to celebrate both life and love, and may Your eternal promises enable 
us to gain a heart of wisdom. Amen.
```
