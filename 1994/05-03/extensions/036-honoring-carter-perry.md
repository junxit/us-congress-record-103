# HONORING CARTER PERRY

**Speaking:** Engel, Eliot L. (E000179) [D-NY]

```
[Congressional Record: May 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                         HONORING CARTER PERRY

                                 ______


                          HON. ELIOT L. ENGEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 3, 1994

  Mr. ENGEL. Mr. Speaker, I would like to take this opportunity to join 
the friends and family of my constituent, Carter Perry, in praising his 
dedication of more than 30 years of his life to community and political 
services.
  Through his work at the Morrisania Health Center, Carter Perry has 
helped many alcoholic patients, and he has also worked with foster 
children and other disenfranchised persons. His concern for the 
community extends beyond work hours, where he is active in local 
political and neighborhood functions. His wife of 39 years, Helen, and 
their 3 children and 7 grandchildren can also testify to his dedication 
to family.
  Therefore, on behalf of the many people in the Bronx whose lives have 
been touched by Carter Perry in a positive way, I extend our 
appreciation and wish him continued health, happiness and success.
```
