# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Coverdell, Paul (C000813) [R-GA]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Also under the previous order, there will 
now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 12:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for not to exceed 5 minutes each.
  The first hour of morning business will be under the control of the 
Senator from Georgia [Mr. Coverdell] or his designee.
  Mr. COVERDELL. Thank you, Mr. President.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Mr. Coverdell is recognized to control the 
first hour.

                          ____________________
```
