# REREFERRAL OF NOMINATION

**Speaking:** Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

```
                        REREFERRAL OF NOMINATION

  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, as if in executive session, I ask 
unanimous consent that the nomination of Jere Glover to be Chief 
Counsel for Advocacy, Small Business Administration, Executive Calendar 
831, be referred back to the Committee on Small Business, at the 
request of the chairman.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection? Hearing no objection, 
the request of the Senator from South Dakota is granted.
```
