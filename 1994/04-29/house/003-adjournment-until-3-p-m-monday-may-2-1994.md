# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 3 P.M. MONDAY, MAY 2, 1994

```
[Congressional Record: April 29, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
              ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 3 P.M. MONDAY, MAY 2, 1994

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in adjournment until 3 o'clock p.m. on Monday, May 2, 
1994.
  Thereupon, at 9 o'clock and 57 seconds a.m., the Senate adjourned 
until Monday, May 2, 1994, at 3 p.m.
```
