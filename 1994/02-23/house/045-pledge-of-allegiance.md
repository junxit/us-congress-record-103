# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
[Congressional Record: February 23, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Montgomery). The Chair will ask the 
gentlewoman from Virginia [Mrs. Byrne] if she would kindly come forward 
and lead the membership in the Pledge of Allegiance.
  Mrs. BYRNE led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the <gr-thn-eq>United 
     States of America, and to the Republic for which it stands, 
     one nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice 
     for all.
```
