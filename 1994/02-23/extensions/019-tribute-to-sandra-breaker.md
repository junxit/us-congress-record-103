# TRIBUTE TO SANDRA BREAKER

**Speaking:** Towns, Edolphus (T000326) [D-NY]

```
[Congressional Record: February 23, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                       TRIBUTE TO SANDRA BREAKER

                                 ______


                          HON. EDOLPHUS TOWNS

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 23, 1994

  Mr. TOWNS. Mr. Speaker, it is my pleasure to recognize Sandra 
Breaker, a native New Yorker. She has been a resident of East New York 
for the past 20 years.
  Sandra attends Salem Baptist Missionary Church. She is active in many 
community organizations, including local parent teacher associations 
and public housing advocacy groups.
  During her summers, Ms. Breaker can be found working with youth 
groups. She brings a special vitality to everything she does. For 
example, she spearheaded, along with the Fiorentino Plaza Tenant 
Association president Juanita Fisher, an increase the peace, stop the 
violence demonstration. She is currently pursuing a degree at the 
Metropolitan Educational Center, a community based campus of Molloy 
College.
  I  want  to  congratulate  Sandra  Breaker  on  her  community  
activism  as  an  East  New  Yorker.

                          ____________________
```
