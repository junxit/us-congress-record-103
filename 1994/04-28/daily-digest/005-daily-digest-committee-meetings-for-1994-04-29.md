                     COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR FRIDAY,

                             APRIL 29, 1994

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Finance, Subcommittee on Health for Families and the 
Uninsured, to hold hearings to examine health care reform issues,

215.
  Committee on Foreign Relations, to hold hearings on the

Maryland, to be Ambassador to Ethiopia; Robert Krueger, of Texas, to 
be Ambassador to the Republic of Burundi; and Johnny Young, of 
Pennsylvania, to be Ambassador to the Republic of Togo, 2 p.m., SD-
419.

Robert Harlan Henry, of Oklahoma, to be United States Circuit Judge 
for the Tenth Circuit; Deborah A. Batts, to be United States 
District Judge for the Southern District of New York; Raymond L. 
Finch, to be a Judge for the District Court of the Virgin Islands; 
and Solomon Finch, Jr., to be United States District Judge for the 
Northern District of Ohio, 10 a.m., SD-226.


                                 House

  Committee on Government Operations, Subcommittee on Environment, 
Energy, and Natural Resources, hearing on Clean Air Protection 
problems at National Parks Wilderness Areas, 9 a.m., 2247 Rayburn.
