# EXPLANATION OF MISSED ROLLCALL VOTE

**Speaking:** Becerra, Xavier (B000287) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 222, H.R. 2333

```
[Congressional Record: April 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                  EXPLANATION OF MISSED ROLLCALL VOTE

                                 ______


                          HON. XAVIER BECERRA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 28, 1994

  Mr. BECERRA. Mr. Speaker, on April 18, 1994, I unavoidably missed 
rollcall votes No. 113 and No. 114. Had I been present I would have 
voted ``aye'' to House Concurrent Resolution 222 which would authorize 
the placement of the bust of Raoul Wallenberg in the Capitol. I would 
have also voted ``aye'' to H.R. 2333, a motion to instruct conferees on 
the State Department authorization bill.
```
