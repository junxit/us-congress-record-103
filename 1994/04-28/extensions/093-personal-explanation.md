# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** English, Karan (E000186) [D-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2333

```

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                           HON. KARAN ENGLISH

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 28, 1994

  Ms. ENGLISH of Arizona. Mr. Speaker, because of a family illness I 
was unavoidably absent during rollcall vote Nos. 146 and 147.
  On rollcall vote No. 146 regarding the Iraqi Claims Act, had I been 
present I would have voted ``aye.'' On rollcall vote No. 147, the 
motion to recommit the conference report on H.R. 2333, State Department 
authorization, had I been present I would have voted ``no.''
```
