# PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY MADE DURING MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Livingston, Bob (L000371) [R-LA]

```
[Congressional Record: April 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
           PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY MADE DURING MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. LIVINGSTON. Madam Speaker, I have a parliamentary inquiry.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Clayton). The gentleman will state it.
  Mr. LIVINGSTON. Madam Speaker, is it permissible for me at this time 
to withdraw my name from cosponsorship of an amendment by unanimous 
consent?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair is informed the gentleman will 
have to get that permission when the House reconvenes at 12 noon to 
conduct business.
```
