# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4600, EXPEDITED RESCISSIONS ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.Res. 467, H.R. 4600

```
[Congressional Record: June 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4600, 
                   EXPEDITED RESCISSIONS ACT OF 1994

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-565) on the resolution (H. Res. 467) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4600) to amend the Congressional 
Budget and Impoundment Control Act of 1974 to provide for the expedited 
consideration of certain proposed rescissions of budget authority, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
