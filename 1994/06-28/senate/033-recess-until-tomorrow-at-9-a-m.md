# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Rockefeller, John D., IV (R000361) [D-WV]

```
[Congressional Record: June 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT
                                 9 A.M.

  Mr. ROCKEFELLER. Madam President, if there is no further business to 
come before the Senate today, and I see no other Senator seeking 
recognition, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
recess as previously ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate at 7:16 p.m., recessed until 
tomorrow, Wednesday, June 29, 1994, at 9 a.m.
```
