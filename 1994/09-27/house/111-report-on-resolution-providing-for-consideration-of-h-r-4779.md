# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4779, LOCAL GOVERNMENT INTERSTATE WASTE CONTROL ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 551, H.R. 4779

```
[Congressional Record: September 27, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4779, LOCAL 
                GOVERNMENT INTERSTATE WASTE CONTROL ACT

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-756) on the resolution (H. Res. 551) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4779) to amend the Solid Waste 
Disposal Act to authorize local governments and Governors to restrict 
receipt of out-of-State municipal solid waste, and for other purpose, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
