```
[Congressional Record: September 27, 1994]

REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
               ON S. 349, LOBBYING DISCLOSURE ACT OF 1993

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-755) on the resolution (H. Res. 550) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill (S. 
349) to provide for the disclosure of lobbying activities to influence 
the Federal Government, and for other purposes, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
