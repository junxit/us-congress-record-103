# ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Metzenbaum, Howard M. (M000678) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 4602

```
[Congressional Record: September 27, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

  Mr. METZENBAUM. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I 
ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its businesses 
today, it stand in recess until 10 a.m., Wednesday, September 28, that 
following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be deemed approved to 
date and the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in 
the day; that immediately thereafter the Senate proceed to the 
consideration of the conference report accompanying H.R. 4602, 
Department of Interior appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
