# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 10 A.M.

**Speaking:** Metzenbaum, Howard M. (M000678) [D-OH]

```
[Congressional Record: September 27, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 10 A.M.

  Mr. METZENBAUM. Mr. President, if there is no further business to 
come before the Senate, and I see no other Senator seeking recognition, 
I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess as 
previously ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 8:21 p.m., recessed until 
Wednesday, September 28, 1994, at 10 a.m.
```
