# ROY M. WHEAT POST OFFICE ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 3839

```

                      ROY M. WHEAT POST OFFICE ACT

  The bill (H.R. 3839) to designate the U.S. Post Office located at 220 
South 40th Avenue in Hattiesburg, MS, as the Roy M. Wheat Post Office 
was considered, ordered to a third reading, deemed read the third time,
```
