# PERMISSION FOR MEMBERS TO SUBMIT MATERIAL FOR EXTENSION OF REMARKS ON JUNE 30, 1994

**Speaking:** Schroeder, Patricia (S000142) [D-CO]

```
[Congressional Record: June 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 PERMISSION FOR MEMBERS TO SUBMIT MATERIAL FOR EXTENSION OF REMARKS ON 
                             JUNE 30, 1994

  Mrs. SCHROEDER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that for today,
```
