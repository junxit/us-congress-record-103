# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON MERCHANT MARINE AND FISHERIES TO HAVE UNTIL 5 P.M., JULY 8, 1994, TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 4008, THE NATIONAL OCEANIC AND ATMOSPHERIC ADMINISTRATION AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1994

**Speaking:** Studds, Gerry E. (S001040) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4008

```
[Congressional Record: June 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON MERCHANT MARINE AND FISHERIES TO HAVE UNTIL 
5 P.M., JULY 8, 1994, TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 4008, THE NATIONAL OCEANIC 
        AND ATMOSPHERIC ADMINISTRATION AUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1994

  Mr. STUDDS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Merchant Marine and Fisheries may have until 5 p.m. on July 8, 1994, 
to file a report on the bill, H.R. 4008, the National Oceanic and 
Atmospheric Administration Authorization Act of 1994, as amended.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Massachusetts?
  There was no objection.
```
