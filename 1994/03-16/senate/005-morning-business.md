# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** S. 1275

```
[Congressional Record: March 16, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that there now 
be a period for morning business, during which time Senators may be 
recognized to speak for up to 10 minutes each; that the period for 
morning business extend until 12:30 p.m. today, and that at 12:30 p.m., 
the Senate proceed to the consideration of Calendar Order No. 259, S. 
1275, a bill to facilitate the establishment of community development 
financial institutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
