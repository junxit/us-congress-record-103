# QUARTERLY REPORTS--OCTOBER 1994

```
[Congressional Record: October 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    QUARTERLY REPORTS--OCTOBER 1994

  The mailing and filing date of the October quarterly report required 
by the Federal Election Campaign Act, as amended, is Saturday, October 
15, 1994. All principal campaign committees supporting Senate 
candidates in the 1994 races must file their reports with the Senate 
Office of Public Records, 232 Hart Building, Washington, DC 20510-7116. 
Senators may wish to advise their campaign committee personnel of this 
requirement.
  The Public Records Office will be open from 12 noon until 4 p.m. on 
October 15, to receive these filings. For further information, please 
contact the Office of Public Records on (202) 224-0322.

                          ____________________
```
