       IMPROVING AMERICA'S SCHOOLS ACT OF 1994--CONFERENCE REPORT

  The Senate resumed consideration of the conference report.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Ford). The question is on the adoption of 
the conference report. All in favor, say aye?
