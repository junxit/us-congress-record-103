# THIRTY-DAY POST-GENERAL REPORTS--1994

```
[Congressional Record: October 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                 THIRTY-DAY POST-GENERAL REPORTS--1994

  The mailing and filing date of the 30-day post-general report 
required by the Federal Election Campaign Act, as amended, is Thursday, 
December 8, 1994. All principal campaign committees supporting Senate 
candidates in the 1994 races must file their reports with the Senate 
Office of Public Records, 232 Hart Building, Washington, DC 20510-7116. 
Senators may wish to advise their campaign committee personnel of this 
requirement.
  The Public Records Office will be open from 9 a.m. until 5 p.m. on 
Thursday, December 8, to receive these filings. For further 
information, please contact the Office of Public Records on (202) 224-
0322.
```
