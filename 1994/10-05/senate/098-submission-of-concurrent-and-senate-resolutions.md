            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were

           By Mr. DeCONCINI (for himself and Mr. Grassley):

     independent nation of Moldova; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Relations.

       S. Con. Res. 79. A concurrent resolution recognizing 
     Belleville, New Jersey, as the birthplace of the industrial 
     revolution in the United States; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
