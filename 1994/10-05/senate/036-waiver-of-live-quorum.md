# WAIVER OF LIVE QUORUM

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 6, S. 349

```
[Congressional Record: October 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                         WAIVER OF LIVE QUORUM

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that the live quorum required under rule XXII be 
waived with respect to the cloture motions filed relative to H.R. 6 and 
S. 349.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Under the previous order, the time until 9:45 a.m. shall be under the 
control of the Senator from Wyoming, Mr. Wallop. The Chair recognizes 
the Senator from Wyoming, Mr. Wallop.
```
