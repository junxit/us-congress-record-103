# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H. J. RES.

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 416, H.Res. 570

```
[Congressional Record: October 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H. J. RES. 
  416, LIMITED AUTHORIZATION FOR THE UNITED STATES-LED FORCE IN HAITI 
                               RESOLUTION

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-840) on the resolution (H. Res. 570) providing 
for further consideration of the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 416) 
providing limited authorization for the participation of United States 
Armed Forces in the multinational force in Haiti and providing for the 
prompt withdrawal of United States Armed Forces from Haiti, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
