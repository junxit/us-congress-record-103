# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
[Congressional Record: October 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Visclosky). Pursuant to the provisions 
of clause 5 of rule I, the Chair announces that he will reduce to a 
minimum of 5 minutes the period of time within which a vote by 
electronic device may be taken on each of the additional motions to 
suspend the rules on which the Chair has postponed further proceedings.
```
