# WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 21, CALIFORNIA DESERT PROTECTION ACT

**Measures:** S. 21, H.Res. 568

```
[Congressional Record: October 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 21, CALIFORNIA 
                         DESERT PROTECTION ACT

  Mr. Gordon, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-839) on the resolution (H. Res. 568) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill (S. 
21) to designate certain lands in the California Desert as wilderness, 
to establish Death Valley, Joshua Tree, and Mojave National Parks, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
