# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gilman, Benjamin A. (G000212) [R-NY]

```
[Congressional Record: April 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. GILMAN. Mr. Speaker, with regard to rollcall vote 136, I was 
recorded as voting in support of the amendment. However, I intended to 
vote in opposition to the amendment. I ask that the Record reflect my 
opposition to rollcall vote 136, the Gordon amendment prohibiting the 
award of Pell grants to incarcerated prisoners.
```
