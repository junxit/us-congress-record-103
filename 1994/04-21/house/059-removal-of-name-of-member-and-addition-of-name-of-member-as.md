# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AND ADDITION OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 173

**Speaking:** Barcia, James A. (B000134) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 173

```
[Congressional Record: April 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AND ADDITION OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR 
                   OF HOUSE CONCURRENT RESOLUTION 173

  Mr. BARCIA of Michigan. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
name of Mr. Price of North Carolina be withdrawn as a cosponsor of 
House Concurrent Resolution 173, and that the name of Ms. Pryce of Ohio 
be added as a cosponsor of said resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. McNulty). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.
```
