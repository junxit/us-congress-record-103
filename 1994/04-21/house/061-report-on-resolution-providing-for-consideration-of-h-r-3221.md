**Measures:** H.Res. 410, H.R. 3221

 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3221, IRAQI

  Mr. WHEAT, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 103-481), on the resolution (H. Res. 410) providing for
