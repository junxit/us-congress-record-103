# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Peterson, Douglas (Pete) (P000259) [D-FL]

```
[Congressional Record: April 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                     HON. DOUGLAS ``PETE'' PETERSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 21, 1994

  Mr. PETERSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, in order to attend a funeral, I 
missed four votes on April 13. Had I been present, I would have voted 
``aye'' on rollcall votes 99, 100, and 102; ``no'' on 101.
  Due to an illness in my family, I was unable to attend House votes on 
April 18 and 19. Had I been present, I would have voted ``aye'' on 
rollcall votes 113, 114, 115, 116, 117, 118, 119, 122, 124, and 126; 
``no'' on 120, 121, 123, and 125.
```
