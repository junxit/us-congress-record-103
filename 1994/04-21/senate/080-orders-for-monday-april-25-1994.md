# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 25, 1994

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Edward M. (K000105) [D-MA]

**Measures:** S. 1963

```
[Congressional Record: April 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 25, 1994

  Mr. KENNEDY. Madam President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
stand in recess until 3 p.m., Monday, April 25; that following the 
prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be deemed approved to date and 
the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in the day; 
that immediately thereafter, the Senate then, as provided for under a 
previous unanimous consent agreement, proceed to the consideration of 
calendar No. 393, S. 1963, the Interstate Banking Bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
