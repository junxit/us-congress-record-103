# PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

**Speaking:** Dornan, Robert K. (D000435) [R-CA]

```
[Congressional Record: April 14, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                         PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

  Mr. DORNAN. Mr. Speaker, I have a parliamentary inquiry.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Tucker). The gentleman will state his 
parliamentary inquiry.
  Mr. DORNAN. Are there any 60-minute special orders tonight?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair is not aware of any.
  Mr. DORNAN. Then I would ask unanimous consent to be allowed to 
address the House for 20 minutes and to revise and extend my remarks.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair recognizes the gentleman from 
California [Mr. Dornan] for 20 minutes.
```
