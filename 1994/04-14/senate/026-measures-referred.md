# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 3498, H.R. 3770

```
[Congressional Record: April 14, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following measures were read the first and second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 3498. An Act to establish the Great Falls Historic 
     District, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Energy 
     and Natural Resources.
       H.R. 3770. An Act to designate the United States courthouse 
     located at 940 Front Street in San Diego, California, and the 
     Federal building attached to the courthouse as the ``Edward 
     J. Schwartz Courthouse and Federal Building''; to the 
     Committee on Environment and Public Works.
```
