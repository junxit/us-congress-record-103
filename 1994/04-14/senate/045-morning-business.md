# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Gorton, Slade (G000333) [R-WA]

```
[Congressional Record: April 14, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 9:30 a.m. with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for not to exceed 5 minutes each.
  In my capacity as a Senator from Wisconsin, I suggest the absence of 
a quorum.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. GORTON. Mr. President, is the Senate in morning business?
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. That is correct.

                          ____________________
```
