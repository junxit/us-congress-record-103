# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Ballenger, Cass (B000104) [R-NC]

```

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                          HON. CASS BALLENGER

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 23, 1994

  Mr. BALLENGER. Mr. Speaker, I missed rollcall votes 229, 230, and 
231. Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' in support of the 
Thomas amendment, ``yea'' in support of the Hunter amendment, and 
``yea'' in support of the Pombo amendment to the California Desert 
Protection Act.
```
