# CHANGE OF VOTE

**Speaking:** Nunn, Sam (N000171) [D-GA]

```
[Congressional Record: June 23, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                             CHANGE OF VOTE

  Mr. NUNN. On rollcall vote No. 165, Senator Exon was present and 
voted ``no.'' The official record listed him as ``absent''. Therefore, 
I ask unanimous consent that the official record be corrected to 
accurately reflect his vote. This will in no way change the outcome of 
the vote.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
