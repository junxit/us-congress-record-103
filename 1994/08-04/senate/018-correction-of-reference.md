# CORRECTION OF REFERENCE

**Speaking:** Smith, Bob (S000606) [R-NH]

```
[Congressional Record: August 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        CORRECTION OF REFERENCE

  Mr. SMITH. Mr. President, on Monday during debate on the Elementary 
and Secondary Education Act, I inadvertently attributed a publication 
entitled ``Young, Gay and Proud'' to the Sex Information and Education 
Council of the United States, or SIECUS. This is actually a publication 
of Alyson Publications.
  SIECUS is responsible for one publication that I read from, entitled 
``Let's Talk About Sex.''
  I apologize for the error.

                          ____________________
```
