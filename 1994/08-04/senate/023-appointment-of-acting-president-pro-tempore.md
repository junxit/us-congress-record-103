# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
[Congressional Record: August 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore [Mr. Byrd].
  The assistant legislative clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                   Washington, DC, August 4, 1994.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, section 3, of the Standing 
     Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Herb 
     Kohl, a Senator from the State of Wisconsin, to perform the 
     duties of the Chair.
                                                   Robert C. Byrd,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. KOHL thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________
```
