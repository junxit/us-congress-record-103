# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 9 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for up to 5 minutes each.
  The Senator from Iowa [Mr. Grassley] is recognized to speak for up to 
15 minutes.
  Mr. GRASSLEY. Mr. President, thank you very much.
```
