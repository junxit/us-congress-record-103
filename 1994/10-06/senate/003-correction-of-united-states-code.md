# CORRECTION OF UNITED STATES CODE

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 3477

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    CORRECTION OF UNITED STATES CODE

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 3477, a bill to correct 
the United States Code to reflect the current names of congressional 
committees just received from the House; that the bill be deemed read 
three times, passed, and the motion to reconsider laid upon the table; 
that any statement appear at the appropriate place in the Record as if 
read.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 3477) was deemed to have been read three times and 
passed.
```
