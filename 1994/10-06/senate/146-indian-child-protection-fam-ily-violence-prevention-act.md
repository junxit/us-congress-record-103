# INDIAN CHILD PROTECTION FAM- ILY VIOLENCE PREVENTION ACT

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2075

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
        INDIAN CHILD PROTECTION FAM- ILY VIOLENCE PREVENTION ACT

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to the immediate consideration of calendar No. 707, S. 2075, a bill to 
amend the Indian Child Protection Family Violence Prevention Act; that 
the committee substitute amendment be agreed to; that the bill, as 
amended, be passed and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table; 
further, that any statements on the measure appear in the Record at the 
appropriate place as though read.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (S. 2075), as amended, was deemed read the third time and 
passed.
  (The text of the bill will be printed in a future edition of the 
Record.)
```
