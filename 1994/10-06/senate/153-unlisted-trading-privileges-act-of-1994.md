# UNLISTED TRADING PRIVILEGES ACT OF 1994

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4535

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                UNLISTED TRADING PRIVILEGES ACT OF 1994

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Banking 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of H.R. 4535, the 
Unlisted Trading Privileges Act of 1994; that the Senate then proceed 
to its immediate consideration; that the bill be deemed read three 
times, passed, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table; and 
that any statement appear in the Record as if read.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 4535) was deemed read the third time and passed.
```
