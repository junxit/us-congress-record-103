# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Dunn, Jennifer (D000549) [R-WA]

**Measures:** S. 1225

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. JENNIFER B. DUNN

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, October 6, 1994

  Ms. DUNN. Mr. Speaker, because of a mechanical malfunction, my vote 
was incorrectly recorded on Wednesday, October 5, 1994 during the vote 
on S. 1225. My vote was electronically recorded as a ``yea'' vote when 
it should have been ``nay''.
```
