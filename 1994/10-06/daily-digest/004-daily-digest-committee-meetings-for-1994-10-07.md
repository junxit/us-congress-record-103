                     COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR FRIDAY,

Enforcement of Employment Taxes--A Status Report on Ideas for 
Change''; ``Uncertainty and Data Quality Problems Affecting Federal 
Reserve Monetary Policy''; ``Financial Management and CFO Act

States''; and ``Waste and Mismanagement in HUD's Section 8 Project-

  Subcommittee on Employment, Housing and Aviation, to consider one 
draft report, 9 a.m., 2247 Rayburn.
  Subcommittee on Legislation and National Security, hearing on 
National Drug Control Policy: The Andean Strategy, 12 p.m., 2154 
Rayburn.


                             Joint Meetings

  Joint Economic Committee, to hold hearings to examine the 
employment-unemployment situation for September, 9:30 a.m., 2359 
Rayburn Building.
