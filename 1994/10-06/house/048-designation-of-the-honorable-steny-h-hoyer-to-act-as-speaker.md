# DESIGNATION OF THE HONORABLE STENY H. HOYER TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH NOVEMBER 29, 1994

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]


                              {time}  0210
 
   DESIGNATION OF THE HONORABLE STENY H. HOYER TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO 
 TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS THROUGH NOVEMBER 
                                29, 1994

  The Speaker pro tempore (Mr. Peterson of Florida) laid before the 
House the following communication from the Speaker:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, October 7, 1994.
       I hereby designate the Honorable Steny H. Hoyer to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through November 29, 1994.

                                              Thomas S. Foley,

                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the designation is agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
