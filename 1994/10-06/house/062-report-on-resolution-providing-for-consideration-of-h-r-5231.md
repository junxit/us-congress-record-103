# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5231, PROVIDING FOR MANAGEMENT OF PORTIONS OF THE PRESIDIO

**Measures:** H.Res. 576, H.R. 5231

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5231, 
          PROVIDING FOR MANAGEMENT OF PORTIONS OF THE PRESIDIO

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-847) on the resolution (H. Res. 576) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5231) to provide for the management 
of portions of the Presidio under the jurisdiction of the Secretary of 
the Interior, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
```
