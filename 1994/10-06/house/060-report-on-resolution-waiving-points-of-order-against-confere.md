# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON S. 1569, DISADVANTAGED MINORITY HEALTH IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.Res. 574, S. 1569

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
   ON S. 1569, DISADVANTAGED MINORITY HEALTH IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1994

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-845) on the resolution (H. Res. 574) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the Senate 
bill (S. 1569) to amend the Public Health Service Act to establish, 
reauthorize and revise provisions to improve the health of individuals 
from disadvantaged backgrounds, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
