# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
[Congressional Record: October 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Gephardt). After consultation with the 
majority and minority leaders and with their consent and approval, the 
Chair announces that during the joint meeting to hear an address by Mr. 
Nelson Mandela, only the doors immediately opposite the Speaker and 
those on his right and left will be open. No one will be allowed on the 
floor of the House who does not have the privilege of the floor of the 
House. Due to the large attendance which is anticipated, the Chair 
feels that the rule regarding the privilege of the floor must be 
strictly adhered to.
  Children of Members will not be permitted on the floor, and the 
cooperation of all Members is requested.
```
