# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 153, S.J.Res. 196, S. 2099, H.R. 2178, H.R. 2243, H.R. 2815, H.R. 2942, H.R. 3197, H.R. 4506, H.R. 4603, H.R. 4812

```
[Congressional Record: September 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest p. D1043)
  H.R. 4812, to direct the Administrator of General Services to acquire 
by transfer the Old U.S. Mint in San Francisco, California. Signed 
August 25, 1994. (P.L. 103-310)
  H.R. 2178, to amend the Hazardous Materials Transportation Act to 
authorize appropriations for fiscal years 1994, 1995, 1996, and 1997. 
Signed August 26, 1994. (P.L. 103-311)
  H.R. 2243, to amend the Federal Trade Commission Act to extend the 
authorization of appropriations in such Act. Signed August 26, 1994. 
(P.L. 103-312)
  H.R. 2815, to designate a portion of the Farmington River in 
Connecticut as a component of the National Wild and Scenic Rivers 
System. Signed August 26, 1994. (P.L. 103-313)
  H.R. 2942, to designate certain lands in the Commonwealth of Virginia 
as a National Scenic Area for protection of the watershed and scenic 
values, recreation use, and protection of wildlife and their habitat. 
Signed August 26, 1994. (P.L. 103-314)
  H.R. 3197, to redesignate the Post Office building located at 13th 
and Rockland Streets in Reading, Pennsylvania, as the ``Gus Yatron 
Federal Postal Facility''. Signed August 26, 1994. (P.L. 103-315)
  H.R. 4506, making appropriations for energy and water development for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 1995. Signed August 26, 1994. 
(P.L. 103-316)
  H.R. 4603, making appropriations of the Departments of Commerce, 
Justice, and State, the Judiciary, and related agencies programs for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 1995, and making supplemental 
appropriations for these departments and agencies for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1994. Signed August 26, 1994. (P.L. 103-317)
  S. 2099, to establish the Northern Great Plains Rural Development 
Commission. Signed August 26, 1994. (P.L. 103-318)
  S.J. Res. 153, to designate the week beginning on November 20, 1994, 
and ending on November 26, 1994, as ``National Family Caregivers 
Week''. Signed August 26, 1994. (P.L. 103-319)
  S.J. Res. 196, designating September 16, 1994, as ``National POW/MIA 
Recognition Day'' and authorizing display of the National League of 
Families POW/MIA flag. Signed August 26, 1994. (P.L. 103-320)
```
