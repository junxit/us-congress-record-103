# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 4041

**Speaking:** Bliley, Tom (B000556) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4041

```
[Congressional Record: March 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 4041

  Mr. BLILEY. Mr. Speaker, I introduced H.R. 4041 on March 17. 
Unfortunately, the original bill contained a typographical error which 
resulted in the gentleman from New York [Mr. Paxon] being listed as a 
cosponsor. I, therefore ask unanimous consent that the name of the 
gentleman from New York [Mr. Paxon] be removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 
4041.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.
```
