# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: March 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We know gracious God, that we have been given this day to serve 
people in their need and to nurture both life and love, and so we pray 
that we will respond to this brief time with all good grace and with 
thankful hearts. With gratefulness for all Your gifts to us and with 
appreciation for the occasions that this time presents, we offer this 
word of gratitude and praise. Amen.
```
