# APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE COMMISSION ON CHILD AND FAMILY WELFARE

```
[Congressional Record: March 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF THE COMMISSION ON CHILD AND FAMILY WELFARE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Ms. Eshoo). Without objection, pursuant to 
the provisions of section 5(B)(1) of Public Law 102-521, the Chair 
announces the Speaker's appointment of the following members on the 
part of the House to the Commission on Child and Family Welfare.
  Mr. Donald R. Bardill of Tallahassee, FL;
  Mr. George C. Cheek of Spokane, WA;
  Mr. John Guidubaldi of Kent, OH; and
  Mr. Bill Harrington of Tacoma, WA.
  There was no objection.
```
