# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Glenn, John H., Jr. (G000236) [D-OH]

```
[Congressional Record: March 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


    subcommittee on federal services, post office, and civil service

  Mr. GLENN. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the 
Subcommittee on Federal Services, Post Office, and Civil Service, of 
the Committee on Governmental Affairs, will hold a hearing on March 24, 
1994. The Postmaster General of the United States will present the 
Annual Report of the Postal Service.
  The hearing is scheduled for 10 a.m., in room 342 of the Senate 
Dirksen Office Building. For further information, please contact Todd 
Menotti, at 224-2254.

                          ____________________
```
