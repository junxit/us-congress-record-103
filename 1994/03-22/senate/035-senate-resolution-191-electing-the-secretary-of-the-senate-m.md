# SENATE RESOLUTION 191--ELECTING THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE, MARTHA S. POPE

**Measures:** S.Res. 191

```
[Congressional Record: March 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
SENATE RESOLUTION 191--ELECTING THE SECRETARY OF THE SENATE, MARTHA S. 
                                  POPE

  Mr. MITCHELL submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 191

       Resolved, That Martha S. Pope be, and she is hereby, 
     elected Secretary of the Senate, effective April 15, 1994.
```
