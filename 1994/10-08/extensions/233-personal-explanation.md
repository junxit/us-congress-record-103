# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Porter, John Edward (P000444) [R-IL]

**Measures:** S. 1569

```
[Congressional Record: October 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                        HON. JOHN EDWARD PORTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 7, 1994

  Mr. PORTER. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to be present on the House 
floor for the vote on S. 1569, the Minority Health Improvement Act. I 
requested to be paired against this bill, and had I been present, I 
would have voted ``No.''
```
