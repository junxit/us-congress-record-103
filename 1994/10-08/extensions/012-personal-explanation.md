# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Lehman, Richard H. (L000225) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 579

```
[Congressional Record: October 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. RICHARD H. LEHMAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 7, 1994

  Mr. LEHMAN. Mr. Speaker, due to a scheduling conflict I missed 
today's floor vote on House Resolution 579 to apply employee protection 
laws to the House of Representatives. My voting record on this issue 
clearly indicates my strong support for these measures. Had I been 
present, I would have voted in the affirmative on House Resolution 579.
```
