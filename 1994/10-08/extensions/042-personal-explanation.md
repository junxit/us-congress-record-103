# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Hastert, J. Dennis (H000323) [R-IL]

**Measures:** S. 636

```
[Congressional Record: October 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. J. DENNIS HASTERT

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 7, 1994

  Mr. HASTERT. Mr. Speaker, I had to miss the roll call vote on a 
motion to commit S. 636 to the Judiciary Committee on March 17, 1994. 
Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on roll call vote number 
69. I also had to miss the roll call vote on passage of S. 636. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``nay'' on roll call vote number 70.
```
