                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                        HON. JOHN EDWARD PORTER

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 7, 1994

  Mr. PORTER. Mr. Speaker, I was unable to be present on the House 
floor for the vote on H.Res. 571 regarding the application of employee

paired for this resolution, and had I been present, I would have voted
