# PROVIDING FOR THE CONVENING OF THE FIRST SESSION OF THE 104TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Bumpers, Dale (B001057) [D-AR]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 425

```
[Congressional Record: October 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 PROVIDING FOR THE CONVENING OF THE FIRST SESSION OF THE 104TH CONGRESS

  Mr. BUMPERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of House Joint Resolution 425, a 
joint resolution providing for the convening of the first session of 
the 104th Congress, received from the House and at the desk; that the 
joint resolution be deemed read the third time, passed, and the motion 
to reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 425) was deemed read the third 
time, and passed.
```
