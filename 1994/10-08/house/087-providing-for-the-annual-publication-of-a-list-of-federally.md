# PROVIDING FOR THE ANNUAL PUBLICATION OF A LIST OF FEDERALLY RECOGNIZED INDIAN TRIBES

**Speaking:** Bumpers, Dale (B001057) [D-AR]

**Measures:** H.R. 4108

```
[Congressional Record: October 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
PROVIDING FOR THE ANNUAL PUBLICATION OF A LIST OF FEDERALLY RECOGNIZED 
                             INDIAN TRIBES

  Mr. BUMPERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the earlier 
action on H.R. 4108, a bill relating to the recognition of Alaskan 
Native Indian Tribes, be vitiated; that the Senate then proceed to its 
immediate consideration, the bill be read three times, passed, and the 
motion to reconsider be laid upon the table; and, further, that any 
statements on this measure appear in the Record at the appropriate 
place, as though read.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 4108) was passed.
```
