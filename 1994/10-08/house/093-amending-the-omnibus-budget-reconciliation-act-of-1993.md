# AMENDING THE OMNIBUS BUDGET RECONCILIATION ACT OF 1993

**Speaking:** Bumpers, Dale (B001057) [D-AR]

**Measures:** H.R. 5161

```
[Congressional Record: October 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
         AMENDING THE OMNIBUS BUDGET RECONCILIATION ACT OF 1993

  Mr. BUMPERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 5161, a bill making a 
technical correction regarding the prompt sharing of timber sale 
receipts, just received from the House; that bill be read three times, 
passed, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table; and that 
any statements relating to this matter be printed in the Record at the 
appropriate place.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 5161) was passed.
```
