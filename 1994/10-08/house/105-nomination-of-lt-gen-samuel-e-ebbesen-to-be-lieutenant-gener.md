# NOMINATION OF LT. GEN. SAMUEL E. EBBESEN, TO BE LIEUTENANT GENERAL

**Speaking:** Bumpers, Dale (B001057) [D-AR]

```
[Congressional Record: October 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
   NOMINATION OF LT. GEN. SAMUEL E. EBBESEN, TO BE LIEUTENANT GENERAL

  Mr. BUMPERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to consider the 
following nomination reported today by the Committee on Armed Services, 
and that the Senate proceed to its immediate consideration: Lt. Gen. 
Samuel E. Ebbesen, to be lieutenant general.
  I further ask unanimous consent that the nominee be confirmed; that 
any statements appear in the Record as if read; that the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action; and that the Senate return to 
legislative session.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the nomination was confirmed.
```
