# AWARDING THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL TO RABBI SCHNEERSON

**Speaking:** Bumpers, Dale (B001057) [D-AR]

**Measures:** H.R. 4497

```
[Congressional Record: October 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
       AWARDING THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL TO RABBI SCHNEERSON

  Mr. BUMPERS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 4497, a bill to award 
the Congressional Gold Medal to Rabbi Schneerson, just received from 
the House, that the bill be read three times, passed, the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table; further that any statements on this 
measure appear in the Record at the appropriate place as though read.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection to the several 
requests? Hearing no objection, they are agreed to.
   The bill (H.R. 4497) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
