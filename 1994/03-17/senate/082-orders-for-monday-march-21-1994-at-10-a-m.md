# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 21, 1994, AT 10 A.M.

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 208

```
[Congressional Record: March 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
             ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 21, 1994, AT 10 A.M.

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
stand in recess until 10 a.m. Monday, March 21; that following the 
prayer, the Journal of the proceedings be approved to date; that the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; 
that at 10 a.m., the Senate proceed to the consideration of Calendar 
No. 369, S. 208, the parks concessions bill, as previously ordered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
