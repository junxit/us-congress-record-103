                  MILITARY NOMINATIONS AND PROMOTIONS

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the military nominations and promotions reported today by 
the Committee on Armed Services. I further ask unanimous consent that 
the nominees be confirmed en bloc; that any statements appear in the 
Record as if read; that upon confirmation, the motions to reconsider be 
laid upon the table en bloc; that the President be immediately notified 
of the Senate's action; that the Senate return to legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? The Chair hears none, and 
it is so ordered.
