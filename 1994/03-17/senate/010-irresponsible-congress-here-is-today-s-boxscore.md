# IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: March 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
            IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, the Federal debt stood at 
$4,550,473,471,421.39 as of the close of business on Wednesday, March 
16. Averaged out, every man, woman, and child in America owes a part of 
this massive debt, and that per capita share is $17,454.09.
```
