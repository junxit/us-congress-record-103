# NATIONAL HISTORICAL PUBLICATIONS AND RECORDS COMMISSION APPROPRIATIONS AUTHORIZATION ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 2139

```
[Congressional Record: March 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
NATIONAL HISTORICAL PUBLICATIONS AND RECORDS COMMISSION APPROPRIATIONS 
                           AUTHORIZATION ACT

                                 ______


                      LIEBERMAN AMENDMENT NO. 1549

  Mr. FORD (for Mr. Lieberman) proposed an amendment to the bill (H.R. 
2139) to amend title 44, United States Code, to authorize 
appropriations for the National Historical Publications and Records 
Commission; as follows:

       Strike out all after the enacting clause and insert in lieu 
     thereof the following:

     SECTION 1. AUTHORIZATION OF APPROPRIATIONS.

       Section 2504(f)(1) of title 44, United States Code, is 
     amended--
       (1) in subparagraph (B), by striking out ``and'' after the 
     semicolon;
       (2) in subparagraph (C), by striking out the period and 
     inserting in lieu thereof a semicolon; and
       (3) by adding at the end the following new subparagraphs:
       ``(D) $6,000,000 for fiscal year 1994;
       ``(E) $7,000,000 for fiscal year 1995;
       ``(F) $8,000,000 for fiscal year 1996; and
       ``(G) $10,000,000 for fiscal year 1997.''.
```
