# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1901

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 1901

```
[Congressional Record: March 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1901

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Agriculture 
Committee be discharged from further consideration of S. 1901, a bill 
relating to the purchase and eradication of swine infected with or 
exposed to brucellosis; and that the measure then be placed on the 
calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.
```
