# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gilman, Benjamin A. (G000212) [R-NY]

```
[Congressional Record: March 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. GILMAN. Mr. Speaker, I regret that my being involved in an event 
in my home State of New York prevented me from voting on rollcall Nos. 
61, No. 62, and No. 63. Had I been present, on Rollcall 61 I would have 
voted ``present.'' On rollcalls 62 and 63, I would have voted ``nay.''
```
