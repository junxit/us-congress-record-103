# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Payne, Donald M. (P000149) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 103

```
[Congressional Record: March 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. PAYNE of New Jersey. Mr. President, I was unavoidably delayed in 
returning to Washington from my district today as my flight was unable 
to leave the airport due to mechanical problems. Had I been present for 
the vote on passage of the balanced budget amendment, House Joint 
Resolution 103, I would have voted ``no.''
```
