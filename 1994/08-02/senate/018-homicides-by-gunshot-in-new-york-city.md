# HOMICIDES BY GUNSHOT IN NEW YORK CITY

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY]

```
[Congressional Record: August 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                 HOMICIDES BY GUNSHOT IN NEW YORK CITY

  Mr. MOYNIHAN. As I have done each week the 103d Congress has been in 
session, I rise to report to the Senate on the gruesome toll taken by 
gun violence in New York City. This past week, 17 persons were killed 
by gunshot, bringing this year's total to 584.
```
