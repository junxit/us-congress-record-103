# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: August 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  By the power of Your spirit we pray O God, that we will have eyes to 
see any injustice in our world; may we have strong hands to do the 
works of peace; may we have caring hearts that are not content with 
vain promises or gestures; may we have souls that sense the unity of 
creation and our solidarity with every person, so that all we do or say 
or think may be to Your glory, now and evermore. Amen.
```
