# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutiérrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

```
[Congressional Record: September 20, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 20, 1994

  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, at the end of the afternoon of Monday, 
September 19, 1994, I was unavoidably absent from this Chamber and 
therefore missed rollcall vote No. 424, a vote to commend President 
Clinton and the special delegation to Haiti, and to support the United 
States Armed Forces in Haiti. I want the Record to show that if I had 
had the opportunity to be in this Chamber when this important vote was 
cast, I would have certainly voted ``aye.''
```
