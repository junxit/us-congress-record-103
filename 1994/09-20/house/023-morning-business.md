# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: September 20, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
February 11, 1994, and June 10, 1994, the Chair will now recognize 
Members from lists submitted by the majority and minority leaders for 
morning hour debates. The Chair will alternate recognition between the 
parties, with each party limited to not to exceed 30 minutes, and each 
Member, except the majority and minority leaders, limited to not to 
exceed 5 minutes.
```
