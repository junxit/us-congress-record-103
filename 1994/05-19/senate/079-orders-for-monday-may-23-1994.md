# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 23, 1994

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1933

```
[Congressional Record: May 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 23, 1994

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
stand in recess until 11:30 a.m., Monday, May 23; that following the 
Prayer, the Journal of proceedings be deemed approved to date and the 
time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in the day; that 
there then be a period for morning business, not to extend beyond 12 
noon, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 minutes 
each; with Senator Murray recognized to speak for up to 15 minutes; 
that at 12 noon, the Senate proceed to the consideration of Calendar 
No. 423, H.R. 1933, under the conditions and limitations of a previous 
unanimous-consent agreement.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
