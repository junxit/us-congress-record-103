# PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR--H.R. 3355

**Speaking:** Biden, Joseph R., Jr. (B000444) [D-DE]

**Measures:** H.R. 3355

```
[Congressional Record: May 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR--H.R. 3355

  Mr. BIDEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Andrew 
Plepler, a Department of Justice employee detailed to the Senate 
Judiciary Committee staff, be allowed the privileges of the floor 
during the appointment of conferees and consideration of the conference 
report on H.R. 3355, the Violent Crime Control and Law Enforcement Act 
of 1994.
```
