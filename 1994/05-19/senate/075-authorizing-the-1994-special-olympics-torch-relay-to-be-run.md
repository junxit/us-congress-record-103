# AUTHORIZING THE 1994 SPECIAL OLYMPICS TORCH RELAY TO BE RUN THROUGH THE CAPITOL GROUNDS

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 236

```
[Congressional Record: May 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
AUTHORIZING THE 1994 SPECIAL OLYMPICS TORCH RELAY TO BE RUN THROUGH THE 
                            CAPITOL GROUNDS

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of House Concurrent Resolution 
236, a concurrent resolution authorizing the running of the Special 
Olympics Torch Relay through the Capitol grounds just received form the 
House; that the concurrent resolution be agreed to, and the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
