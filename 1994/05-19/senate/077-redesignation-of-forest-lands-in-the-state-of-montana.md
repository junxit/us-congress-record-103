# REDESIGNATION OF FOREST LANDS IN THE STATE OF MONTANA

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY], Brown, Hank (B000919) [R-CO]

**Measures:** S. 2137

```
[Congressional Record: May 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
         REDESIGNATION OF FOREST LANDS IN THE STATE OF MONTANA

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I understand that S. 2137 was introduced 
earlier today. Is that correct?
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask that the bill be read for the first 
time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2137) to designate certain National Forest lands 
     in the State of Montana as wilderness, to release other 
     National Forest lands in the State of Montana for multiple 
     use management, and for other purposes.

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask for its second reading.
  Mr. BROWN. I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will receive its second reading on the next legislative day.
```
