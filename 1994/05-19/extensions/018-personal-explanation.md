# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gutiérrez, Luis V. (G000535) [D-IL]

```
[Congressional Record: May 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. LUIS V. GUTIERREZ

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 19, 1994

  Mr. GUTIERREZ. Mr. Speaker, at the end of the afternoon of Thursday, 
April 12, 1994, I was unavoidably absent from this Chamber and 
therefore missed rollcall vote No. 168, a vote for passage of the 
reauthorization of the Economic Development Administration, rollcall 
vote No. 169 regarding Social Security taxes for domestic workers and 
rollcall vote No. 170 to reauthorize Head Start. I deeply regret I was 
unable to cast my votes but could I have been here I would have voted 
``yes.''
```
