**Measures:** H.R. 4453

```
[Congressional Record: May 19, 1994]

  REPORT ON H.R. 4453, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 1995

privileged report (Rept. No. 103-516) on the bill (H.R. 4453) making
```
