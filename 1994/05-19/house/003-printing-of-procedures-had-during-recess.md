# PRINTING OF PROCEDURES HAD DURING RECESS

**Speaking:** Wise, Robert E., Jr. (W000654) [D-WV]

```
[Congressional Record: May 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                PRINTING OF PROCEDURES HAD DURING RECESS

  Mr. WISE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the proceedings 
had during the recess be printed in the Congressional Record and that 
all Members and former Members who spoke during the recess have the 
privilege of revising and extending their remarks.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from West Virginia?
  There was no objection.
```
