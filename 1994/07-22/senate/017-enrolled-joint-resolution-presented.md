# ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 172

```
[Congressional Record: July 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                  ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on July 22, 1994 she had 
presented to the President of the United States, the following enrolled 
joint resolution:

       S.J. Res. 172. Joint Resolution designating May 29, 1995, 
     through June 6, 1995, as a ``Time for the National Observance 
     of fiftieth Anniversary of World War II.''
```
