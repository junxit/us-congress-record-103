# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 25, 1994, AT 9:30 A.M.

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
[Congressional Record: July 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
            RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JULY 25, 1994, AT 9:30 A.M.

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, at the request of the distinguished majority 
leader, I move, in accordance with the order previously entered, that 
the Senate now stand in recess until the hour of 9:30 a.m. on Monday 
next.
  The motion was agreed to, and at 8:47 p.m., the Senate recessed until 
Monday, July 25, 1994, at 9:30 a.m.
```
