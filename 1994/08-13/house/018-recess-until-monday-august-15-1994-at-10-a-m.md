# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, AUGUST 15, 1994, AT 10 A.M.

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
[Congressional Record: August 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
            RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, AUGUST 15, 1994, AT 10 A.M.

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, If there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, and I see no other Senator seeking 
recognition, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in 
recess as previously ordered.
```
