# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 2947, H.R. 4970

```
[Congressional Record: August 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  At 8:09 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bills:

       H.R. 2947. An act to extend for an additional two years the 
     authorization of the Black Revolutionary War Patriots 
     Foundation to establish a memorial; and
       H.R. 4970. An act to designate the United States courthouse 
     under construction in St. Louis, Missouri, as the ``Thomas F. 
     Eagleton United States Courthouse.''
```
