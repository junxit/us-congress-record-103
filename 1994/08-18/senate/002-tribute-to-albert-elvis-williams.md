# TRIBUTE TO ALBERT ELVIS WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Heflin, Howell (H000445) [D-AL]

```
[Congressional Record: August 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    TRIBUTE TO ALBERT ELVIS WILLIAMS

  Mr. HEFLIN. Mr. President, Albert Elvis ``Al'' Williams, the manager 
of Listerhill Employees Credit Union in Sheffield, AL, for 22 years, 
died on August 2. He had served as chairman of the Credit Union 
National Association as well as holding other national offices.
  Al was the first vice president of the World Council of Credit 
Unions; chairman of the Credit Union National Association's Election 
and Governance Task Force; vice chairman of its bylaws committee; and a 
member of the governmental affairs committee.
  He was the former chairman of the Alabama Credit Union League; a 
board member of the Alabama Credit Union Administration; chairman of 
the Alabama Credit Union Legislative Action Council; and president of 
the State Credit Union Forum. Al was named Outstanding Alumnus of the 
Year by the University of North Alabama in 1988.
  Al Williams contributed a great deal to his community and State over 
the course of his career, and he will be sorely missed. I extend my 
sincerest condolences to his wife, Charlotte Young Williams, and her 
entire family in the wake of their painful loss.
```
