# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 3800, H.R. 4624, H.R. 4683, H.R. 4779

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Energy and Commerce: Ordered reported as amended the 
following bills: H.R. 4779, State and Local Government Interstate Waste 
Control Act; and H.R. 4683, Flow Control Act of 1994.
SUPERFUND REFORM ACT
Committee on Ways and Means: Began consideration of H.R. 3800, 
Superfund Reform Act of 1994.
  Will continue tomorrow.

Joint Meetings
APPROPRIATIONS--VA/HUD
Conferees agreed to file a conference report on the differences between 
the Senate- and House-passed versions of H.R. 4624, making 
appropriations for fiscal year 1995 for the Departments of Veterans 
Affairs and Housing and Urban Development, and certain independent 
agencies.
```
