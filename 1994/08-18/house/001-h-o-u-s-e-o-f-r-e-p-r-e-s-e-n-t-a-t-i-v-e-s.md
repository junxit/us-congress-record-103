             H O U S E  O F  R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S


Vol. 140


WASHINGTON, THURSDAY, AUGUST 18, 1994

No. 117


House of Representatives

  The House met at 10 a.m.
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We pray, O gracious God, that You would strengthen us in our inner 
being and give us the grace to be at peace in body, mind, and spirit. 
Your Word reminds us that You are our shepherd and that Your love is 
for the welfare of Your flock. We pray that You will bless us with the 
gift of a sturdy faith, a faith that withstands the pressures of the 
day. Give us the assurance that deep in our hearts is Your peace that 
passes all human understanding. Amen.
