# MEASURE SEQUENTIALLY REFERRED

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 687

```
[Congressional Record: March 1, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                     MEASURE SEQUENTIALLY REFERRED

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Calendar No. 
330, S. 687, the Product Liability Fairness Act be sequentially 
referred to the Committee on the Judiciary for a period not to extend 
beyond April 11, 1994, that if the Committee on the Judiciary has not 
reported the measure within that time, then the bill be automatically 
discharged and returned to the Calendar.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
