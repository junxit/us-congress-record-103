# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 10 A.M.

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
[Congressional Record: March 1, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 10 A.M.

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today--I see no Senator seeking recognition--I now 
ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess as previously 
ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate at 9:05 p.m., recessed until 
Wednesday, March 2, 1994, at 10 a.m.
```
