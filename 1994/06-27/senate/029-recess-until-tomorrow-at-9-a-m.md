# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Rockefeller, John D., IV (R000361) [D-WV]

                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. ROCKEFELLER. Mr. President, if there is no further business to
