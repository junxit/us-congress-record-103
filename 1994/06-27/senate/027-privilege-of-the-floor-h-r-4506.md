# PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR--H.R. 4506

**Speaking:** Rockefeller, John D., IV (R000361) [D-WV]

**Measures:** H.R. 4506

```
[Congressional Record: June 27, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   PRIVILEGE OF THE FLOOR--H.R. 4506

    
    
  Mr. ROCKEFELLER. Mr. President, on behalf of Senator Johnston, I ask 
unanimous consent that Dr. Robert Simon, Science Fellow to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, be granted floor privileges 
for the duration of H.R. 4506, a bill making appropriations for energy 
and water development for fiscal year ending September 30, 1995, and 
for other purposes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
