# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--FILING OF AMENDMENTS TO S. 4

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** S. 4

```
[Congressional Record: March 11, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
       UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--FILING OF AMENDMENTS TO S. 4

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Monday, 
March 14, notwithstanding the recess of the Senate, with respect to the 
cloture motions filed regarding S. 4, that Senators with listed 
amendments may file first-degree amendments until 1 p.m. in their 
respective cloakrooms; further, that with respect to second-degree 
amendments, Senators may file until 9:15 a.m. on Tuesday, March 15.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
