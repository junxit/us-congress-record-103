# CLOTURE MOTION

**Measures:** S. 4

```
[Congressional Record: March 11, 1994]

                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion, previously 
filed by the majority leader.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of Rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close the debate on the Modified 
     Committee Substitute to S. 4, the National Competitiveness 
     Act of 1993.

           Ford, James J. Exon, Jay Rockefeller, Don Riegle, 
           George Mitchell, Tom Daschle, Byron L. Dorgan, Barbara 
           Boxer, Patrick Leahy, Fritz Hollings, Jeff Bingaman, 
           Paul Simon, J. Lieberman, John F. Kerry.
```
