# IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: March 11, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
            IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business on Thursday, 
March 10, the Federal debt stood at $4,546,800,625,410.70, meaning that 
on a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$17,440.00 as his or her share of that debt.
```
