# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1933, H.R. 4277

```
[Congressional Record: August 11, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 7:03 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Goetz, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
agrees to the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 1933) to 
authorize appropriations for the Martin Luther King, Jr. Federal 
Holiday Commission, and to support the planning and performance of 
national service opportunities in conjunction with the Federal legal 
holiday honoring the birthday of Martin Luther King, Jr.
  The message further announced that the House agrees to the report of 
the committee of conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses 
on the amendment of the Senate to the bill (H.R. 4277) to establish the 
Social Security Administration as an independent agency and to make 
other improvements in the old-age survivors, and disability insurance 
program.
```
