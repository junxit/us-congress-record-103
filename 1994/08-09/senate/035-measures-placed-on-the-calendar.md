# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 1562, H.R. 3964, H.R. 4812

```
[Congressional Record: August 9, 1994]

                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills were read the first and second times by unanimous 
consent and placed on the calendar:

       H.R. 1562. An act to amend title V of Public Law 96-550, 
     designating the Chaco Culture Archeological Protection sites, 
     and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3964. An act to expand the boundaries of the Santa Fe 
     National Forest, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4812. An act to direct the Administrator of General 
     Services to acquire by transfer the Old U.S. Mint in San 
     Francisco, California, and for other purposes.
```
