# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Clinger, William F., Jr. (C000523) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4301

```
[Congressional Record: June 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                      HON. WILLIAM F. CLINGER, JR.

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, June 10, 1994

  Mr. CLINGER. Mr. Speaker, on Wednesday, June 8, 1994, I was absent 
and missed five recorded votes on amendments to the fiscal year 1995 
Department of Defense Authorization (H.R. 4301). If I had been present, 
I would have voted ``aye'' on rollcall vote 217, ``no'' on rollcall 
vote 218, ``no'' on rollcall vote 219, ``no'' on rollcall vote 220, and 
``aye'' on rollcall vote 221.
```
