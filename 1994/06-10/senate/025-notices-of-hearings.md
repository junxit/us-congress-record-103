# NOTICES OF HEARINGS

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
[Congressional Record: June 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          NOTICES OF HEARINGS


                 committee on rules and administration

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, the Senate Rules Committee will meet on 
Thursday, June 16, 1994, at 9:30 a.m., in SR-301, to continue its 
markup of legislation on legislative reorganization and consider three 
original resolutions to amend the Standing Rules of the Senate with 
respect to committee procedure, floor procedure, and germaneness of 
amendments.
  The hearing previously scheduled for Thursday, June 16, at 9:30 a.m., 
on Senate Resolutions 69, 157, and 158, to amend the Standing Rules of 
the Senate with respect to unfunded Federal mandates, has been 
canceled. It is the understanding of the Senate Rules Committee that 
the Senate Committee on Governmental Affairs plans to consider 
legislation on unfunded Federal mandates at a markup on June 16. Any 
rescheduling of a Rules Committee hearing on this matter is subject to 
the actions of the Governmental Affairs Committee.
```
