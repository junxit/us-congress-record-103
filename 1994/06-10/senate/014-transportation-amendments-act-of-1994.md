# TRANSPORTATION AMENDMENTS ACT OF 1994

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 1758

```
[Congressional Record: June 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                 TRANSPORTATION AMENDMENTS ACT OF 1994

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of Calendar No. 432, H.R. 1758, a bill to 
revise and codify certain laws relating to transportation; that the 
bill be read a third time and passed, the motion to reconsider laid 
upon the table, and any statements thereon appear in the Record at the 
appropriate place.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 1758) was passed.
```
