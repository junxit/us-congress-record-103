# IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE'S TODAY'S BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: June 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
            IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE'S TODAY'S BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business on Thursday, 
June 9, the Federal debt stood at $4,601,856,248,523.77. This means 
that on a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$17,651.18 as his or her share of that debt.
```
