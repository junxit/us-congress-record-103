# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Mfume, Kweisi (M000687) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 222

```
[Congressional Record: April 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. MFUME. Mr. Speaker, due to some pressing business in my 
congressional district in Baltimore, I was unable to be on the floor 
during the vote on House Concurrent Resolution 222, placing a bust of 
Raoul Wallenberg in the Capitol.
  Mr. Wallenberg is an extremely deserving individual of this high 
praise, and had I been here I certainly would have voted ``aye.''
```
