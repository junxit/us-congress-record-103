# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: April 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  You have created us as one people, O gracious God, and we share 
together in the hopes and fears that touch the human community. Yet, O 
God, we know that our unity is fragmented and our solidarity can be 
shattered and we do not witness to the wholeness and harmony that is 
Your gift to us. With all our differing circumstances and backgrounds 
and situations, may we hold strong to the high purposes to which we 
have been called, and express in our words and deeds our shared message 
of faith and hope and love. Bless all Your people, O God, this day and 
every day. Amen.
```
