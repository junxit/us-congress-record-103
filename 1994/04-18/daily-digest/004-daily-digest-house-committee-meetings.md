# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 2884

```
Committee Meetings
COMMERCE, JUSTICE, STATE AND JUDICIARY APPROPRIATIONS
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Commerce, Justice, State, 
and Judiciary held a hearing on the FCC. Testimony was heard from Reed 
E. Hundt, Chairman, FCC.

Joint Meetings
SCHOOL-TO-WORK OPPORTUNITIES ACT
Conferees on Thursday, April 14, agreed to file a conference report on 
the differences between the Senate and House-passed versions of H.R. 
2884, to establish a national framework for the development of school-
to-work opportunities systems in all States.
```
