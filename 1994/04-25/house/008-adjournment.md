# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Montgomery, G. V. (Sonny) (M000865) [D-MS]

**Measures:** H.Res. 411

```
[Congressional Record: April 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. MONTGOMERY. Madam Speaker, pursuant to House Resolution 411, I 
move that the House do now adjourn in memory of the late Honorable 
Richard M. Nixon, 37th President of the United States.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 12 o'clock and 20 minutes 
p.m.) pursuant to House Resolution 411, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Tuesday, April 26, 1994, at 10:30 a.m., in memory of the late 
Honorable Richard M. Nixon.
```
