# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Bradley, Bill (B001225) [D-NJ]

```
[Congressional Record: June 20, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


           committee on agriculture, nutrition, and forestry

  Mr. BRADLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry be allowed to meet 
during the session of the Senate on Monday, June 20, 1994, at 9:30 
a.m., in SR-332, on nominations pending before the committee.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


                  subcommittee on international trade

  Mr. BRADLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Subcommittee on International Trade of the Committee on Finance be 
permitted to meet today, June 20, 1994, at 1:30 p.m., to hear testimony 
on the administration's proposal to renew the Generalized System of 
Preferences Program.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
