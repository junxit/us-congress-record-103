# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 38

```
[Congressional Record: August 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent it be in order for 
the Chair to lay before the Senate en bloc messages from the House on 
the following concurrent resolutions: S. Con. Res. 38, 39, 40, and 41; 
that the Senate proceed en bloc to their immediate consideration; that 
the Senate concur en bloc in the House amendments to the concurrent 
resolutions; that the motions to reconsider be laid upon the table en 
bloc; and that the consideration of these items appear individually in 
the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
