# PRINTING OF RICHARD M. NIXON EULOGIES AND ENCOMIUMS

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 248

```
[Congressional Record: August 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
          PRINTING OF RICHARD M. NIXON EULOGIES AND ENCOMIUMS

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Rules Committee 
be discharged from further consideration of H. Con. Res. 248, a 
concurrent resolution providing for the printing of the eulogies of the 
late President of the United States, Richard M. Nixon; that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to; that the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table; and that any statements relating thereto appear in 
the Record at the appropriate place.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 248) was agreed to.
```
