# H O U S E O F R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S

```

             H O U S E  O F  R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S


Vol. 140


WASHINGTON, WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 10, 1994

No. 110


House of Representatives

  The House met at 10 a.m.
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We are grateful, O God, for those to whom great responsibility has 
been given and from whom much is required. For leaders of our Nation 
who have been set aside with special obligations and duties, we offer 
this prayer of support and intercession. May they sense more fully than 
anyone the tasks of their office, the commitment toward justice, a 
fervor for doing the works of peace, a devotion to the neediest among 
us, and an appreciation to strengthen the resources of the land. May 
each of us sense our own obligation to be good stewards of the 
traditions given to us to do justice, to love mercy, and to walk humbly 
with You. Amen.
```
