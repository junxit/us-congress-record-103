# REPUBLICANS RALLY AROUND PRESIDENT ON CORRECT POLICIES

**Speaking:** Dreier, David (D000492) [R-CA]

```
[Congressional Record: August 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
         REPUBLICANS RALLY AROUND PRESIDENT ON CORRECT POLICIES

  (Mr. DREIER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DREIER. Mr. Speaker, at a time when the President of the United 
States is attempting to launch a Government takeover of one-seventh of 
the economy and the Democrat leadership in this House is attempting to 
obliterate something the American people have called for, that being 
Congressional reform, I find it very interesting that yesterday 
Republicans in this House stepped forward with nearly 75 percent of our 
membership supporting the cause of human rights in China and job 
creation here in the United States.
  Yes, the President has had a pattern of criticizing Republicans for 
partisanship. But when the President is right, Republicans step forward 
and do everything that we possibly can to support those correct 
policies. I think it is very important, Mr. Speaker, that the record 
show that.
```
