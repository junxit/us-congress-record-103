# WELCOME TO REV. HARRY F. ANDREWS

**Speaking:** Hefner, W. G. (Bill) (H000448) [D-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: August 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    WELCOME TO REV. HARRY F. ANDREWS

  (Mr. HEFNER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HEFNER. Mr. Speaker, it is a real pleasure to have Reverend 
Andrews and his wife and his lovely daughter, Jennifer, who has been a 
page here for some time; and I understand that he left North Carolina 
last night about 11 o'clock to get here in time for the prayer today. I 
tell you, that has got to be real dedication.
  We are just more than happy to have you here today. With that 
beautiful prayer, I wish you could stay around and make things more 
palatable for us today.
  Again, I want to thank you for your dedication to come here and share 
these few moments with us. Thank you very much.

                          ____________________
```
