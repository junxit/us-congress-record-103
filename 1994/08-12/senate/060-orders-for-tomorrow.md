# ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 2351

```
[Congressional Record: August 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

  Mr. MOYNIHAN. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
stand in recess until 9:30 .a.m., Saturday, August 13, that following 
the prayer, the Journal of proceedings be deemed approved to date and 
the time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in the day; 
that there then be a period for morning business not to extend beyond 
10 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein, with the period for 
morning business under the control of the Republican leader or his 
designee; that at 10 a.m., the Senate resume consideration of S. 2351, 
the Health Security Act, under the conditions and limitations as 
provided for in a previous unanimous consent agreement.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
