# IS CONGRESS IRRESPONSIBLE? YOU BE THE JUDGE OF THAT

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: August 1, 1994]


 
          IS CONGRESS IRRESPONSIBLE? YOU BE THE JUDGE OF THAT

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business on Friday, July 
29, the Federal debt stood at $4,636,361,778,400.62. This means that on 
a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$17,783.53 as his or her share of that debt.
```
