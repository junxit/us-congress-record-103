# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
[Congressional Record: August 1, 1994]


 
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess, as previously ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:09 p.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, August 2, 1994, at 9 a.m.
```
