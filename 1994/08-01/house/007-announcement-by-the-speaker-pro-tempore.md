# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
[Congressional Record: August 1, 1994]


 
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. (Mr. Frank of Massachusetts). Pursuant to 
the provisions of clause 5 of rule I, the Chair announces that he will 
postpone further proceedings today on each motion to suspend the rules 
on which a recorded vote or the yeas and nays are ordered, or on which 
the vote is objected to under clause 4 of rule XV.
  Such rollcall votes, if postponed, will be taken at the end of 
legislative business today, but not before 5 p.m.
```
