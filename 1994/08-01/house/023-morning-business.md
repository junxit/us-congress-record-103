# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: August 1, 1994]


 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the order of the House of 
February 11, 1994, and June 10, 1994, the Chair will now recognize 
Members from lists submitted by the majority and minority leaders for 
morning hour debates.
  The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with each 
party limited to not to exceed 30 minutes, and each Member except the 
majority and minority leader limited to not to exceed 5 minutes.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from New Mexico [Mr. Richardson] 
for 5 minutes.
```
