```
[Congressional Record: August 1, 1994]


 
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman from Arizona [Mr. Stump] will 
come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of Allegiance.
  Mr. STUMP led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the flag of the <gr-thn-eq>United 
     States of America and to the Republic for which it stands, 
     one Nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice
```
