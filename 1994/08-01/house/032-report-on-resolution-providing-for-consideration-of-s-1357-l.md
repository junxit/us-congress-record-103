# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. 1357, LITTLE TRAVERSE BAY BANDS OF ODAWA INDIANS AND LITTLE RIVER BAND OF OTTAWA INDIANS ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 501, S. 1357

```
[Congressional Record: August 1, 1994]


 
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF S. 1357, LITTLE 
  TRAVERSE BAY BANDS OF ODAWA INDIANS AND LITTLE RIVER BAND OF OTTAWA 
                              INDIANS ACT

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-647) on the resolution (H. Res. 501) providing 
for consideration of the bill (S. 1357) to reaffirm and clarify the 
Federal relationships of the Little Traverse Bay Bands of Odawa Indians 
and the Little River Band of Ottawa Indians as distinct federally 
recognized Indian tribes, and for other purposes, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
