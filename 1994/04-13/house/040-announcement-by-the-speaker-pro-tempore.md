# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
[Congressional Record: April 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of clause 5 of 
rule I, the Chair announces that he will reduce to a minimum of 5 
minutes the period of time within which a vote by electronic device may 
be taken on the additional motions to suspend the rules and on which 
the Chair has postponed further proceedings.
```
