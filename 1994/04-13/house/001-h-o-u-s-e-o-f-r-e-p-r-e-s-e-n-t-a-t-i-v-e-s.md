```

             H O U S E  O F  R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S


Vol. 140


WASHINGTON, WEDNESDAY, APRIL 13, 1994

No. 39


House of Representatives

  The House met at 2 p.m.
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Let us pray using the words of Psalm 103:
  Bless the Lord, O my soul; and all that is within me, bless His holy 
name!
  Bless the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all His benefits,
  Who forgives all your iniquity, who heals all your diseases,
  Who redeems your life from the Pit, who crowns you with steadfast 
love and mercy,
  Who satisfies you with good as long as you live so that your youth is 
renewed like the eagle's. Amen.
```
