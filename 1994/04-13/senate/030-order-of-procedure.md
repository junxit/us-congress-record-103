# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME], Cochran, Thad (C000567) [R-MS]

```
[Congressional Record: April 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. MITCHELL. Madam President, we have been discussing with our 
colleagues the procedure for a possible agreement for handling the last 
of the three contested nominations which we were to deal with. I am 
awaiting a response from our colleagues.
  So anticipating that I will receive that response momentarily, I will 
for now suggest the absence of a quorum, although I intend to have an 
announcement on dealing with that matter, or hope to have that 
announcement, very shortly.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. COCHRAN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
