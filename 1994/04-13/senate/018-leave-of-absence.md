# LEAVE OF ABSENCE

**Speaking:** Mathews, Harlan (M000236) [D-TN]

```
[Congressional Record: April 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            LEAVE OF ABSENCE

  Mr. MATHEWS. Mr. President, pursuant to rule VI, paragraph 2, of the 
Senate rules, I ask unanimous consent that I be permitted to be absent 
from the Senate from 4:30 p.m. today on through the end of the session 
today. I have business back in my State that I need to take care of. I 
will be returning tomorrow in time for the session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. MATHEWS. I thank the Chair and I yield the floor.
  Mr. DORGAN addressed the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from North Dakota, Mr. Dorgan.
```
