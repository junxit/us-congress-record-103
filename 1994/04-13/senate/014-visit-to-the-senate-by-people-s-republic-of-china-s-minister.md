# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA'S MINISTER OF FOREIGN TRADE

**Speaking:** Johnston, J. Bennett (J000189) [D-LA]

```
[Congressional Record: April 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
VISIT TO THE SENATE BY PEOPLE'S REPUBLIC OF CHINA'S MINISTER OF FOREIGN 
                                 TRADE

  Mr. JOHNSTON. Mr. President, it is with pleasure that I introduce to 
the United States Senate Madam Wu Yi, the Minister of Foreign Trade 
from the People's Republic of China, who is standing in the back of the 
Chamber. She is a very distinguished Minister who is bringing the 
People's Republic of China to a market economy. [Applause.]
```
