# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Cochran, Thad (C000567) [R-MS], Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI], D'Amato, Alfonse (D000018) [R-NY], Heflin, Howell (H000445) [D-AL], Simpson, Alan K. (S000429) [R-WY]

**Measures:** S. 2011, S. 2012, S. 2013, S. 2014, S. 2015

```
[Congressional Record: April 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. COCHRAN (for himself and Mr. Lott):
       S. 2011. A bill to suspend temporarily the duty on certain 
     textile-manufacturing machinery; to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. FEINGOLD:
       S. 2012. A bill to amend the Civil Rights Act of 1964 and 
     other civil rights laws to prohibit employers from requiring 
     employees to submit claims relating to employment 
     discrimination to mandatory arbitration; to the Committee on 
     Labor and Human Resources.
           By Mr. D'AMATO:
       S. 2013. A bill to renew patent numbered 3,387,268, 
     relating to a quotation monitoring unit, for a period of 10 
     years; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. HEFLIN:
       S. 2014. A bill to amend the coastwise trade laws to 
     clarify their application to certain passenger vessels, and 
     for other purposes; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, 
     and Transportation.
           By Mr. SIMPSON:
       S. 2015. A bill to provide for daylight saving time on an 
     expanded basis, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
```
