# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 3790

**Speaking:** Long, Jill L. (L000420) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3790

```
[Congressional Record: May 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
         REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 3790

  Ms. LONG. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the names of the 
gentleman from Iowa [Mr. Grandy], the gentleman from Ohio [Mr. 
Strickland], the gentleman from Nebraska [Mr. Barrett], and the 
gentlewoman from Kansas [Mrs. Meyers], be removed as cosponsors of H.R. 
3790.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Indiana?
  There was no objection.
```
