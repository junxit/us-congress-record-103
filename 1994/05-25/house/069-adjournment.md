# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Moakley, John Joseph (M000834) [D-MA], Walker, Robert S. (W000068) [R-PA]

```
[Congressional Record: May 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. MOAKLEY. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Massachusetts [Mr. Moakley].
  The question was taken; and the Speaker pro tempore announced that 
the ayes appeared to have it.
  Mr. WALKER. Mr. Speaker, on that I demand a division.
  On a division (demanded by Mr. Walker) there were--yeas 4, nays 3.
  So the motion was agreed to: accordingly (at 12 o'clock and 15 
minutes a.m.), the House adjourned until Thursday, May 26, 1994, at 10 
a.m.
```
