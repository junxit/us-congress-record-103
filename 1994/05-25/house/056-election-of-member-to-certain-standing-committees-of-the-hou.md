# ELECTION OF MEMBER TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Michel, Robert H. (M000692) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.Res. 442

```
[Congressional Record: May 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
     ELECTION OF MEMBER TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. MICHEL. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution (H. Res. 
442) and ask for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 442

       Resolved, That Representative Frank D. Lucas of Oklahoma be 
     and is hereby elected to the following standing committees of 
     the House of Representatives:
       The Committee on Agriculture;
       The Committee on Government Operations.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
