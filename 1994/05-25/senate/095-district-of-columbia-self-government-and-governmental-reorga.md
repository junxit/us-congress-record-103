**Measures:** H.R. 1632

```

 DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA SELF-GOVERNMENT AND GOVERNMENTAL REORGANIZATION 
                       ACT AMENDMENT ACT OF 1994

  The bill (H.R. 1632) to amend title 11, District of Columbia Code, 
and Part C of title IV of the District of Columbia Self-Government and
```
