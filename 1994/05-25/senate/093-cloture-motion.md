# CLOTURE MOTION

```
[Congressional Record: May 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. If there is no objection, under the previous 
order, the clerk will report the cloture motion.


                             cloture motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close the debate on the nomination 
     of Sam W. Brown, Jr., for the rank of Ambassador during his 
     tenure of service as Head of the Delegation to the Conference 
     on Security and Cooperation in Europe.
           Claiborne Pell, Paul Wellstone, Dennis DeConcini, John 
             F. Kerry, Carl Levin, Joseph Lieberman, John Glenn, 
             Jeff Bingaman, Byron L. Dorgan, Kent Conrad, Frank R. 
             Lautenberg, Daniel K. Akaka, Charles S. Robb, Pat 
             Leahy, Tom Daschle, Harlan Mathews.
```
