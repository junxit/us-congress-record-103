LITTLE TRAVERSE BAY BANDS OF ODAWA INDIANS AND THE LITTLE RIVER BAND OF 
                           OTTAWA INDIANS ACT

                                 ______


                       INOUYE AMENDMENT NO. 1745

  Mr. MITCHELL (for Mr. Inouye) proposed an amendment to the bill (S. 
1357) to reaffirm and clarify the Federal relationships of the Little 
Traverse Bay Bands of Odawa Indians and the Little River Band of Ottawa 
Indians as distinct federally recognized Indian tribes, and for other 
purposes; as follows:

       In section 2(5), strike ``(25 U.S.C. et seq.;'' and insert 
     ``(25 U.S.C. 461 et seq.;''.
