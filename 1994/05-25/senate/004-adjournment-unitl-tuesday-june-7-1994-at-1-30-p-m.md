# ADJOURNMENT UNITL TUESDAY, JUNE 7, 1994, AT 1:30 P.M.

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** S. 70

```
[Congressional Record: May 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
         ADJOURNMENT UNITL TUESDAY, JUNE 7, 1994, AT 1:30 P.M.

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, I now move that the Senate stand adjourned 
until 1:30 p.m. on Tuesday, June 7, as provided for under the 
provisions of Senate Concurrent Resolution 70.
  The motion was agreed to, and, the Senate, at 8:31 p.m. adjourned 
until Tuesday, June 7, 1994, at 1:30 p.m.
```
