# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: May 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  O God our help in ages past, our hope for years to come, prompt us to 
offer gratefulness and praise for the gifts of this day. May the rich 
association between friends and colleagues enlighten our tasks; may the 
awareness of splendor and beauty in the world increase our joy; may our 
perception of new ideas for difficult problems encourage and inspire 
us, and may Your message of reconciliation and compassion between 
peoples lead us in the paths of peace. In Your name, we pray. Amen.
```
