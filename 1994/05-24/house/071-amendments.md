# AMENDMENTS

**Speaking:** Hefley, Joel (H000444) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 4454

```
[Congressional Record: May 24, 1994]

                               AMENDMENTS

  Under clause 6 of rule XXIII, proposed amendments were submitted as 
follows:

                               H.R. 4454

           By Mr. HEFLEY:
     --Page 30, after line 2, insert the following new section:
       Sec. 307. Each amount appropriated or otherwise made

     reduced by 3.2 percent.
```
