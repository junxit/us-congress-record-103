**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Pell, Claiborne (P000193) [D-RI]

```
[Congressional Record: May 24, 1994]

                    MEASURES INDEFINITELY POSTPONED

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Calendar 
Nos. 112, 325, 331, 365 and 366 be indefinitely postponed.

  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. PELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
```
