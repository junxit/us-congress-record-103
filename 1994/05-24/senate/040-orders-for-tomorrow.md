# ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: May 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 9 a.m. on 
Wednesday, May 25; that following the prayer, the Journal of 
proceedings be deemed approved to date, and the time for the two 
leaders reserved for their use later in the day; that immediately 
thereafter, the Senate proceed in executive session to resume 
consideration of the motion to invoke cloture on the nomination of Sam 
W. Brown, Jr. as provided for under the provisions and limitations of a 
previous unanimous consent agreement.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
