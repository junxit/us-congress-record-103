# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Oberstar, James L. (O000006) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3838

```
[Congressional Record: July 27, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. JAMES L. OBERSTAR

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 27, 1994

  Mr. OBERSTAR. Mr. Speaker, last Friday the House of Representatives 
considered the bill H.R. 3838, housing and community development 
authorization for fiscal year 1995 and fiscal year 1996, and my vote 
was not recorded on the Kim amendment as amended and on final passage.
  During the consideration of this important legislation, I was 
attending several meetings in Minnesota to announce the resumption of 
work at National Steel Pellet Co. in my congressional district.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``nay'' on the Kim amendment 
as amended, rollcall vote 348, and I would have voted ``aye'' on final 
passage for H.R. 3838, rollcall vote 349.
```
