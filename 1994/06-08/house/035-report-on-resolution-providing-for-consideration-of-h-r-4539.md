   TREASURY, POSTAL SERVICE, EXECUTIVE OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT, AND

Treasury Department, the United States Postal Service, the Executive 
Office of the President, and certain Independent Agencies, for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 1995, and for other purposes, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
