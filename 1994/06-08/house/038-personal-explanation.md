# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Barlow, Tom (B000151) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4301

```
[Congressional Record: June 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. BARLOW. Mr. Speaker, I was absent on Friday, may 20, 1994, and 
was not present for rollcall votes 188, 189, and 190. But had I been in 
Washington and not my congressional district, I would have voted to 
approve the Dicks amendment to H.R. 4301, I would have voted against 
the Penny amendment to H.R. 4301, and against the Kennedy amendment to 
H.R. 4301. On rollcall vote No. 188, ``yea''; On rollcall vote No. 189, 
``nay''; and on rollcall vote No. 190, ``nay''.

                          ____________________
```
