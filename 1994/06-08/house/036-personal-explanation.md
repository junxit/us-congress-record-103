# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Franks, Gary A. (F000348) [R-CT]

```
[Congressional Record: June 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. FRANKS of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, on May 26, 1994, I missed 
four rollcall votes. It was due, Mr. Speaker, to my early departure to 
my district to witness the birth of my son, Gary Alvin Franks, Jr. 
Gary, Jr., his mother, Dona, and his two sisters, Azia and Jessica, are 
all doing very well.
  Mr. Speaker, had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' for 
rollcall votes 213, 214, 215, and nay for rollcall vote 216.
```
