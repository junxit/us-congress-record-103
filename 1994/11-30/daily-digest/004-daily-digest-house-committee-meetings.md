# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
HEALTH CARE REFORM
Committee on Education and Labor: Subcommittee on Labor-Management 
Relations and the Subcommittee on Regulation, Business Opportunities, 
and Technology of the Committee on Small Business held a joint hearing 
on health care reform. Testimony was heard from Len Fishman, 
Commissioner of Health, State of New Jersey; Mary Jo O'Brien, 
Commissioner of Health, State of Minnesota; John Kitzhaber, M.D., 
Governor-elect, State of Oregon; and public witnesses.
```
