# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY]

```
[Congressional Record: November 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. MOYNIHAN. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand 
in recess under the previous order.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:35 p.m., recessed until 
Thursday, December 1, 1994, at 9 a.m.
```
