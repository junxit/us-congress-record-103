# CEREMONY OF ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE TO JAMES M. INHOFE AS SENATOR FROM THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA

```
[Congressional Record: November 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  CEREMONY OF ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE TO JAMES M. INHOFE AS 
                   SENATOR FROM THE STATE OF OKLAHOMA

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. If the Senator-elect will now present 
himself at the desk, the Chair will administer the oath of office as 
required by the Constitution of the United States and prescribed by 
law.
  Mr. Inhofe, escorted by Mr. Nickles, advanced to the desk of the 
President pro tempore; the oath prescribed by law was administered to 
him by the President pro tempore; and he subscribed to the oath in the 
Official Oath Book.
  [Applause, Senators rising.]
```
