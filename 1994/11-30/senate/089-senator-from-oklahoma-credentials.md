# SENATOR FROM OKLAHOMA--CREDENTIALS

```
[Congressional Record: November 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   SENATOR FROM OKLAHOMA--CREDENTIALS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Chair lays before the Senate a 
Certificate of Election for Unexpired Term, of the Senator-elect James 
M. Inhofe, of the State of Oklahoma, caused by the resignation of 
Senator Boren.
  Without objection, it will be placed on file, and the Certificate of 
Election will be deemed to have been read.
  The Certificate of Election for Unexpired Term is as follows:

               Certificate of Election for Unexpired Term

     To the President of the Senate of the United States
       This is to certify that on the 8th day of November, 1994, 
     James M. Inhofe was duly chosen by the qualified electors of 
     the State of Oklahoma as Senator for the unexpired term 
     ending at noon on the 3rd day of January, 1997, to fill the 
     vacancy in the representation from said State in the Senate 
     of the United States caused by the resignation of Senator 
     David L. Boren.
       Witness: His excellency our Governor David Walters and our 
     seal hereto affixed at Oklahoma City, Oklahoma this 15th day 
     of November in the year of our Lord 1994.

                          ____________________
```
