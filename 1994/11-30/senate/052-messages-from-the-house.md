# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Res. 587, H.R. 5292

```
[Congressional Record: November 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:06 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 5292. An act to amend the Defense Base Closure and 
     Realignment Act of 1990 to extend the deadline for the 
     submission of nominations for the Defense Base Closure and 
     Realignment Commission.

  The message also announced that the House has agreed to the following 
resolution, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H. Res. 587. Resolution expressing profound sorrow upon the 
     death of the Honorable Dean A. Gallo, a Representative from 
     the State of New Jersey.
```
