# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S. 531, S. 613

```
[Congressional Record: November 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 531

  At the request of Mr. Craig, his name was added as a cosponsor of S. 
531, a bill to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to increase the 
estate and gift tax exemption from $600,000 to $1,000,000.


                                 S. 613

  At the request of Mr. Wellstone, his name was added as a cosponsor of 
S. 613, a bill to prohibit the importation of goods produced abroad 
with child labor, and for other purposes.
```
