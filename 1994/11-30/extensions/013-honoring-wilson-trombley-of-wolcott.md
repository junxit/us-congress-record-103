# HONORING WILSON TROMBLEY OF WOLCOTT

**Speaking:** Franks, Gary A. (F000348) [R-CT]

```
[Congressional Record: November 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                  HONORING WILSON TROMBLEY OF WOLCOTT

                                 ______


                          HON. GARY A. FRANKS

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 29, 1994

  Mr. FRANKS of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, it is a great pleasure to 
recognize and congratulate Wilson Trombley of Wolcott, for his many 
years of public service to the town of Wolcott. For the past 25 years 
Mr. Trombley has played an invaluable role as a dedicated, productive 
member of the Wolcott Republican Party. In each capacity that he has 
served, he has approached each venture with fervor and anticipation. 
Mr. Trombley has served as chairman of the Wolcott Town Council and the 
Wolcot Republican Town Committee. As the current town attorney, Mr. 
Trombley continues to prove that perseverance and dedication manifest 
fruitful, effective results.
  Many people in the town of Wolcott and the surrounding area as well 
have benefitted greatly from Wilson Trombley's sage advice and keen 
wisdom. I salute Mr. Trombley's dedication to the public good.
```
