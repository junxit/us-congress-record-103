# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Stupak, Bart (S001045) [D-MI]

```
[Congressional Record: July 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. STUPAK. Mr. Speaker, yesterday I was inadvertently recorded as 
voting ``nay'' on the LaRocco-Lewis amendment, roll call No. 316, the 
California Desert Protection Act. I should have been recorded as voting 
``yea.'' The LaRocco-Lewis amendment is a commonsense amendment that 
would allow hunters and sportsmen to continue to hunt, trap, and fish 
in the east Mojave scenic area. I strongly support this amendment.
```
