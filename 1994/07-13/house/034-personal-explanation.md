# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Thurman, Karen L. (T000253) [D-FL]

```
[Congressional Record: July 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mrs. THURMAN. Mr. Speaker, this morning I was having a meeting in my 
office during the vote on the Journal, No. 318. The bell system in my 
office was not operating properly. When I realized that a vote was 
occurring, I hurried to the Capitol but arrived just after the vote 
closed. I want the Record to reflect that I would have voted ``aye.''
```
