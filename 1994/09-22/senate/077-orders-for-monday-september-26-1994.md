# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 1994

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: September 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                 ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 1994

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 3 p.m. on 
Monday, September 26; that following the prayer the Journal of 
proceedings be deemed approved to date, and the time for the two 
leaders reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
