# CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF S. 1587

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 291, S. 1587

```
[Congressional Record: September 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF S. 1587

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of House Concurrent Resolution 
291, a concurrent resolution to correct the enrollment of the 
conference report accompanying S. 1587, the Federal Acquisition 
Streamlining Act just received from the House; that the concurrent 
resolution be agreed to, and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 291) was agreed to.
```
