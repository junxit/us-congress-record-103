# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Coppersmith, Sam (C000767) [D-AZ]

```
[Congressional Record: September 22, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                          HON. SAM COPPERSMITH

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 22, 1994

  Mr. COPPERSMITH. Mr. Speaker, due to my unavoidable absence on 
Monday, September 12, and Tuesday, September 13, I was unable to record 
my vote during even rollcalls, Nos. 417 through 423, inclusive. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``aye'' on rollcall Nos. 417, 420, 
421, 422, and 423, and I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall Nos. 418 
and 419.
```
