# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, AUGUST 22, 1994

**Speaking:** Sarbanes, Paul S. (S000064) [D-MD]

**Measures:** S. 2351

```
[Congressional Record: August 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, AUGUST 22, 1994

  Mr. SARBANES. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that on Monday, following the prayer, the Journal of 
proceedings be deemed approved to date and the time for the two leaders 
reserved for their use later in the day; that immediately thereafter, 
the Senate resume consideration of S. 2351, the Health Security Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
