```

             H O U S E  O F  R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S


Vol. 140


WASHINGTON, FRIDAY, AUGUST 19, 1994

No. 118


House of Representatives

  The House met at 10 a.m.
  The Chaplain, James David Ford, D.D., offered the following prayer:
  With all about that needs to be done and all the tasks that cry for 
attention and all the petitions that rise from our hearts, above all 
this, O gracious God, we pause for this moment of gratitude and praise. 
You have created us, You have redeemed us and show us the way, You have 
comforted us by Your spirit. This day we ask for nothing and give 
thanks for everything. Almighty God, for all Your gifts of life and 
love, we offer this prayer of thanksgiving. Amen.
```
