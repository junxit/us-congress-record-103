# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
[Congressional Record: June 13, 1994]

                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentlewoman from Washington [Ms. 
Dunn] please come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of 
Allegiance?

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the <gr-thn-eq>United

     one nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice
```
