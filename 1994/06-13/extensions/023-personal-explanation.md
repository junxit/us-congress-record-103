# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Gejdenson, Sam (G000120) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 518

```
[Congressional Record: June 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                           HON. SAM GEJDENSON

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 13, 1994

  Mr. GEJDENSON. Mr. Speaker, on Friday, June 10, I was absent from the 
House to sponsor a trade conference in my district with Secretary of 
Commerce Ronald Brown, Export-Import Bank Chairman Kenneth Brody, and 
Trade and Development Agency Director Joseph Grandmaison. As a result, 
I missed three rollcall votes on amendments to H.R. 518, the California 
Desert Protection Act. Had I been present, I would have voted as 
follows: rollcall No. 229--Aye, rollcall No. 230--Aye, rollcall No. 
231--No.
```
