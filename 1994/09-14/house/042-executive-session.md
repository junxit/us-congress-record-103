                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                       


                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 1113, 1114, 1115, 
1116, 1119, 1120, 1142, 1144, 1146, 1162, 1163, 1164, 1165, 1166, 1167, 
1171, 1172, 1173, 1174, 1175, 1176, and 1177; I further ask unanimous 
consent that the nominees be confirmed en bloc and that any statements 
appear in the Record as if read; that upon confirmation, the motions to 
reconsider be laid upon the table en bloc, that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action, and that the Senate return 
to legislative session.
