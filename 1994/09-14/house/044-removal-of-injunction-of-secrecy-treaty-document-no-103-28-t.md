# REMOVAL OF INJUNCTION OF SECRECY--TREATY DOCUMENT NO. 103-28; TREATY DOCUMENT NO. 103-29; TREATY DOCUMENT 103-30

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: September 14, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 REMOVAL OF INJUNCTION OF SECRECY--TREATY DOCUMENT NO. 103-28; TREATY 
              DOCUMENT NO. 103-29; TREATY DOCUMENT 103-30

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, as if in executive session, I ask 
unanimous consent that the injunction of secrecy be removed from the 
following three treaties transmitted to the Senate today by the 
President of the United States:
  Protocol Amending the Convention Between the United States of America 
and Canada with Respect to Taxes on Income and on Capital (Treaty 
Document No. 103-28);
  Convention Between the Government of the United States of America and 
the Government of Sweden for the Avoidance of Double Taxation and the 
Prevention of Fiscal Evasion with Respect to Taxes on Income (Treaty 
Document No. 103-29);
  Convention Between the Government of the United States of America and 
the Government of Ukraine for the Avoidance of Double Taxation and the 
Prevention of Fiscal Evasion with Respect to Taxes on Income and 
Capital, with Protocol (Treaty Document No. 103-30);
  I further ask that the treaties be considered as having been read the 
first time; that they be referred, with accompanying papers, to the 
Committee on Foreign Relations and ordered to be printed; and, that the 
President's messages be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  (The messages of the President will printed in Monday's Record.)
```
