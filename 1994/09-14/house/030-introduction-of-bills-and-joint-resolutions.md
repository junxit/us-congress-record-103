# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Thurmond, Strom (T000254) [R-SC], Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

**Measures:** S. 2436, S. 2437

```
[Congressional Record: September 14, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. THURMOND:
       S. 2436. A bill to amend title 1 of the United States Code 
     to clarify the effect and application of legislation; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. CONRAD (for himself and Mr. Daschle):
       S. 2437. A bill to amend the Food Security Act of 1985 to 
     extend, improve, increase flexibility, and increase 
     conservation benefits of the conservation reserve program, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Agriculture, 
     Nutrition, and Forestry.
```
