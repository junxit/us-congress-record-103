# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
[Congressional Record: May 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from Florida, I 
move that the Senate be in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  The motion was agreed to, and the Senate, at 1:19 p.m., recessed 
subject to the call of the Chair.
  Whereupon, the Senate reassembled, at 2:30 p.m., when called to order 
by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Reid).
```
