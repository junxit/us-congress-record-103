# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Fingerhut, Eric D. (F000128) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 3191

```
[Congressional Record: May 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                          HON. ERIC FINGERHUT

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Wednesday, May 4, 1994

  Mr. FINGERHUT. Mr. Speaker, I was not present during the House 
session on Tuesday, May 3. Had I been present, I would have voted 
``yes'' on H.R. 3191, the national flood insurance reauthorization. I 
commend my colleagues on the Banking Committee for their excellent work 
in crafting this comprehensive piece of legislation.
```
