# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** DeLauro, Rosa L. (D000216) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 2333, H.R. 3191, H.R. 3221, H.R. 4013

```
[Congressional Record: May 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. DeLAURO. Mr. Speaker, I was absent on Thursday April 28 and 
Tuesday, May 3, and missed rollcall votes 146, 147, 148, and 149. But 
had I been here, I would have voted in support of H.R. 3221, the Iraqi 
Claims Act; I would have voted against the motion to recommit H.R. 
2333, State Department Authorization; I would have voted in support of 
H.R. 4013, to exempt the Veterans Administration Work Force from 
proposed reductions for 5 years; and I would have voted in support of 
H.R. 3191, the National Flood Insurance Reform Act.
```
