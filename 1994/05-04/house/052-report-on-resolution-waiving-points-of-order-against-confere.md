# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT TO ACCOMPANY S. 636, FREEDOM OF ACCESS TO CLINIC ENTRANCES ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.Res. 417, S. 636

```
[Congressional Record: May 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
 TO ACCOMPANY S. 636, FREEDOM OF ACCESS TO CLINIC ENTRANCES ACT OF 1994

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-493) on the resolution (H. Res. 417) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill (S. 
636) to amend the Public Health Service Act to permit individuals to 
have freedom of access to certain medical clinics and facilities, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
