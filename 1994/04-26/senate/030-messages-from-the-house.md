# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 1936

```
[Congressional Record: April 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 5:09 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by one of its reading clerks, announced that the House agrees to the 
amendment of the Senate to the amendment of the House to the bill (S. 
1936) to authorize appropriations for the Marine Mammal Protection Act 
of 1972 and to improve the program to reduce the incidental taking of 
marine mammals during the course of commercial fishing operations, and 
for other purposes; with an amendment.
```
