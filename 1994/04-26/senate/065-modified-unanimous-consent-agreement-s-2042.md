# MODIFIED UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 2042

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** S. 2042

```
[Congressional Record: April 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
             MODIFIED UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 2042

  Mr. MITCHELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
previous unanimous consent governing consideration of S. 2042, relating 
to the arms embargo on Bosnia, be modified to reflect the following 
change: That the Senate proceed to the consideration of S. 2042 no 
later than the close of business on Thursday, May 5; with all other 
provisions of the previous agreement remaining in effect.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
