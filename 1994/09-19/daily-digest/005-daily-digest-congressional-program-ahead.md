                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                 Week of September 20 through 24, 1994

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will resume consideration of the nomination of 
Admiral Henry H. Mauz, Jr., U.S. Navy, to be placed on the retired
