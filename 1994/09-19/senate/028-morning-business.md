# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: September 19, 1994]

                            MORNING BUSINESS

                                 ______


           MESSAGES FROM THE PRESIDENT RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 5, 1993, 
the Secretary of the Senate on September 15, 1994, received a message 
from the President of the United States submitting sundry nominations 
and a treaty, which were referred to the appropriate committees.
  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 5, 1993, 
the Secretary of the Senate on September 16, 1994, received a message 
from the President of the United States submitting sundry nominations, 
which were referred to the appropriate committees.
  The nominations received on September 15 and 16, 1994, are shown in 
today's Record at the end of the Senate proceedings.
```
