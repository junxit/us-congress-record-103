# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: September 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 3 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for not to exceed 10 minutes each.
  The Senator from North Dakota [Mr. Dorgan] is recognized to speak up 
to 15 minutes.
```
