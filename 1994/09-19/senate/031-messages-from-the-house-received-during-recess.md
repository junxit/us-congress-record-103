**Measures:** H.R. 3841

```
[Congressional Record: September 19, 1994]

             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of January 5, 1993, the Senate on 
September 16, 1994, during the recess of the Senate received a message 
from the House of Representatives announcing the Speaker has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3841. An act to amend the Bank Holding Company Act of 
     1956, the Revised Statutes of the United States, and the 
     Federal Deposit Insurance Act to provide for interstate
```
