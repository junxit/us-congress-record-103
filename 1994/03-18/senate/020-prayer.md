# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: March 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We specially pray this day, O gracious God, for those who serve in 
Government and to whom great responsibility has been given. We admit 
that we see the motions of history in our own way and we differ on the 
solutions available. Yet, O God, we live as one people and are bound 
together for better or for worse and we share together the bounty and 
the gifts by which we all have been blessed. So as one people who live 
in one land we pray for all those in Government who serve the people of 
the Nation that they will be moved by Your good spirit to do the works 
of justice and of mercy, this day and everyday. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
