# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Mfume, Kweisi (M000687) [D-MD]

**Measures:** S. 636

```
[Congressional Record: March 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                           HON. KWEISI MFUME

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 18, 1994

  Mr. MFUME. Mr. Speaker, at the end of the evening yesterday, 
Thursday, March 17, 1994, I was unavoidably absent and therefore missed 
four rollcall votes on S. 636, the freedom of access of clinic 
entrances bill. Specifically, I missed rollcall vote number 69, on 
whether or not to commit the bill with instructions, on which I would 
have voted ``no''; rollcall vote number 70, final passage, on which I 
would have voted ``aye''; rollcall vote number 71, to insist on the 
House amendments, on which I would have voted ``yea''; and rollcall 
vote number 72, to instruct conferees, on which I would have voted 
``yea.''
```
