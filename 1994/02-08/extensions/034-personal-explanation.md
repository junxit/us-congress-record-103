                           HON. KWEISI MFUME

                              of maryland

                       Tuesday, February 8, 1994

  Mr. MFUME. Mr. Speaker, due to the weather I was unavoidably detained 
in my congressional district in Maryland today, Tuesday, February 8, 
1994, and missed rollcall vote No. 14, on approving the Journal of the 
previous day's proceedings.
