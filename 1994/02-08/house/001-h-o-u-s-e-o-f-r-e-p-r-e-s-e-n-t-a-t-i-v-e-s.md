             H O U S E  O F  R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S


Vol. 140


WASHINGTON, TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 8, 1994

No. 11


House of Representatives

  The House met at 2 p.m.
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Gracious God, from Whom comes every good gift, be with all people who 
seek to understand their lives, their hopes, and their faith. May the 
vision that You have given--of a world where the nations live in 
harmony, where peoples of all backgrounds understand and respect each 
other, where peace and trust permeate our relations--may this world be 
our vision and may we dedicate ourselves and our abilities to work to 
that end. In Your name, we pray. Amen.
