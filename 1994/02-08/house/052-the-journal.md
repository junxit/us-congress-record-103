                              THE JOURNAL

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 5 of rule I, the pending 
business is the question of approving the Speaker's approval of the 
Journal.

the noes appeared to have it.
  Mr. KOPETSKI. Mr. Speaker, I object to the vote on the ground that a 
quorum is not present, and make the point of order that a quorum is not 
present.

  The Sergeant at Arms will notify absent Members.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 235,
