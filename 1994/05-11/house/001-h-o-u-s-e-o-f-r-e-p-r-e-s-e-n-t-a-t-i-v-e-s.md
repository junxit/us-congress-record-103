             H O U S E  O F  R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S


Vol. 140


WASHINGTON, WEDNESDAY, MAY 11, 1994

No. 57


House of Representatives

  The House met at 2 p.m.
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  From the rising of the Sun until the going down of the same, Your 
word, O gracious God, is a beacon of hope, a light to dispel the 
darkness, and a signal to point to a better day. May we, each one, heed 
the voice that calls us to contrition and repentance and at the same 
time encourages and inspires us to deeds of justice and acts of mercy. 
May we use the time and the day appointed for us in which to live, so 
we are devoted in the tasks before us and be faithful with the gifts 
You have freely given. In Your name, we pray. Amen.
