# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Barlow, Tom (B000151) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 2333, H.R. 3221, H.R. 4013

```
[Congressional Record: May 11, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. BARLOW. Mr. Speaker, I was absent on Thursday, April 28 and 
missed roll call votes 146, 147, and 148. But had I been here, I would 
have voted in support of H.R. 3221, the Iraqi Claims Act; I would have 
voted against the motion to recommit H.R. 2333, State Department 
authorization; and I would have voted in support of H.R. 4013, to 
exempt the Veterans' Administration work force from proposed reductions 
for 5 years.
```
