# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
COMMERCE, JUSTICE, STATE AND JUDICIARY APPROPRIATIONS
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Commerce, Justice, State 
and Judiciary held a hearing on the Department of Justice (General 
Administration, Executive Office of Immigration Review, Inspector 
General, Foreign Claims Settlement Commission and Fees and Expenses of 
Witnesses). Testimony was heard from the following officials of the 
Department of Justice: Stephen R. Colgate, Assistant Attorney General, 
Administration; Richard J. Hankinson, Inspector General; and David E. 
Bradley, Chief Counsel, Foreign Claims Settlement Commission.
```
