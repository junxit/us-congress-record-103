# RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TUESDAY, APRIL 19, 1994

```
[Congressional Record: April 15, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
             RECESS UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TUESDAY, APRIL 19, 1994

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until the hour of 9:30 a.m. Tuesday, April 19, 
1994.
  Thereupon, at 8:55 o'clock and 13 seconds a.m., the Senate recessed, 
under the order of Thursday, April 14, 1994, until Tuesday, April 19, 
1994, at 9:30 a.m.
```
