# PERMISSION TO FILE SUPPLEMENTAL REPORT TO H.R. 3171, DEPARTMENT OF AGRICULTURE REORGANIZATION ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.R. 3171

```
[Congressional Record: September 21, 1994]



  PERMISSION TO FILE SUPPLEMENTAL REPORT TO H.R. 3171, DEPARTMENT OF 
                 AGRICULTURE REORGANIZATION ACT OF 1994

  Mr. de la GARZA. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Committee on Agriculture may be permitted to file a supplemental report 
to the bill (H.R. 3171) to authorize the Secretary of Agriculture to 
reorganize the Department of Agriculture, and for other purposes.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
