# DESIGNATION OF SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
[Congressional Record: September 21, 1994]



                   DESIGNATION OF SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

communication from the Speaker:

                                               Washington, DC,

                                               September 21, 1994.
       I hereby designate the Honorable G.V. (Sonny) Montgomery to 
     act as Speaker pro tempore on this day.
```
