# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

```
[Congressional Record: September 21, 1994]



                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed en bloc to the immediate consideration of calendar Nos. 601, 
602, 606; that the committee amendment, where appropriate, be agreed 
to, the bills be deemed read the third time, passed, and the motions to 
reconsider be laid upon the table en bloc; that the title amendment, 
where appropriate, be agreed to; further, that any statements relating 
to these calendar items appear at the appropriate place in the Record, 
and the consideration of these items appear individually in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
