# HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES CAMPAIGN SPENDING LIMIT AND ELECTION REFORM ACT OF 1993--MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

**Measures:** S. 3

```
[Congressional Record: September 21, 1994]



 HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES CAMPAIGN SPENDING LIMIT AND ELECTION REFORM 
                  ACT OF 1993--MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask the Chair to lay before the Senate a 
message from the House on S. 3, entitled the ``Congressional Spending 
Limit and Election Reform Act of 1993.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the House of Representatives:

       Resolved, That the bill from the Senate (S. 3) entitled 
     ``An Act entitled the `Congressional Spending Limit and 
     Election Reform Act of 1993,''' do pass with amendments.

  The Senate resumed consideration of the message from the House.

                          ____________________
```
