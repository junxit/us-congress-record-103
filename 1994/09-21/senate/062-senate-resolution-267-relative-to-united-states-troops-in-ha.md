# SENATE RESOLUTION 267--RELATIVE TO UNITED STATES TROOPS IN HAITI

**Measures:** S.Res. 267

```
[Congressional Record: September 21, 1994]



    SENATE RESOLUTION 267--RELATIVE TO UNITED STATES TROOPS IN HAITI

  Mr. BROWN submitted the following resolution; which was referred to 
the Committee on Foreign Relations:

                              S. Res. 267

       It is the sense of the Senate that U.S. troops should be 
     withdrawn from Haiti not later than December 31, 1994.
```
