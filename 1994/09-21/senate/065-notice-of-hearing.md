# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
[Congressional Record: September 21, 1994]



                           NOTICE OF HEARING


           committee on agriculture, nutrition, and forestry

  Mr. LEAHY. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry will hold a hearing 
on Thursday, September 29, 1994, at 10:30 a.m. in SR-332, to consider 
the nomination of Marsha P. Martin, of Texas, to be a member of the 
Farm Credit Administration. Senator Kent Conrad will preside.
  For further information, please contact Christine Sarcone of the 
committee staff at 224-2035.

                          ____________________
```
