# BILL REFERRED TO COMMITTEE--S. 1686

**Speaking:** Conrad, Kent (C000705) [D-ND]

**Measures:** S. 1686

```
[Congressional Record: September 21, 1994]



                  BILL REFERRED TO COMMITTEE--S. 1686

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Indian Affairs be discharged from further consideration of S. 1686, 
a bill to amend the Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act, and that the 
bill be referred to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
