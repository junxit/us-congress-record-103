# TOO LIBERAL FOR TOO LONG

**Speaking:** Linder, John (L000321) [R-GA]

```
[Congressional Record: October 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]


                              {time}  1220
 
                        TOO LIBERAL FOR TOO LONG

  (Mr. LINDER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. LINDER. Mr. Speaker, President Clinton seeks to change his colors 
just like the fall leaves. And he only does it during autumns with an 
election.
  This chameleon candidacy of the Democrat party has become a see-
through strategy to the American people.
  So in addition to trying to hide themselves, Democrats are trying to 
disguise and distort the Republican record. That is why President 
Clinton has begun attacking our contract with America.
  Our contract was signed, sealed, and delivered to the American 
people, not in backroom deals, but in broad daylight on the Capitol 
steps.
  It is what America wants from Washington. A positive statement that 
we will cut the spending, balance the budget, rebuild our defense, and 
secure this country.
  Without good ideas of his own, President Clinton has decided to 
attack the good ideas of others.
  President Clinton and his party have been too liberal for too long. 
They know it, the Nation knows it, and their only hope is that voters 
forget it in time for the election.
```
