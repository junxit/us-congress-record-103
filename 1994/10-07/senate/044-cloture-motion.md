# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: October 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk and 
ask that it be stated.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination of 
     Lt. General Buster C. Glosson, USAF, for appointment to the 
     grade of lieutenant general on the retired list pursuant to 
     the provisions to title 10, United States Code, section 1370:
         Trent Lott, Thad Cochran, Malcolm Wallop, Bob Smith, Kay 
           Bailey Hutchison, Lauch Faircloth, Orrin G. Hatch, Sam 
           Nunn, Dan Coats, Strom Thurmond, John McCain, Hank 
           Brown, Jesse Helms, Larry Craig, Paul Coverdell, Al 
           D'Amato.

                          ____________________
```
