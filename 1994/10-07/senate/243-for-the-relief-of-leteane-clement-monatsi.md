# FOR THE RELIEF OF LETEANE CLEMENT MONATSI

**Speaking:** Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2411

```
[Congressional Record: October 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
               FOR THE RELIEF OF LETEANE CLEMENT MONATSI

  Mr. FEINGOLD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar No. 526, H.R. 2411, 
a bill for the relief of the Leteane Clement Monatsi, that the bill be 
deemed read a third time and passed; that the motion to reconsider be 
laid upon the table, and that any statements relative to the passage of 
this item appear at the appropriate place in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 2411) was deemed read the third time, and passed.
```
