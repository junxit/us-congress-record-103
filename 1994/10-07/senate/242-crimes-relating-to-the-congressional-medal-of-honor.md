# CRIMES RELATING TO THE CONGRESSIONAL MEDAL OF HONOR

**Speaking:** Feingold, Russell D. (F000061) [D-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 5102

```
[Congressional Record: October 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
          CRIMES RELATING TO THE CONGRESSIONAL MEDAL OF HONOR

  Mr. FEINGOLD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 5102, a bill relating to 
certain crimes relating to the Congressional Medal of Honor just 
received from the House, that the bill be read three times, passed, and 
the motion to reconsider laid upon the table, and that any statements 
appear at the appropriate place in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 5102) was deemed read the third time, and passed.
```
