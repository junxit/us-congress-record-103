# CODIFICATION OF LAWS RELATED TO TRANSPORTATION

**Speaking:** Breaux, John B. (B000780) [D-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4778

```
[Congressional Record: October 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
             CODIFICATION OF LAWS RELATED TO TRANSPORTATION

  Mr. BREAUX. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 4778, relating to 
codification of laws related to transportation to improve the code, 
just received from the House; that the bill be read a third time, 
passed, the motion to reconsider be laid on the table; and that any 
statement appear at the appropriate place in the Record as if read.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 4778) was passed.
```
