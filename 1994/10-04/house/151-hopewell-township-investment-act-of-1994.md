# HOPEWELL TOWNSHIP INVESTMENT ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.R. 4704

```
[Congressional Record: October 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                HOPEWELL TOWNSHIP INVESTMENT ACT OF 1994

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question of 
suspending the rules and passing the bill, H.R. 4704, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Ohio [Mr. Traficant] that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, H.R. 4704, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
