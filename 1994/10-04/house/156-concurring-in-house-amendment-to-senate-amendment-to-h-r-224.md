# CONCURRING IN HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 2240, INDEPENDENT SAFETY BOARD ACT AMENDMENTS OF 1994

**Measures:** H.Res. 558, H.R. 2240

```
[Congressional Record: October 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
    CONCURRING IN HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 2240, 
            INDEPENDENT SAFETY BOARD ACT AMENDMENTS OF 1994

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question of 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution, House Resolution 
558.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Minnesota [Mr. Oberstar] that the House suspend the 
rules and agree to the resolution, House Resolution 558.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds having voted in favor 
thereof) the rules were suspended and the resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
