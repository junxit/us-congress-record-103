# NAME REMOVED AS A COSPONSOR

**Speaking:** Brown, Hank (B000919) [R-CO], Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT], Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** S. 1770

```
[Congressional Record: October 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                      NAME REMOVED AS A COSPONSOR

  Mr. BROWN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent my name be 
removed as a cosponsor from S. 1770.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. LEAHY. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LEAHY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. MITCHELL addressed the Chair.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. The majority leader.
  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask that the Senate be in order and 
that all Senators take their seats.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. The Senate will be in order. Senators will please 
take their seats.
```
