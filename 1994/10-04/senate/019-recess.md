# RECESS

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: October 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 RECESS

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, and Members of the Senate, the camera is 
located in this corner of the gallery. I ask that all Senators turn 
their chairs toward the camera, and then there will be a total of 10 
flashes.
  So I ask Senators to remain until the photographer has indicated that 
they have completed their work.
  Mr. President, I now ask that the Senate stand in recess subject to 
the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 2:34 p.m. recessed until 
2:40 p.m.; whereupon, the Senate at 2:40 p.m. reassembled when called 
to order by the Presiding Officer (Mrs. Murray).
```
