# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
[Congressional Record: October 4, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will now 
proceed to the consideration of the following nominations en bloc:
  Ricki Tigert, to be chairperson of the FDIC, Executive Calendar No. 
693; Andrew Hove, to be a member of the FDIC, Executive Calendar No. 
694; Andrew Hove, to be vice chairperson of the FDIC, Calendar No. 695.
  Under the previous order, the nominations shall be considered as 
having been confirmed en bloc; that the motions to reconsider be laid 
on the table en bloc; and that the President be notified of the 
Senate's action.
  So the nominations were considered and confirmed en bloc.
```
