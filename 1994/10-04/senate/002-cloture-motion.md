                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the

     United States Circuit Judge for the Third Circuit:

           D.K. Inouye, Kent Conrad, Carl Levin, John F. Kerry, 
           Pat Leahy, J. Lieberman, Bill Bradley, Ben Nighthorse 
           Campbell, Paul Simon, John Glenn, Harry Reid, Charles
