# PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

```
[Congressional Record: August 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentlewoman from Colorado [Mrs. 
Schroeder] please come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of 
Allegiance.
  Mrs. SCHROEDER led the Pledge of Allegiance as follows:

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the <gr-thn-eq>United 
     States of America, and to the Republic for which it stands, 
     one nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice
```
