# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: August 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We are grateful, O God, that You nourish us with Your good spirit all 
our days. From our own beginnings as infants to our last breath You 
have promised to bless us and provide Your nurture and grace so we will 
be Your people and belong to You. We give our thanks for faithful 
parents and caring friends who support us through our pilgrimage on 
Earth and who are witnesses to Your abiding presence and love. In Your 
name, we pray. Amen.
```
