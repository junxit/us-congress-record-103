# H.R. 3433, A BILL TO PROVIDE FOR THE MANAGEMENT OF PORTIONS OF THE PRESIDIO UNDER THE JURISDICTION OF THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

**Speaking:** Gibbons, Sam (G000153) [D-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3433

```
[Congressional Record: August 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  H.R. 3433, A BILL TO PROVIDE FOR THE MANAGEMENT OF PORTIONS OF THE 
   PRESIDIO UNDER THE JURISDICTION OF THE DEPARTMENT OF THE INTERIOR

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Under a previous order of the House, the 
gentleman from Florida [Mr. Gibbons] is recognized for 5 minutes.
  Mr. GIBBONS. Madam Speaker, pursuant to the rules of the Democratic 
Caucus, I wish to serve notice to my colleagues that I have been 
instructed by the Committee on Ways and Means to seek less than an open 
rule for the consideration by the House of Representatives of the bill, 
H.R. 3433, a bill to provide for the management of portions of the 
Presidio under the jurisdiction of the Department of the Interior, as 
amended by the committee.
```
