# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** H.R. 1804

```
[Congressional Record: March 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I send a second cloture motion to the 
desk and I ask it be stated by the clerk.
  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. The cloture motion having been presented 
under rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:


                             cloture motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close the debate on the conference 
     report accompanying H.R. 1804, Goals 2000: Educate America.
         Byron L. Dorgan, Paul Wellstone, Edward M. Kennedy, 
           Harlan Mathews, Jeff Bingaman, Barbara Boxer, Pat 
           Leahy, John F. Kerry, B. Mikulski, John Glenn, Frank R. 
           Lautenberg, Bill Bradley, J. Lieberman, D. Pryor, Patty 
           Murray, Harris Wofford.
```
