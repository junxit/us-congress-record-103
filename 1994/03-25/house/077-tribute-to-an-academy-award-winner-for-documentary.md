# TRIBUTE TO AN ACADEMY AWARD WINNER FOR DOCUMENTARY

**Speaking:** Pell, Claiborne (P000193) [D-RI]

```
[Congressional Record: March 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
           TRIBUTE TO AN ACADEMY AWARD WINNER FOR DOCUMENTARY

  Mr. PELL. Mr. President, I salute today an exceptional filmmaker and 
Rhode Island native, Alice M. Maurice, who has been recognized by the 
Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences as associate producer of 
1994's best short subject documentary.
  Ms. Maurice's commitment to excellence in filmmaking has not only 
brought her and the State of Rhode Island honor, but has made a 
positive difference in the lives of women throughout the United States. 
Her documentary film, ``Defending Our Lives,'' details the suffering of 
eight abused women who killed their assailants in self defense. Ms. 
Maurice's film draws attention to an important subject which deserves 
such national recognition.
  I ask that my distinguished colleagues join me in saluting this 
remarkable woman, a truly accomplished artist and filmmaker.
```
