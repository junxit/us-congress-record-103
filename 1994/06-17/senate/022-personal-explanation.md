# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Houghton, Amo (H000814) [R-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 4554

```
[Congressional Record: June 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. HOUGHTON. Mr. Speaker, today I missed rollcall vote No. 255, the 
motion to rise and report on H.R. 4554, the Agriculture appropriations 
for fiscal year 1995.
  Due to a miscommunication between the floor and the Ways and Means 
Committee leadership, I missed that vote along with 37 other members of 
the Ways and Means Committee.
  Had I been able, I would have voted ``no'' on the motion to rise, to 
allow Mr. Rohrabacher to debate his amendment to the Agriculture 
appropriations bill.
```
