```

                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Will the gentleman from New York [Mr. King] 
please come forward and lead the House in the Pledge of Allegiance?

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the <gr-thn-eq>United
```
