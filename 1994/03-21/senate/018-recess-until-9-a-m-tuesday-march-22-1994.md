# RECESS UNTIL 9 A.M., TUESDAY, MARCH 22, 1994

**Speaking:** Bumpers, Dale (B001057) [D-AR]

```
[Congressional Record: March 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
              RECESS UNTIL 9 A.M., TUESDAY, MARCH 22, 1994

  Mr. BUMPERS. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, and if no other Senator is seeking 
recognition, I now ask unanimous consent the Senate stand in recess as 
previously ordered.

Tuesday, March 22, 1994, at 9 a.m.
```
