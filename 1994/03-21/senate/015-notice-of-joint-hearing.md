# NOTICE OF JOINT HEARING

**Speaking:** Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI]

```
[Congressional Record: March 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        NOTICE OF JOINT HEARING


 committee on indian affairs and committee on labor and human resources

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs and the Senate Committee on Labor and Human 
Resources will be holding a joint hearing on Friday, March 25, 1994, 
beginning at 10 a.m., in 485 Russell Senate Office Building on the 
Headstart Programs serving native Americans.
  Those wishing additional information should contact the Committee on 
Indian Affairs at 224-2251.
```
