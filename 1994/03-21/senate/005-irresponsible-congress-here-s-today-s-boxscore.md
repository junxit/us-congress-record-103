# IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE'S TODAY'S BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: March 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
            IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE'S TODAY'S BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, as of the close of business on Friday, 
March 18, the Federal debt stood at $4,554,110,591,484.90, meaning that 
on a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$17,468.04 as his or her share of that debt.
```
