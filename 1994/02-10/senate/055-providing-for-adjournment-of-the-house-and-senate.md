# PROVIDING FOR ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE AND SENATE

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 206

```
[Congressional Record: February 10, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
           PROVIDING FOR ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE AND SENATE

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of House Concurrent Resolution 
206, the adjournment resolution, just received from the House; that the 
concurrent resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 206) was agreed to.
```
