# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

```
[Congressional Record: September 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                            HON. ROB PORTMAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 13, 1994

  Mr. PORTMAN. Mr. Speaker, because of a schedule conflict, I was 
unable to arrive in Washington until today. As a result, I missed three 
votes yesterday. Had I been in attendance, I would have voted ``no'' on 
each of rollcall votes No. 417 and No. 418 and ``yes'' on rollcall vote 
No. 419.
```
