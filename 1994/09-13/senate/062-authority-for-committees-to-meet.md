# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Roth, William V., Jr. (R000460) [R-DE]

```
[Congressional Record: September 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                     COMMITTEE ON VETERANS' AFFAIRS

  Mr. ROTH. Mr. President, the Committee on Veteran's Affairs would 
like to request unanimous consent to hold a hearing on the nomination 
of Kenneth W. Kizer to be Under Secretary for health of the Department 
of Veterans Affairs. The hearing will be held in room 418 of the 
Russell Senate Office Building at 2:30 p.m. on Tuesday, September 13, 
1994.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
