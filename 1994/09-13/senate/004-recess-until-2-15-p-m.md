# RECESS UNTIL 2:15 P.M.

**Speaking:** Riegle, Donald W., Jr. (R000249) [D-MI]

```
[Congressional Record: September 13, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                         RECESS UNTIL 2:15 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12:30 
p.m. having arrived, the Senate will now stand in recess until the hour 
of 2:15 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:31 p.m., the Senate recessed until 2:14 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Kohl).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. In my capacity as a Senator from the State of 
Wisconsin, I suggest the absence of a quorum. The clerk will call the 
roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. RIEGLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered. The Chair 
recognizes the Senator from Michigan [Mr. Riegle].

                          ____________________
```
