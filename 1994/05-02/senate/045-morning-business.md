# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Craig, Larry E. (C000858) [R-ID]

```
[Congressional Record: May 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order there will 
now be a period for the transaction of morning business, with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for not to exceed 5 minutes each.
  The Chair, in her capacity as a Senator from the State of California, 
notes the absence of a quorum.
  The clerk will please call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. CRAIG. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. CRAIG. I ask I may be permitted to speak in morning business.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered. The Senator is recognized to speak for up to 5 minutes.
```
