# APPOINTMENT BY THE MAJORITY LEADER

**Speaking:** Bryan, Richard H. (B000993) [D-NV]

```
[Congressional Record: May 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   APPOINTMENT BY THE MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Will the Senator from Nevada permit the Chair 
to make an announcement?
  Mr. BRYAN. The Senator from Nevada will yield to the Chair.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the majority leader, 
pursuant to Public Law 103-227, appoints the Senator from New Mexico 
[Mr. Bingaman] as a member of the National Education Goals panel.
  The Senator from Nevada is recognized.
```
