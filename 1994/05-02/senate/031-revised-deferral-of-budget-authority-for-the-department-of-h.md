# REVISED DEFERRAL OF BUDGET AUTHORITY FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 105

```
[Congressional Record: May 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 REVISED DEFERRAL OF BUDGET AUTHORITY FOR THE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH AND 
           HUMAN SERVICES--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 105

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred jointly pursuant to the order of January 30, 
1975, as modified by the order of April 11, 1986, to the Committee 
Appropriations, the Committee on the Budget, and the Committee on 
Finance.

To the Congress of the Untied States:
  In accordance with the Congressional Budget and Impoundment Control 
Act of 1974, I herewith report one revised deferral of budget 
authority, totaling $7.3 million.
  The deferral affects the Department of Health and Human Services. The 
details of the revised deferral is contained in the attached report.
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, May 2, 1994.
```
