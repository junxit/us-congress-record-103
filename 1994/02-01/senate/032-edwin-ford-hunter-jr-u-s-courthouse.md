# EDWIN FORD HUNTER, JR., U.S. COURTHOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3356

```

                EDWIN FORD HUNTER, JR., U.S. COURTHOUSE

  A bill (H.R. 3356) to designate the U.S. Courthouse under 
construction at 611 Broad Street, in Lake Charles, LA, as the ``Edwin 
Ford Hunter, Jr., U.S. Courthouse,'' was considered, order to a third
```
