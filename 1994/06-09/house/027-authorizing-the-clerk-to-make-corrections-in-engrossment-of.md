# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 4301, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1995

**Speaking:** Dellums, Ronald V. (D000222) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4301

```
[Congressional Record: June 9, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 4301, 
        NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 1995

  Mr. DELLUMS. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 4301, the Clerk be authorized to make 
corrections in section numbers, punctuation, and cross references, and 
to make such other technical, clerical, and conforming changes as may 
be necessary to reflect the actions of the House in amending the bill, 
H.R. 4301.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Darden). In there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
