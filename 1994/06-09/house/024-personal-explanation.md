# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Lehman, Richard H. (L000225) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4301

```
[Congressional Record: June 9, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. LEHMAN. Mr. Speaker, today the House of Representatives approved 
H.R. 4301, the Defense authorization for fiscal year 1995.
  Due to an important meeting I had with the U.S. Ambassador to 
Armenia, Harry Gilmore, and the U.S. Ambassador to Azerbaijan, Rich 
Kauzlarich, I was unavoidably detained during the vote on final passage 
for H.R. 4301.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``aye.''
```
