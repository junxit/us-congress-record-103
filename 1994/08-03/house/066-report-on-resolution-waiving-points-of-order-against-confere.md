# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 3474, REGULATORY REFORM ACT OF 1993

**Measures:** H.Res. 506, H.R. 3474

```
[Congressional Record: August 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
              ON H.R. 3474, REGULATORY REFORM ACT OF 1993

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-659) on the resolution (H. Res. 506) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill 
(H.R. 3474) to reduce administrative requirements for insured 
depository institutions to the extent consistent with safe and sound 
banking practices, to facilitate the establishment of community 
development financial institutions, and for other purposes, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
