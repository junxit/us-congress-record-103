# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT ON H.R. 3841, INTERSTATE BANKING EFFICIENCY ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.Res. 505, H.R. 3841

```
[Congressional Record: August 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
        ON H.R. 3841, INTERSTATE BANKING EFFICIENCY ACT OF 1994

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-658) on the resolution (H. Res. 505) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill 
(H.R. 3841) to amend the Bank Holding Company Act of 1956, the Revised 
Statutes of the United States, and the Federal Deposit Insurance Act to 
provide for interstate banking and branching, which was referred to the 
House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
