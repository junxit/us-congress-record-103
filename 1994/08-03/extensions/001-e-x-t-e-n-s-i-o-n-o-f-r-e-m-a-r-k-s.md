                            HON. ROB PORTMAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 2, 1994

  Mr. PORTMAN. Mr. Speaker, because of mechanical problems, my airline 
flight into Washington, DC, yesterday was canceled. As a result, I 
missed three votes. Had I been in attendance, I would have voted ''no'' 
on each of rollcall votes numbered 366, 367, and 368.
