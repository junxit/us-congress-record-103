# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Mikulski, Barbara A. (M000702) [D-MD]

```
[Congressional Record: August 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Ms. MIKULSKI. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess as previously ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 8:15 p.m., recessed until 
Thursday, August 4, 1994, at 9 a.m.
```
