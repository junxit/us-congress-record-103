# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Kennedy, Edward M. (K000105) [D-MA]

**Measures:** S. 2042

```
[Congressional Record: May 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. KENNEDY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that upon the 
disposition of the budget resolution conference report, Senator Lott be 
recognized to speak for up to 10 minutes in morning business, after 
which the Senate resume consideration of S. 2042, the Bosnia arms 
embargo legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
