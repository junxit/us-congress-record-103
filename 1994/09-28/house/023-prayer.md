# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: September 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Our hearts reach out to every person who suffers from the violence in 
our communities and in our world, to those who are refugees and have no 
home and to children that are abandoned, to those who have no work and 
whose lives seem meaningless, to those who are hungry and do not have 
the necessities of daily life. O gracious God, from whom comes every 
good gift, bless all those who look for hope in their lives and who 
seek purpose and security and peace. May Your good spirit, that lifts 
up every person, and allows us to see this new day with renewed faith 
and confidence, be with us and remain with us now and all our days. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
