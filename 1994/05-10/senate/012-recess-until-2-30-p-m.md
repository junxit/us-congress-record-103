# RECESS UNTIL 2:30 P.M.

```
[Congressional Record: May 10, 1994]



                         RECESS UNTIL 2:30 P.M.

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the order, the hour of 12 o'clock 
noon having arrived, the Senate will stand in recess until the hour of 
2:30 p.m. today.
  Thereupon, the Senate at 12:02 p.m., recessed until 2:30 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer [Mr. Campbell].

                          ____________________
```
