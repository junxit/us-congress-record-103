# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME], Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
[Congressional Record: March 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. MITCHELL. Madam President, pursuant to this agreement, the Senate 
will take up nominations to the National Labor Relations Board 
beginning at 3 p.m. There is a maximum possible of 2 hours of debate, 
meaning that a vote will occur on the nomination of William B. Gould IV 
to be a member of the National Labor Relations Board no later than 5 
p.m.
  I am advised by staff of the managers that it is likely that not all 
of the time for debate will be used. And, therefore, Senators should be 
aware that a vote may occur at some time, which is not now possible to 
state with certainty, between 3 p.m. and 5 p.m.
  Senators should be aware of that and be prepared to be on the floor 
within 20 minutes.
  I thank my colleagues for their cooperation.
  Madam President, I now yield the floor.
  Madam President, seeing no other Senator seeking recognition, I 
suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LEAHY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
