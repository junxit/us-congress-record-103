# NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD

```
[Congressional Record: March 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                     NATIONAL LABOR RELATIONS BOARD

  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Margaret A. Browning, of 
Pennsylvania, to be a member; the nomination of Frederick L. Feinstein, 
of Maryland, to be general counsel; and the nomination of Charles I. 
Cohen, of Maryland, to be a member.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the nominees are 
confirmed en bloc, the motions to reconsider are laid upon the table en 
bloc, and the President will be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action.
  The nominations considered and confirmed en bloc are as follows:

                     National Labor Relations Board

       Margaret A. Browning, of Pennsylvania, to be a member of 
     the National Labor Relations Board for the remainder of the 
     term expiring December 16, 1997;
       Frederick L. Feinstein, of Maryland, to be general counsel 
     of the National Labor Relations Board for a term of 4 years; 
     and
       Charles I. Cohen, of Maryland, to be a member of the 
     National Labor Relations Board for the term of 5 years 
     expiring August 27, 1996.
```
