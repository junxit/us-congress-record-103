# REPORT ON RESOLUTION REQUESTING A CONFERENCE WITH THE SENATE ON AN AMENDMENT OF THE HOUSE TO S. 636, FREEDOM OF ACCESS TO CLINIC ENTRANCES ACT OF 1993

**Measures:** H.Res. 374, S. 636

```
[Congressional Record: March 2, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION REQUESTING A CONFERENCE WITH THE SENATE ON AN 
AMENDMENT OF THE HOUSE TO S. 636, FREEDOM OF ACCESS TO CLINIC ENTRANCES 
                              ACT OF 1993

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-427) on the resolution (H. Res. 374) to request a 
conference with the Senate on an amendment of the House to the bill (S. 
636) to amend the Public Health Service Act to permit individuals to 
have freedom of access to certain medical clinics and facilities, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
