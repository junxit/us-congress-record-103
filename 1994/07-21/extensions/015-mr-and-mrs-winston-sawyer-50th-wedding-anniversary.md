# MR. AND MRS. WINSTON SAWYER--50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Clyburn, James E. (C000537) [D-SC]

```
[Congressional Record: July 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
         MR. AND MRS. WINSTON SAWYER--50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______


                         HON. JAMES E. CLYBURN

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 21, 1994

  Mr. CLYBURN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to salute Mr. and Mrs. Winston 
Sawyer of Darlington, SC, on the occasion of their 50th wedding 
anniversary.
  Mr. and Mrs. Sawyer's half century of devotion to each other was 
celebrated during a June 11, 1994 cookout, hosted by their children. 
The Sawyers were married on May 24, 1944.
  Mr. Speaker, I join the Sawyers' family and friends in wishing them 
many more happy years together.
```
