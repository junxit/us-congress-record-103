# MR. AND MRS. GUY VASSY DAVIDSON--50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Clyburn, James E. (C000537) [D-SC]

```
[Congressional Record: July 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
       MR. AND MRS. GUY VASSY DAVIDSON--50TH WEDDING ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______


                         HON. JAMES E. CLYBURN

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 21, 1994

  Mr. CLYBURN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to salute Mr. And Mrs. Guy 
Vassy Davidson of Effingham, SC, on the occasion of their 50th wedding 
anniversary.
  Mr. and Mrs. Vassy renewed their vows on June 5, 1994, at Elim 
Baptist Church and were honored with a reception after the ceremony.
  The couple were married on June 9, 1944. Mrs. Davidson, the former 
Frances Ruth Hill, and Mr. Davidson are both retirees of Florence 
Public School District 1.
  Mr. Speaker, the Davidsons' half century of devotion to each other 
should be commended and I join their family and friends in wishing them 
many more happy years together.
```
