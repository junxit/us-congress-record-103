# CONGRATULATING JOHN C. STUBENRAUCH

**Speaking:** Mann, David (M000100) [D-OH]

```
[Congressional Record: July 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   CONGRATULATING JOHN C. STUBENRAUCH

                                 ______


                            HON. DAVID MANN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 21, 1994

  Mr. MANN. Mr. Speaker, I take this opportunity today to congratulate 
Mr. John C. Stubenrauch as he prepares to retire after 35 years of 
service to the citizens of Hamilton County.
  Mr. Stubenrauch graduated from Xavier University in 1958 and began 
working as a caseworker with Hamilton County Human Services that 
September. John began his career as a caseworker and rose through the 
ranks to his current management position as area supervisor in the 
income maintenance division of the department. Most of John's career 
has focused on the disabled and the elderly. His expertise in the area 
of Medicaid and nursing home vendor payments will be sorely missed.
  I join John's family, friends, and colleagues in congratulating him 
on a job well done. I wish him all the best in retirement.
```
