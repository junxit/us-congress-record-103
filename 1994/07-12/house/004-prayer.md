# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: July 12, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We place before You, O God, our needs, our hopes, our joys, and our 
expectations, and all the petitions of our own hearts, that You would 
bless us in our concerns and give us peace according to our needs. We 
recognize that there is nothing too great for Your power and nothing 
too burdensome for Your mercy. As you, O God, have given life in the 
beginning and nurtured every good thing for all Your people, be with us 
this day and every day, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
