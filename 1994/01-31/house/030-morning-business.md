# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Gramm, Phil (G000365) [R-TX]

```
[Congressional Record: January 31, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, there will now be a 
period for the transaction of morning business not to extend beyond the 
hour of 1:15 p.m. with Senators permitted to speak for not to exceed 5 
minutes each. The Senator from Texas [Mr. Gramm] is recognized for up 
to 10 minutes.
  The Senator from Texas.
  Mr. GRAMM. Mr. President, I thank you for your recognition.
```
