# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 2442, H.R. 4278

```
[Congressional Record: May 16, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 2:02 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Ms. Goetz, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 2442. An act to reauthorize economic development 
     programs under the Public Works and Economic Development Act 
     of 1965 and the Appalachian Regional Development Act of 1965 
     for fiscal years 1994 through 1996, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4278. An act to make improvements in the old-age, 
     survivors, and disability insurance program under title II of 
     the Social Security Act.
```
