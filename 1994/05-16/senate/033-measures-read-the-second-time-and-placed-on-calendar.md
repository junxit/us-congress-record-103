# MEASURES READ THE SECOND TIME AND PLACED ON CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT]

```
[Congressional Record: May 16, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
          MEASURES READ THE SECOND TIME AND PLACED ON CALENDAR

  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that it be in 
order for those items currently listed under bills and joint 
resolutions read the third time to be considered to have received their 
second reading, en bloc, and objection having been heard to further 
proceedings, the measures be placed on the calendar as provided for 
under rule XIV, paragraph 2.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
