# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
TRANSFER ANTI-PROGESTIN TECHNOLOGY
Committee on Small Business: Subcommittee on Regulation, Business 
Opportunities, and Technology held a hearing on a report on 
negotiations between European and American interests to transfer anti-
progestin technology to the United States in order to make available 
new drugs and medical therapies. Testimony was heard from 
Representative Schroeder; David A. Kessler, M.D., Commissioner, FDA, 
Department of Health and Human Services; and public witnesses.
  Hearings continue May 23.
DRAFT URUGUAY ROUND IMPLEMENTING LEGISLATIVE PROPOSALS
Committee on Ways and Means: Subcommittee on Trade began consideration 
of draft Uruguay Round implementing legislative proposals.
```
