# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 4912

**Speaking:** Mfume, Kweisi (M000687) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.R. 4912

```
[Congressional Record: September 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 4912

  Mr. MFUME. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that my name be 
removed as a cosponsor of H.R. 4912. While I have no objection to this 
legislation, my name was added as a cosponsor by error.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Maryland?
  There was no objection.
```
