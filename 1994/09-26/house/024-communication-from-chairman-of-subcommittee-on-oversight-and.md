# COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIRMAN OF SUBCOMMITTEE ON OVERSIGHT AND INVESTIGATIONS OF THE COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND COMMERCE

```
[Congressional Record: September 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
     COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIRMAN OF SUBCOMMITTEE ON OVERSIGHT AND 
         INVESTIGATIONS OF THE COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND COMMERCE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable John D. Dingell, chairman of the 
Subcommittee on Oversight and Investigations of the Committee on Energy 
and Commerce:

                                    U.S. House of Representatives,


                             Committee on Energy and Commerce,

                               Washington, DC, September 22, 1994.
     Hon. Thomas S. Foley,
     Speaker, U.S. House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to formally notify you pursuant 
     to Rule L (50) of the Rules of the House that I have received 
     a subpoena for testimony issued by the United States District 
     Court for the District of Columbia.
       After appropriate consultation, I have determined that 
     compliance with the subpoena is inconsistent with the 
     privileges and precedents of the House.
           Sincerely,

                                              John D. Dingell,

                                         Chairman, Subcommittee on
     Oversight and Investigations.

                          ____________________
```
