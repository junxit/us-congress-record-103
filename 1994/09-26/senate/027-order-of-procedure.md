# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY], Specter, Arlen (S000709) [R-PA]

```
[Congressional Record: September 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I understand that the distinguished Senator 
from Pennsylvania wishes to speak. I would like to go ahead and order 
the recess and let him speak as long as he wants to and then we go out 
after his remarks.
  Would that be all right if I do that?
  Mr. SPECTER. It certainly will be.
  Mr. FORD. Fine.
  We are just about ready.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
