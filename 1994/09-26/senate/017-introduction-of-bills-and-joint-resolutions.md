# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Speaking:** Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI], Johnston, J. Bennett (J000189) [D-LA], Cohen, William S. (C000598) [R-ME]

**Measures:** S. 2460, S. 2461, S. 2462

```
[Congressional Record: September 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second time by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. CHAFEE (for himself and Mr. Durenberger):
       S. 2460. A bill to extend for an additional two years the 
     period during which medicare select policies may be issued; 
     to the Committee on Finance.
           By Mr. JOHNSTON:
       S. 2461. A bill to amend the Energy Policy and Conservation 
     Act to manage the Strategic Petroleum Reserve more 
     effectively, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Energy and Natural Resources.
           By Mr. COHEN:
       S. 2462. A bill to amend section 1956 of title 18, United 
     States Code to include equity skimming as a predicate 
     offense, to amend section 1516 of title 18, United States 
     Code to curtail delays in the performance of audits, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Banking, Housing, and 
     Urban Affairs.
```
