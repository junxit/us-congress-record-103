LEGAL INTERCEPTION OF COMMUNICATIONS
Committee on the Judiciary: On Friday, September 23, Subcommittee on 
Technology and the Law approved for full committee consideration, with 
an amendment in the nature of a substitute, S. 2375, to make clear a 
telecommunications carrier's duty to cooperate in the interception of 
communications for law enforcement purposes.
