# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 329, S. 1284, H.R. 1804, S. 1913, S. 1926, H.R. 3345, H.R. 4122

```
[Congressional Record: April 11, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest p. D340)
  S. 1926, to amend the Food and Stamp Act of 1977 to modify the 
requirements relating to monthly reporting and staggered issuance of 
coupons for households residing on Indian reservations, to ensure 
adequate access to retail food stores by food stamp households, and to 
maintain the integrity of the Food Stamp Program. Signed March 25, 
1994. (P.L. 103-225)
  H.R. 3345, to amend title 5, United States Code, to eliminate certain 
restrictions on employee training, and to provide temporary authority 
to agencies relating to voluntary separation incentive payments. Signed 
March 30, 1994. (P.L. 103-226)
  H.R. 1804, to improve learning and teaching by providing a national 
framework for education reform, to promote the research, consensus 
building, and systemic changes needed to ensure equitable educational 
opportunities and high levels of educational achievement for all 
American students, to provide a framework for reauthorization of all 
Federal education programs, and to promote the development and adoption 
of a voluntary national system of skill standards and certifications. 
Signed March 31, 1994. (P.L. 103-227)
  H.R. 4122, to temporarily extend certain provisions of the Marine 
Mammal Protection Act. Signed March 31, 1994. (P.L. 103-228)
  H.J. Res. 329, designating March 23, 1994, as ``Education and Sharing 
Day, U.S.A.''. Signed April 6, 1994. (P.L. 103-229)
  S. 1284, to amend the Developmental Disabilities Assistance and Bill 
of Rights Act to expand or modify certain provisions relating to 
programs for individuals with developmental disabilities, Federal 
assistance for priority area activities for individuals with 
developmental disabilities, protection and advocacy of individual 
rights, university affiliated programs, and projects of national 
significance. Signed April 6, 1994. (P.L. 103-230)
  S. 1913, to extend certain compliance dates for pesticide safety 
training and labeling requirements. Signed April 6, 1994. (P.L. 103-
231)
```
