# IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXSCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: April 11, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
            IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXSCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Mr. President, at the close of business on Friday, April 
8, the Federal debt stood at $4,560,730,816,474.55, meaning that on a 
per capita basis, every man, woman and child in America owes $17,493.43 
as his or her share of that debt.
```
