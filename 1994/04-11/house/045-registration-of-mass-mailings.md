# REGISTRATION OF MASS MAILINGS

```
[Congressional Record: April 11, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                     REGISTRATION OF MASS MAILINGS

  The filing date for 1994 first quarter mass mailings is April 25, 
1994. If a Senator's office did no mass mailings during this period, 
please submit a form that states ``none.''
  Mass mailing registrations, or negative reports, should be submitted 
to the Senate Office of Pubic Records, 232 Hart Building, Washington, 
DC 20510-7116.
  The Public Records Office will be open from 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. on the 
filing date to accept these filings. For further information, please 
contact the Public Records Office on (202) 224-0322.

                          ____________________
```
