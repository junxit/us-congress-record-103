                         COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR

                       WEDNESDAY, AUGUST 17, 1994

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on the Judiciary, to hold hearings on the nominations of 
William C. Bryson, of Maryland, to be U.S. Circuit Judge for the 
Federal Circuit; Salvador E. Casellas and Daniel R. Dominguez, each 
to be a U.S. District Judge for the District of Puerto Rico; and 
Sarah S. Vance, to be U.S. District Judge for the Eastern District 
of Louisiana, 2 p.m., SD-226.


                                 House

  Committee on the Judiciary, Subcommittee on Civil and 
Constitutional Rights, to mark up H.R. 4922, to amend title 18, 
United States Code, to make clear a telecommunications carrier's 
duty to cooperate in the interception of communications for law 
enforcement purposes, 2 p.m., 2226 Rayburn.
  Subcommittee on Economic and Commercial Law, oversight hearing on 
Bankruptcy Reform, 10 a.m., 2141 Rayburn.
  Committee on Rules, to continue consideration of Health Care 
Reform, 11 a.m., H-313 Capitol.
