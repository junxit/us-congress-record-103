# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2396

**Speaking:** Lautenberg, Frank R. (L000123) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** S. 2396

```
[Congressional Record: August 16, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2396

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I understand that S. 2396, the 
Affordable Health Care Now Act, introduced earlier today by Senator 
Lott, is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator is correct.
  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2396), entitled the ``Affordable Health Care Now 
     Act.''

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask now for its second reading; and, 
on behalf of the Republican leader, I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
