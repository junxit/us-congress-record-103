# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: August 16, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. David James Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Teach us, we pray, to savor the moments of quiet when the intensity 
of the day is eased and the burdens of responsibility are laid aside. 
Teach us then, in the silence of meditation and reflection, to place 
before You our thoughts and ideas, our feelings and worries, so that 
You can forgive us and nurture us and strengthen us for the days ahead. 
O gracious God, as You have created the Heavens and the Earth and each 
of us, bless us this day and every day. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
