                         COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR

                        WEDNESDAY, JULY 27, 1994

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, Subcommittee on Water 
and Power, to hold hearings on S. 2253, to modify the Mountain Park 
Project in Oklahoma; S. 2262, to amend the Elwha River Ecosystem and 
Fisheries Restoration Act; and S. 2266, to amend the Recreation 
Management Act of 1992, 2 p.m., SD-366.
  Committee on Finance, business meeting, to resume markup of 
proposed legislation to implement the Uruguay Round of Multilateral 
Trade Negotiations, 10 a.m., SD-215.
  Committee on Foreign Relations, to hold hearings on the nomination 
of Ralph Earle II, of the District of Columbia, to be Deputy 
Director of the U.S. Arms Control and Disarmament Agency, 10 a.m., 
SD-419.
  Full Committee, to hold a closed briefing on the status of 
negotiations on Bosnia, 2:30 p.m., S-116, Capitol.
  Committee on Governmental Affairs, to hold hearings to examine the 
pricing of prescription drugs, 9:30 a.m., SD-342.
  Committee on the Judiciary, to hold hearings on the nomination of 
Lois Jane Schiffer, of the District of Columbia, to be an Assistant 
Attorney General, Department of Justice, 10 a.m., SD-226.
  Committee on Small Business, to hold hearings on the Small 
Business Administration's Minority Business Development program, 
focusing on the implementation of the Business Opportunity 
Development Reform Act of 1988 (P.L. 100-656) and the 
recommendations of the Commission on Minority Business Development, 
2 p.m., SR-428A.
