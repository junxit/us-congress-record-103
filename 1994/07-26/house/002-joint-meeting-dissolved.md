# JOINT MEETING DISSOLVED

```
[Congressional Record: July 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        JOINT MEETING DISSOLVED

  The SPEAKER. The purpose of the joint meeting having been completed, 
the Chair declares the joint meeting of the two Houses dissolved, and 
the House will meet in session at about 1:30 p.m.
  Accordingly, at 12 o'clock and 9 minutes p.m., the joint meeting of 
the two Houses was dissolved.
  The Members of the Senate retired to their Chamber.
```
