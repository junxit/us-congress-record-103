# FOR THE RELIEF OF MELISSA JOHNSON

**Measures:** H.R. 572

```
[Congressional Record: July 15, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   FOR THE RELIEF OF MELISSA JOHNSON

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar No. 489, H.R. 572, a 
bill to provide for the relief of Melissa Johnson; that the bill be 
deemed read three times, passed and the motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table; and that any statements relating to this measure be 
placed in the Record at the appropriate place.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 572) was deemed read three times and passed.
```
