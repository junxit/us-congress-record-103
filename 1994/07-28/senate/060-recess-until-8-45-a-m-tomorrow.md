# RECESS UNTIL 8:45 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 246

```
[Congressional Record: July 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    RECESS UNTIL 8:45 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, I now ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
stand in recess as provided for under S. Res. 246, as a mark of further 
respect for the late Honorable Hugh Scott.
```
