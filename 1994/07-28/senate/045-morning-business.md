# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: July 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not to 
extend beyond the hour of 10:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for not to exceed 5 minutes each.
  Under the previous order, the Senator from Florida [Mr. Graham] is 
now recognized to speak for up to 30 minutes. The Chair recognizes the 
Senator from Florida [Mr. Graham].
```
