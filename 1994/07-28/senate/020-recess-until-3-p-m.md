# RECESS UNTIL 3 P.M.

```
[Congressional Record: July 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          RECESS UNTIL 3 P.M.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the hour of 12 noon 
having arrived, the Senate will stand in recess until the hour of 3 
p.m.
  Thereupon, at 12:02 p.m., the Senate recessed until 3 p.m.; 
whereupon, the Senate reassembled when called to order by the Presiding 
Officer (Mr. Lieberman.)
```
