# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Hoagland, Peter (H000652) [D-NE]

**Measures:** S. 2182

```
[Congressional Record: July 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                          HON. PETER HOAGLAND

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 28, 1994

  Mr. HOAGLAND. Mr. Speaker, I would like to submit for the record that 
I missed one recorded vote on Monday, July 25, 1994, due to an 
unexpected weather delay during air travel from Omaha, NE to 
Washington, DC.
  Had I been present I would have voted in support of the motion to 
close portions of the conference on S. 2182, the Defense Authorization 
Act and the Military Construction Act for Fiscal Year 1995.
```
