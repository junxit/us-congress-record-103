# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION WAIVING THE REQUIREMENT OF THE LEGISLATIVE REORGANIZATION ACT FOR ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE OF THE CONGRESS NOT LATER THAN...

**Measures:** H.Res. 495

```
[Congressional Record: July 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
   REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF A CONCURRENT 
 RESOLUTION WAIVING THE REQUIREMENT OF THE LEGISLATIVE REORGANIZATION 
  ACT FOR ADJOURNMENT SINE DIE OF THE CONGRESS NOT LATER THAN JULY 31

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-628) on the resolution (H. Res. 495) providing 
for consideration of a concurrent resolution waiving the requirement in 
section 132 of the Legislative Reorganization Act of 1946 that the 
Congress adjourn sine die not later than July 31 of each year, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
