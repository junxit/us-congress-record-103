# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
[Congressional Record: July 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Engel). The Chair would remind the 
Members that remarks in debate may not include references to the 
official conduct of other Members unless pertinent to a Report of the 
Committee on Standards of Official Conduct or a question of the 
privileges of the House then under consideration in the House. The 
Chair would reiterate the caution issued by Speaker Pro Tempore Foley 
on June 15, 1988, that Members refrain from engaging in personalities 
in debate by referring to the official conduct of other Members.
```
