# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** English, Karan (E000186) [D-AZ]

**Measures:** S. 208

```
[Congressional Record: July 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. ENGLISH of Arizona. Mr. Speaker, because my husband's business is 
a concession operation at Grand Canyon National Park and operates 
pursuant to permits issued by the National Park Service as required by 
the Concessions Policy Act, I have officially recused myself from 
consideration of the National Park Service Concessions Policy Reform 
Act, S. 208, as required by House rules. Accordingly, I have and will 
vote present on all votes pertaining to this legislation.

                          ____________________
```
