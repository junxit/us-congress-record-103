# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 2182

```
[Congressional Record: July 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Hallen, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate disagrees to the amendment of the House to the bill (S. 
2182) ``An act to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 1995 for 
military activities of the Department of Defense, for military 
construction, and for defense programs of the Department of Energy, to 
prescribe personnel strengths for such fiscal year for the Armed 
Forces, and for other purposes,'' agrees to the conference asked for by 
the House on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses thereon, and 
appoints Mr. Nunn, Mr. Exon, Mr. Levin, Mr. Kennedy, Mr. Bingaman, Mr. 
Glenn, Mr. Shelby, Mr. Byrd, Mr. Graham, Mr. Robb, Mr. Lieberman, Mr. 
Bryan, Mr. Thurmond, Mr. Warner, Mr. Cohen, Mr. McCain, Mr. Lott, Mr. 
Coats, Mr. Smith, Mr. Kempthorne, Mr. Faircloth, and Mrs. Hutchison, to 
be the conferees on the part of the Senate.
```
