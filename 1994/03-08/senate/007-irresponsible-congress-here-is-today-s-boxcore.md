# IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS? HERE IS TODAY'S BOXCORE

**Speaking:** Helms, Jesse (H000463) [R-NC]

```
[Congressional Record: March 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        IRRESPONSIBLE CONGRESS?
                        HERE IS TODAY'S BOXCORE

  Mr. HELMS. Madam President, as of the close of business on Monday, 
March 7, the Federal debt stood at $4,547,792,538,698.51, meaning that 
on a per capita basis, every man, woman, and child in America owes 
$17,443.81 as his or her share of that debt.
```
