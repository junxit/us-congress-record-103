# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING THE RECESS

**Measures:** S. 1789

```
[Congressional Record: March 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING THE RECESS

  Under the authority of the order of January 5, 1993, the Secretary of 
the Senate, on March 3, 1994, during the recess of the Senate, received 
a message from the House announcing that the Speaker has signed the 
following enrolled bill:

       S. 1789. An act to amend title 23, United States Code, to 
     permit the use of funds under the highway bridge replacement 
     and rehabilitation for seismic retrofit of bridges and for 
     other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was signed on today, March 8, 1994, by the 
President pro tempore [Mr. Byrd].

                          ____________________
```
