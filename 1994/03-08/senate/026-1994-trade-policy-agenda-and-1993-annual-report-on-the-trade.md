# 1994 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND 1993 ANNUAL REPORT ON THE TRADE AGREEMENTS PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 95

```
[Congressional Record: March 8, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
1994 TRADE POLICY AGENDA AND 1993 ANNUAL REPORT ON THE TRADE AGREEMENTS 
               PROGRAM--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 95

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Finance:

To the Congress of the United States:
  As required by section 163 of the Trade Act of 1974, as amended (19 
U.S.C. 2213), I transmit herewith the 1994 Trade Policy Agenda and 1993 
Annual Report on the Trade Agreements Program.
                                                   William J. Clinton. 
  The White House, March 8, 1994.

                          ____________________
```
