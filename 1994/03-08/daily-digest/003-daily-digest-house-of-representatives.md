                        House of Representatives


Chamber Action
Bills Introduced: 16 public bills, H.R. 3967-3982; 1 private bill, H.R. 
3983; and 5 resolutions, H.J. Res. 331, H. Con. Res. 216-217, and H.

                                                             Page H1087

Report Filed: One report was filed as follows: H. Con. Res. 218, 
setting forth the congressional budget for the United States Government 
for fiscal years 1995, 1996, 1997, 1998, and 1999 (H. Rept. 103-428).

                                                             Page H1087

Recess: House recessed at 11:23 a.m. and reconvened at noon.

                                                             Page H1075

Journal: By a yea-and-nay vote of 211 yeas to 132 nays, Roll No. 42, 
the House approved the Journal of Monday, March 7.

                                                     Pages H1075, H1085

Federal Workforce Restructuring: House agreed to H. Res. 380, providing 
for the concurrence by the House with an amendment to the amendment of 
the Senate to H.R. 3345, to provide temporary authority to Government 
agencies relating to voluntary separation incentive payments.

                                                         Pages H1077-82

Presidential Messages: Read the following messages from the President:
  Trade policy agenda: Message wherein he transmits the 1994 Trade 
Policy Agenda and the 1993 Annual Report on the Trade Agreements 
Program--referred to the Committee on Ways and Means; and

                                                             Page H1082

  Corporation for Public Broadcasting: Message wherein he transmits the 
report of the Corporation for Public Broadcasting--referred to the 
Committee on Education and Labor.

                                                             Page H1082

Recess: House recessed at 12:43 p.m. and reconvened at 5:03 p.m.

                                                             Page H1084

Meeting Hour: Agreed that the House will meet at noon on Wednesday, 
March 9.

                                                             Page H1086

Late Report: The Committee on the Budget received permission to have 
until midnight tonight to file a report on H. Con. Res. 218, setting 
forth the congressional budget for the United States Government for 
fiscal years 1995, 1996, 1997, 1998, and 1999.

                                                             Page H1086

Quorum Calls--Votes: One yea-and-nay vote developed during the 
proceedings of the House today and appears on page H1085. There were no 
quorum calls.
Adjournment: Met at 10:30 a.m. and adjourned at 5:36 p.m.
