# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 275

```
[Congressional Record: July 29, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:05 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Hays, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
agreed to the following concurrent resolution, in which it requests of 
the Senate:

       H. Con. Res. 275. Concurrent Resolution waiving the 
     requirement in section 132 of the Legislative Reorganization 
     Act of 1946 that the Congress adjourn sine die not later than 
     July 31 of each year.
```
