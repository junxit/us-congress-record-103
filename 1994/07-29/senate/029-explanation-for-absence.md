# EXPLANATION FOR ABSENCE

**Speaking:** Graham, Bob (G000352) [D-FL]

```
[Congressional Record: July 29, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        EXPLANATION FOR ABSENCE

● Mr. GRAHAM. Mr. President, I will be absent the afternoon of 
Friday, July 29, 1994, to attend my daughter's wedding. Thus, I will 
not be present for the vote on the nomination of Mr. Breyer to be an 
Associate Supreme Court Justice. Had I been present, I would have voted 
``aye.''●
```
