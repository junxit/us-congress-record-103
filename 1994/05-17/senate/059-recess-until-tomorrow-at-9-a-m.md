# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I thank the Chair.
  Mr. President, if there is no further business to come before the 
Senate today, and no Senator wishes to speak, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Senate stand in recess as previously ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:28 p.m., recessed until 
tomorrow, Wednesday, May 18, 1994, at 9 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
