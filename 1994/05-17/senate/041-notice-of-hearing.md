# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Inouye, Daniel K. (I000025) [D-HI]

**Measures:** S. 2074, S. 2075

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      Committee on Indian Affairs

  Mr. INOUYE. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Senate 
Committee on Indian Affairs will be holding a hearing on Tuesday, May 
24, 1994, beginning at 9:30 a.m., in 485 Russell Senate Office Building 
on S. 2075, to amend the Indian Child Protection and Family Violence 
Prevention Act to reauthorize and improve programs under the act; and 
S. 2074, the Crime Victim Assistance Improvement Act.
  Those wishing additional informational should contact the Committee 
on Indian Affairs at 224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
