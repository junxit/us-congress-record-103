# VIETNAM HUMAN RIGHTS DAY

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY], Chafee, John H. (C000269) [R-RI]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 168

```
                        VIETNAM HUMAN RIGHTS DAY

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask that the Chair lay before the Senate a 
message from the House of Representatives on a joint resolution (S.J. 
Res. 168) designating May 11, 1994, as ``Vietnam Human Rights Day.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the House of Representatives:

       Resolved, That the resolution from the Senate (S.J. Res. 
     168) entitled ``Joint Resolution designating May 11, 1994, as 
     `Vietnam Human Rights Day''', do pass with the following 
     amendments:
       Page 1, in the third clause of the preamble, strike out ``, 
     Dr. Nguyen Dan Que,''.
       Page 2, in the last clause of the preamble, strike out 
     ``including Dr. Nguyen Dan Que,''.
  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I move that the Senate concur in the 
amendments of the House.
  The motion was agreed to.
  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I move to reconsider the vote by which the 
motion was agreed to.
  Mr. CHAFEE. I move to lay that motion on the table.
  The motion to lay on the table was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
