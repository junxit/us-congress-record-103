# CHILD ABUSE ACCOUNTABILITY ACT

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 3694

```
[Congressional Record: September 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                     CHILD ABUSE ACCOUNTABILITY ACT

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 3694, the Child Abuse 
Accountability Act, just received from the House; and that bill be 
deemed read three times, passed, and a motion to reconsider be laid 
upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the bill (H.R. 3694) was deemed read three times, and passed.
```
