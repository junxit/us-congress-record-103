# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 3, 1994

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
[Congressional Record: September 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   ORDERS FOR MONDAY, OCTOBER 3, 1994

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
stand in recess until 5 p.m., Monday, October 3; that following the 
prayer, the Journal of proceedings be deemed approved to date and the 
time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in the day; that 
the time until 6 p.m. be equally divided and controlled between the 
majority leader and the minority leader, or their designees.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
