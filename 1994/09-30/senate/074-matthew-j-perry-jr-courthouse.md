# MATTHEW J. PERRY, JR. COURTHOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 4543

```
[Congressional Record: September 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    MATTHEW J. PERRY, JR. COURTHOUSE

  The bill (H.R. 4543) to designate the United States courthouse to be 
constructed at 907 Richland Street in Columbia, SC, as the ``Matthew J. 
Perry, Jr. United States Courthouse'' was considered, ordered to a 
third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
