# PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON PUBLIC WORKS AND TRANSPORTATION TO FILE REPORT ON H.R. 4460, WATER RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 1994

**Speaking:** Laughlin, Greg (L000119) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 4460

```
[Congressional Record: September 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  PERMISSION FOR COMMITTEE ON PUBLIC WORKS AND TRANSPORTATION TO FILE 
      REPORT ON H.R. 4460, WATER RESOURCES DEVELOPMENT ACT OF 1994

  Mr. LAUGHLIN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Public Works and Transportation may have until 6 p.m., September 30, 
1994, to file the committee report on H.R. 4460, the Water Resources 
Development Act 1994.
  It is my understanding that this request has been cleared by both the 
minority leadership of the House and the Committee on Public Works and 
Transportation.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
