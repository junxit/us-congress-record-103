# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, OCTOBER 6, 1994, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING MR. NELSON MANDELA, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF SOUTH AFRICA

**Speaking:** Byrne, Leslie L. (B001213) [D-VA]

```
[Congressional Record: September 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
  AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, OCTOBER 6, 
1994, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING MR. NELSON MANDELA, 
               PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF SOUTH AFRICA

  Mrs. BYRNE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order at any time on Thursday, October 6, 1994, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting Mr. Nelson Mandela, President of the 
Republic of South Africa.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.
```
