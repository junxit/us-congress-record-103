# H.R. 6: MOST SWEEPING EDUCATIONAL IMPROVEMENT IN DECADES

**Speaking:** Klink, Ron (K000270) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 6

```
[Congressional Record: September 30, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
        H.R. 6: MOST SWEEPING EDUCATIONAL IMPROVEMENT IN DECADES

  (Mr. KLINK asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. KLINK. Mr. Speaker, the vote on H.R. 6 is a vote on the future of 
education.
  All the issues that you may hear about, all the motions to recommit, 
are red herrings. H.R. 6 represents the most sweeping educational 
improvement and reform legislation in decades. It is our best chance to 
increase Federal aid for elementary and secondary education.
  Despite what you may hear, ``constitutionally protected'' school 
prayer is protected under H.R. 6. H.R. 6 also prohibits the use of 
Federal funds to promote or encourage any sexual practice.
  But the real issue is funding for education. If we recommit H.R. 6 to 
conference, $11 billion in education aid is in jeopardy. Federal 
education funding could be cut off to 13,000 school districts.
  So ignore the red herrings. Support education and H.R. 6.
```
