# CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE AND SENATE

**Speaking:** Gephardt, Richard A. (G000132) [D-MO]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 289

```
[Congressional Record: August 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]


                              {time}  2000
 
            CONDITIONAL ADJOURNMENT OF THE HOUSE AND SENATE

  Mr. GEPHARDT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns on the legislative day of Sunday, August 21, 1994, it 
adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. on Tuesday and Friday of each subsequent 
week until the House has in its possession the message informing the 
House that the Senate has agreed to House Concurrent Resolution 289. 
The House shall then stand adjourned in accordance with that 
resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Kildee). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Missouri?
  There was no objection.
```
