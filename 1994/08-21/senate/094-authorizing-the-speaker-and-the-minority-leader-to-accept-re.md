# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER AND THE MINORITY LEADER TO ACCEPT RESIGNATIONS AND MAKE APPOINTMENTS, NOTWITHSTANDING ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Gephardt, Richard A. (G000132) [D-MO]

```
[Congressional Record: August 21, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER AND THE MINORITY LEADER TO ACCEPT RESIGNATIONS 
           AND MAKE APPOINTMENTS, NOTWITHSTANDING ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. GEPHARDT. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that 
notwithstanding any adjournment of the House until Thursday, September 
8, 1994, the Speaker and the minority leader shall be authorized to 
accept resignations and to make appointments authorized by law or by 
the House.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Missouri?
  There was no objection.
```
