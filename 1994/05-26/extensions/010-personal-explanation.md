# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Ortiz, Solomon P. (O000107) [D-TX]

```
[Congressional Record: May 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                         HON. SOLOMON P. ORTIZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 26, 1994

  Mr. ORTIZ. Mr. Speaker, I was unavoidably absent from proceedings of 
the House on Monday, May 23; Tuesday, May 24; and part of Wednesday, 
May 25 owing to the death of my mother.
  I further ask unanimous consent that a statement reflecting how I 
would have voted with respect to each rollcall vote appear immediately 
following the vote in the permanent Congressional Record.
  Had I been here, I would have voted as follows: Rollcall vote No. 
191, ``aye''; rollcall vote No. 192, ``nay''; rollcall vote No. 193, 
``aye''; rollcall vote No. 194, ``nay''; rollcall vote No. 195, 
``aye''; rollcall vote No. 196, ``aye''; rollcall vote No. 197, 
``nay''; rollcall vote No. 198, ``nay''; rollcall vote No. 199, 
``nay''; rollcall vote No. 200, ``aye''; rollcall vote No. 201, 
``aye''; and rollcall vote No. 202, ``aye.''
```
