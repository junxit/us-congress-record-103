# ON CHINA

**Speaking:** Traficant, James A., Jr. (T000350) [D-OH]

```
[Congressional Record: May 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                ON CHINA

  (Mr. TRAFICANT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TRAFICANT. Mr. Speaker, China is now dictating to Congress. China 
said they will not tolerate or accept any conditions from the Congress 
of the United States at all on their most-favored-nation trade status.
  Wow, Congress, how does that tame your dragon hair?
  Chinese dictators--any Congress that will allow a bunch of Chinese 
dictators to set down the law on American trade policy should be 
impeached. China is now No. 2, right behind Japan, with a $26 billion 
surplus and telling us how we should vote. Shame, Congress. Shame. Who 
do you represent, the workers in a Chinese prison camp, or the laid-off 
workers in cities all over American?

                          ____________________
```
