House of Representatives

Thursday, May 26, 1994
  The House met at 10 a.m.
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We pray, O God, for our homes and our communities, those people and 
places that are close to our hearts and touch us in our daily lives. We 
recognize that our families and communities are critical to a healthy 
national life, to our own freedom, and to the fullness of the joy that 
is Your wish for us. Teach us to work for understanding and respect, to 
honor our differences, and to learn to grow in appreciation with one 
another, so our families and communities will be a haven of blessing 
and of peace. In Your name, we pray. Amen.
