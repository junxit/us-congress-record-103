# RESIGNATION AS CONFEREE AND APPOINTMENT OF REPLACEMENT CONFEREE ON H.R. 3841, INTERSTATE BANKING EFFICIENCY ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.R. 3841

```
[Congressional Record: May 26, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
RESIGNATION AS CONFEREE AND APPOINTMENT OF REPLACEMENT CONFEREE ON H.R. 
            3841, INTERSTATE BANKING EFFICIENCY ACT OF 1994

  The SPEAKER pro tempore, laid before the House the following 
resignation as a conferee:

                                   Committee on the Judiciary,

                                     Washington, DC, May 25, 1994.
     Hon. Thomas S. Foley,
     Speaker, U.S. House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: I wish to be excused from services as a 
     conferee on the conference committee on the bill H.R. 3841, 
     to amend the Bank Holding Company Act of 1956.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
                                                      Jack Brooks,
                                                         Chairman.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the resignation is 
accepted and without objection, the Chair appoints the gentleman from 
Kentucky, Mr. Mazzoli, to serve in lieu of Mr. Brooks of Texas, 
resigned, as a manager on the part of the House at the conference on 
the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the bill, H.R. 3841.
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The clerk will notify the Senate of the 
change in conferees.

                          ____________________
```
