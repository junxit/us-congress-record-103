# ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

**Measures:** S. 1587

```
[Congressional Record: June 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

  Mr. FORD. On behalf of the majority leader, I ask unanimous consent 
that when the Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess 
until 9:30 a.m., June 8; that following the prayer the Journal of the 
proceedings be deemed approved to date, and the time for the two 
leaders reserved for their use later in the day; that there be a period 
for morning business not to extend beyond 10 a.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 5 minutes each, with Senator 
Faircloth recognized to speak for up to 15 minutes; that at 10 a.m., 
the Senate resume consideration of S. 1587, the Federal Procurement 
Streamlining Act; that on Wednesday, the Senate stand in recess from 
12:30 p.m. to 2:15 p.m. in order to accommodate the respective party 
conferences.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
