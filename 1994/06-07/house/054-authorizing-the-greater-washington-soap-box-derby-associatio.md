# AUTHORIZING THE GREATER WASHINGTON SOAP BOX DERBY ASSOCIATION TO USE THE CAPITOL GROUNDS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 238

```
[Congressional Record: June 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 AUTHORIZING THE GREATER WASHINGTON SOAP BOX DERBY ASSOCIATION TO USE 
                          THE CAPITOL GROUNDS

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of House Concurrent Resolution 
238, authorizing the use of the Capitol grounds by the Greater 
Washington Soap Box Derby Association, just received from the House; 
that the concurrent resolution be agreed to and the motion to 
reconsider be laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  So the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 238) was agreed to.
```
