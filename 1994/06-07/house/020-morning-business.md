# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: June 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that there now be 
a period for morning business with Senators permitted to speak therein 
for up to 5 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
