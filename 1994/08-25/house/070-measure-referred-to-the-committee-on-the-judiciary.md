# MEASURE REFERRED TO THE COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

**Measures:** S. 2375

```
[Congressional Record: August 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
           MEASURE REFERRED TO THE COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that Calendar No. 
603, S. 2375, a bill to make clear a telecommunications carrier's duty

purposes be referred to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
