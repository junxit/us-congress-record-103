# NOMINATIONS PLACED ON CALENDAR

```
[Congressional Record: August 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                     NOMINATIONS PLACED ON CALENDAR

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Committee on 
Commerce, Science and Transportation be discharged from further 
consideration of the following nominations, that they be placed on the 
calendar: Henry J. Cauthen, to be a Member of the Board of Directors of 
the Corporation for Public Broadcasting, and Frank Henry Cruz, to be a 
Member of the Board of Directors of the Corporation for Public 
Broadcasting.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
