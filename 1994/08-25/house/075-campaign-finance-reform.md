# CAMPAIGN FINANCE REFORM

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 3

```
[Congressional Record: August 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        CAMPAIGN FINANCE REFORM

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Chair lay 
before the Senate the message from the House on S. 3, the campaign 
finance reform bill; that the Senate disagree to the House amendments, 
request a conference with the House on the disagreeing votes of the two 
Houses; and that the Chair be authorized to appoint conferees.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I object.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  Mr. FORD. It is on the record.
```
