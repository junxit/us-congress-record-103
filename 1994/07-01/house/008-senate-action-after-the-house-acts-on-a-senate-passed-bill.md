# SENATE ACTION AFTER THE HOUSE ACTS ON A SENATE-PASSED BILL

**Speaking:** Nunn, Sam (N000171) [D-GA]

**Measures:** S. 2182, S. 2206, S. 2211

```
[Congressional Record: July 1, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
       SENATE ACTION AFTER THE HOUSE ACTS ON A SENATE-PASSED BILL

  Mr. NUNN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that with respect to 
S. 2182 and S. 2206 through S. 2211, as just passed by the Senate, 
that, if the Senate receives a message with regard to any one of these 
bills from the House of Representatives, the Senate disagree with the 
House on its amendment or amendments to the Senate-passed bill, and 
agree to or request for a conference with the House on the disagreeing 
votes of the two Houses, whichever is appropriate; that the Chair be 
authorized to appoint conferees; and, that the foregoing occur without 
any intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
