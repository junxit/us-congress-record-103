# CORRECTION ON AMENDMENT NO. 2104 TO H.R. 4426

**Speaking:** Nunn, Sam (N000171) [D-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4426

```
[Congressional Record: July 1, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
             CORRECTION ON AMENDMENT NO. 2104 TO H.R. 4426

  Mr. NUNN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that amendment No. 
2104 to the committee amendment on page 2, line 21 to H.R. 4426, the 
Foreign Operations Appropriations bill, that was adopted by the Senate 
on June 29, 1994, be corrected as follows:
  Section (c) finding: Subsection 4, line 4: The figure should be 
1,200,000,000 and not 1,200,000.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
