# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Exon, J. James (E000284) [D-NE]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 215

```
[Congressional Record: August 23, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

                                 ______


                       HONORING JAMES NORMAN HALL

  Mr. EXON. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar No. 581, House 
Concurrent Resolution 215, a concurrent resolution honoring and 
recognizing James Norman Hall and his contribution to the United States 
and the South Pacific; that the concurrent resolution be agreed to; 
that the motion to reconsider be laid on the table; and that the 
preamble be agreed to; further, that any statement appear in the Record 
as if read.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 215) was considered and 
agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
```
