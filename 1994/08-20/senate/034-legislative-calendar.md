# LEGISLATIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Gephardt, Richard A. (G000132) [D-MO]

```
[Congressional Record: August 20, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          LEGISLATIVE CALENDAR

  (Mr. GEPHARDT asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. GEPHARDT. Mr. Speaker, I take this time to announce to Members 
what our plan for the day is. We have had a series of negotiations 
through the night. We are close to being able to move to the conference 
and to be able to produce a conference report, but additional writing 
and work needs to go on, and it would be my estimate that we could not 
get to a vote on a conference report until 4 or 5 o'clock this 
afternoon. We have put this information on the recording devices so 
that Members calling can find that out.
  We would not intend to have votes until we can get to the rule, if we 
can, and the conference report. We would ask for recess authority so 
that we could very soon here go into recess until that time.
  We would have a 2-hour notice through the Cloakrooms before a vote 
would occur.
```
