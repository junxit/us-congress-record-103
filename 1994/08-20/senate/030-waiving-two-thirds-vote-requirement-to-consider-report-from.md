# WAIVING TWO-THIRDS VOTE REQUIREMENT TO CONSIDER REPORT FROM COMMITTEE ON RULES ON SUNDAY, AUGUST 21, 1994

**Speaking:** Moakley, John Joseph (M000834) [D-MA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3355

```
[Congressional Record: August 20, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 WAIVING TWO-THIRDS VOTE REQUIREMENT TO CONSIDER REPORT FROM COMMITTEE 
                  ON RULES ON SUNDAY, AUGUST 21, 1994

  Mr. MOAKLEY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
requirement of clause 4(b) of rule XI for a two-thirds vote to consider 
a report from the Committee on Rules on the same day it is presented to 
the House be waived on the legislative day of Sunday, August 21, 1994, 
with respect to a resolution providing for the consideration or 
disposition of the conference report to accompany H.R. 3355.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
Massachusetts?
  There was no objection.
```
