# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 15, 1994

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** H.R. 4426

```
[Congressional Record: July 14, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JULY 15, 1994

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it stand in recess until 9 a.m., 
Friday, July 15, that following the prayer, the Journal of proceedings 
be deemed approved to date and the time for the two leaders reserved 
for their use later in the day; that immediately thereafter the Senate 
resume consideration of H.R. 4426, the Foreign Operations 
Appropriations bill, under the limitations and conditions of a previous 
unanimous consent agreement.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
