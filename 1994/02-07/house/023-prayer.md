# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: February 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We remember in this our prayer, O God, those who face the anxieties 
of the day and desire the comfort and reassurance that Your Spirit can 
give. For those who are ill in body or spirit, we pray for healing; for 
those who know distress because of conflict or controversy, we pray for 
serenity and concord; for those who wish for understanding in 
relationships, we pray for patience and tolerance; for those who are 
separated from family or friends, we pray for reconciliation; and for 
all Your people who seek to live lives worthy of Your calling, we pray 
for Your peace that passes all human understanding. This is our earnest 
prayer. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
