# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Pell, Claiborne (P000193) [D-RI]

```
[Congressional Record: February 7, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                     committee on foreign relations

  Mr. PELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Foreign Relations be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on Tuesday, February 8, 1994, at 12 p.m. to hear nominees Wesley 
W. Egan, Jr., Ambassador to Jordan, and Robert H. Pelletreau, Jr., 
Assistant Secretary of State for Near Eastern Affairs.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
