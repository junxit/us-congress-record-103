# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 131, H.J.Res. 175, H.R. 1426, H.R. 1631, H.R. 1933, H.R. 2739, H.R. 4426, H.R. 4453

```
[Congressional Record: August 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

     (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D1035)
  H.R. 1426, to provide for the maintenance of dams located on Indian 
lands by the Bureau of Indian Affairs or through contracts with Indian 
tribes. Signed August 23, 1994. (P.L. 103-302)
  H.R. 1631, to amend title 11, District of Columbia Code, to increase 
the maximum amount in controversy permitted for cases under the 
jurisdiction of the Small Claims and Conciliation Branch of the 
Superior Court of the District of Columbia. Signed August 23, 1994. 
(P.L. 103-303)
  H.R. 1933, to authorize appropriations for the Martin Luther King, 
Jr., Federal Holiday Commission, and establish a National Service Day 
to promote community service. Signed August 23, 1994. (P.L. 103-304)
  H.R. 2739, to amend the Airport and Airway Improvement Act of 1982 to 
authorize appropriations for fiscal years 1994, 1995, and 1996. Signed 
August 23, 1994. (P.L. 103-305)
  H.R. 4426, making appropriations for foreign operations, export 
financing, and related programs for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 1995. Signed August 23, 1994. (P.L. 103-306)
  H.R. 4453, making appropriations for military construction for the 
Department of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1995. 
Signed August 23, 1994. (P.L. 103-307)
  H.J. Res. 131, designating December 7 of each year as ``National 
Pearl Harbor Remembrance Day''. Signed August 23, 1994. (P.L. 103-308)
  H.J. Res. 175, designating October 1993 and October 1994 as 
``Italian-American Heritage and Culture Month''. Signed August 23, 
1994. (P.L. 103-309)
```
