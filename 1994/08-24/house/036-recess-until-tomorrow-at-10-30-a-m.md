# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 10:30 A.M.

```
[Congressional Record: August 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                  RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 10:30 A.M.

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, if there be no further business to come 
before the Senate today, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand 
in recess as previously ordered.

Thursday, August 25, 1994, at 10:30 a.m.
```
