# THANKING THE VICE PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME], Dole, Robert J. (D000401) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S.Res. 291

```
[Congressional Record: December 1, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                      THANKING THE VICE PRESIDENT

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I send a resolution to the desk, and I 
ask for its immediate consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 291) tendering the thanks of the 
     Senate to the Vice President for the courteous, dignified and 
     impartial manner in which he has presided over the 
     deliberations of the Senate.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to the immediate 
consideration of the resolution?
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the resolution.
  The resolution (S. Res. 291) was agreed to, as follows:

                              S. Res. 291

       Resolved, That the thanks of the Senate are hereby tendered 
     to the Honorable Al Gore, Vice President of the United States 
     and President of the Senate, for the courteous, dignified, 
     and impartial manner in which he has presided over its 
     deliberations during the second session of the One Hundred 
     Third Congress.

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I move to reconsider the vote by which 
the resolution was agreed to.
  Mr. DOLE. I move to lay that motion on the table.
  The motion to lay on the table was agreed to.
```
