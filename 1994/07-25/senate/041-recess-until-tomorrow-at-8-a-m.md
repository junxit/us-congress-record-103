# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 8 A.M.

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
[Congressional Record: July 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 8 A.M.

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, if there be no further business to come 
before the Senate, I move, in accordance with the order previously 
entered, that the Senate stand in recess until the hour of 8 o'clock 
tomorrow morning.
  The motion was agreed to, and the Senate, at 6:41 p.m., recessed 
until Tuesday, July 26, 1994, at 8 a.m.
```
