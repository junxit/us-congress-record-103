# HOPING FOR A SPEEDY RECOVERY

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
[Congressional Record: July 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                      HOPING FOR A SPEEDY RECOVERY

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I was advised a little while ago by the 
Sergeant at Arms that a young man collapsed in the visitors' gallery 
this morning and that his name was Carlos Worley. The Sergeant at Arms 
told me that he is a 19-year-old Senate security aide. He was taken to 
George Washington University Hospital for evaluation, and initial 
indications seem to be that he suffered from either a collapsed lung or 
a blood clot in his lung.
  I know that Senators hope that the young man will enjoy a speedy 
recovery and that this matter is not life-threatening.
```
