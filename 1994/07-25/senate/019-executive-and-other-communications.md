                   EXECUTIVE AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS

  The following communications were laid before the Senate, together 
with accompanying papers, reports, and documents, which were referred 
as indicated:

       EC-3100. A communication from the Chairman of the Board of 
     Directors of the Panama Canal Commission, transmitting, a 
     draft of proposed legislation to amend the Panama Canal Act 
     of 1979 to reconstitute the Panama Canal Commission as a 
     United States Government corporation, and for other purposes; 
     to the Committee on Armed Services.
       EC-3101. A communication for the Assistant Secretary of 
     Defense, Economic Security, transmitting, pursuant to law, a 
     report relative to the standardization of equipment with NATO 
     members; to the Committee on Armed Services.
