# AUTHORITY TO APPOINT A COMMITTEE OF ESCORT

**Speaking:** Byrd, Robert C. (B001210) [D-WV]

```
[Congressional Record: July 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
               AUTHORITY TO APPOINT A COMMITTEE OF ESCORT

  Mr. BYRD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the President 
of the Senate be authorized to appoint a committee on the part of the 
Senate to join with a like committee on the part of the House of 
Representatives to escort His Majesty Hussein I, King of Jordan, and 
His Excellency, Yitzhak Rabin, Prime Minister of Israel, into the House 
Chamber for the joint meeting tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
