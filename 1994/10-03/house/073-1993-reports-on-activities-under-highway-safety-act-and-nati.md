# 1993 REPORTS ON ACTIVITIES UNDER HIGHWAY SAFETY ACT AND NATIONAL TRAFFIC MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY ACT OF 1966--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

```
[Congressional Record: October 3, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
   1993 REPORTS ON ACTIVITIES UNDER HIGHWAY SAFETY ACT AND NATIONAL 
TRAFFIC MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY ACT OF 1966--MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT OF 
                           THE UNITED STATES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following message 
from the President of the United States; which was read and, together 
with the accompanying papers, without objection, referred to the 
Committee on Public Works and Transportation, and the Committee on 
Energy and Commerce:

To the Congress of the United States:
  I transmit herewith the 1993 calendar year reports as prepared by the 
Department of Transportation on activities under the Highway Safety Act 
and the National Traffic Motor Vehicle Safety Act of 1966, as amended 
(23 U.S.C. 401 note and 15 U.S.C. 1408).
                                                  William J. Clinton.  
  The White House, October 3, 1994.
```
