# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
[Congressional Record: October 3, 1994]

                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. de la Garza). Pursuant to the provisions 
of clause 5 of rule I the Chair announces that he will reduce to a

electronic device may be taken on each additional motion to suspend the
```
