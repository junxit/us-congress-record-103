# CLOTURE MOTION

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the hour 
of 6 p.m. having arrived, the clerk will report the motion to invoke 
cloture.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close the debate on the nomination 
     of Ricki Rhodarmer Tigert to be a member of the Board of 
     Directors of the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation:
         Byron L. Dorgan, J. Lieberman, Patty Murray, Wendell 
           Ford, Daniel Patrick Moynihan, Pat Leahy, George 
           Mitchell, Paul Sarbanes, Harry Reid, Don Riegle, Harlan 
           Mathews, John F. Kerry, Frank R. Lautenberg, John 
           Glenn, Dennis DeConcini, Christopher Dodd.

                          ____________________
```
