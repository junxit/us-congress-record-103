# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I thank my colleagues for their 
cooperation, beginning, of course, with the distinguished Republican 
leader and all of those Senators who have an interest in both of these 
matters.
  Pursuant to the agreement just approved, the Senate will vote at 10 
a.m. tomorrow on the nomination of Ricki Tigert to be a Member of the 
FDIC. If the nomination is confirmed, the Senate will approve, pursuant 
to this order, the nominations of Ricki Tigert to be Chairperson, 
Andrew Hove to be a Member and Vice Chairperson of the FDIC; and then, 
immediately following that, the Senate will vote on cloture on the 
nomination of H. Lee Sarokin to be a U. S. circuit judge.
  In light of this agreement, there will be no further rollcall votes 
this evening.
  There will be two votes tomorrow morning, the first to occur at 10 
a.m. and the second to occur shortly after completion of the first 
vote.

                          ____________________
```
