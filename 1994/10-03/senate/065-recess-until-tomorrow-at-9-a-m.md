# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand 
in recess, as previously ordered.
```
