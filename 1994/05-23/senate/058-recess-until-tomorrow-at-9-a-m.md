# RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: May 23, 1994]

                    RECESS UNTIL TOMORROW AT 9 A.M.

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate today, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate stand
```
