# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** S. 2087

```
[Congressional Record: May 23, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS


                          ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  Under the authority of the order of January 5, 1993, the Secretary of 
the Senate on May 20, 1994, during the recess of the Senate received a 
message from the House of Representatives announcing that the Speaker 
has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 2087. An act to extend the time period for compliance 
     with the Nutrition Labeling and Education Act of 1990 for 
     certain food products packaged prior to August 8, 1994.

  Under the authority of the order of January 5, 1993, the enrolled 
bill was signed on May 20, 1994, during the recess of the Senate, by 
the President pro tempore [Mr. Byrd].
```
