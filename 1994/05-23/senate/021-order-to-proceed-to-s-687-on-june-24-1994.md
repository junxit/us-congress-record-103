# ORDER TO PROCEED TO S. 687 ON JUNE 24, 1994

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** S. 687

```
[Congressional Record: May 23, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
              ORDER TO PROCEED TO S. 687 ON JUNE 24, 1994

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that no later

the product liability fairness bill.
```
