REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING CERTAIN POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST H.R. 4453, 
           MILITARY CONSTRUCTION APPROPRIATIONS BILL FOR 1995

  Mr. FROST, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 103-523), on the resolution (H. Res. 433) waiving certain 
points of order against the bill (H.R. 4453) making appropriations for 
military construction for the Department of Defense for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1995, and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
