# THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE UKRAINIAN NATIONAL ASSOCIATION

**Speaking:** Moynihan, Daniel Patrick (M001054) [D-NY]

```
[Congressional Record: February 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
      THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE UKRAINIAN NATIONAL ASSOCIATION

● Mr. MOYNIHAN. Mr. President, I understand that this month 
marks the 100th anniversary of the Ukrainian National Association and I 
am pleased to extend my warmest congratulations on this milestone.
  During those 100 years the people of Ukraine suffered unimaginable 
deprivations. And yet, throughout Ukraine's national nightmare its 
friends and supporters never wavered in their support of the Ukrainian 
people. Today, we rejoice in the independence of Ukraine and look 
forward to ever stronger cultural, social, and political relations 
between Ukraine and the United States.●
```
