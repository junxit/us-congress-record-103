# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: February 28, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Teach us, O God, how we can know and experience the meaning of 
covenant, a covenant with you, our Creator, and a covenant with those 
about us. May we be open to the spirit that nurtures and forgives and 
supports us in all we do, with all our hopes and desires. And may we 
grow in our covenant with each other, to bless each other in our needs, 
to learn to respect each other and to truly live, to experience the 
joys of life that are greater than ever we could ask or imagine. Bless 
us this day and every day, we pray. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
