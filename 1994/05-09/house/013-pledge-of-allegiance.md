```

                          PLEDGE OF ALLEGIANCE

  The SPEAKER. The Chair will ask the gentleman from Nebraska [Mr. 
Bereuter] if he would kindly come forward and lead the membership in 
the Pledge of Allegiance.

       I pledge allegiance to the Flag of the <gr-thn-eq>United

     one nation under God, indivisible, with liberty and justice
```
