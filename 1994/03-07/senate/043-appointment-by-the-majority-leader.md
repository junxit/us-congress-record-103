```
[Congressional Record: March 7, 1994]

                   APPOINTMENT BY THE MAJORITY LEADER

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Feinstein). The Chair, on behalf of the 
majority leader of the Senate and the Speaker of the House, pursuant to 
Public Law 102-166, appoints the following individual as a member of 
the Glass Ceiling Commission:
  Mr. John Jenkins, of Maine, vice Marion O. Sandler, resigned.

The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
