# REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3527

**Speaking:** Hefner, W. G. (Bill) (H000448) [D-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 3527, H.R. 3537

```
[Congressional Record: February 9, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
          REMOVAL OF NAME OF MEMBER AS COSPONSOR OF H.R. 3527

  Mr. HEFNER. Mr. Speaker, on January 25, 1994, my name was added by 
mistake to H.R. 3537, the Assault Weapons ban introduced by Congressman 
Schumer. This was a clerical error, and I hereby ask unanimous consent 
that my name be removed immediately.
  I do not support the proposed weapons ban and I would never cosponsor 
a bill of this nature.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
