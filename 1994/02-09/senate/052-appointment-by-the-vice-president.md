# APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

```
[Congressional Record: February 9, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   APPOINTMENT BY THE VICE PRESIDENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to the order of the Senate of January 24, 1901, appoints the 
Senator from Illinois, Ms. Moseley-Braun, to read Washington's Farewell 
Address on Tuesday, February 22, 1994.
  Mr. LEVIN. I cannot think of a better choice, Madam President.
```
