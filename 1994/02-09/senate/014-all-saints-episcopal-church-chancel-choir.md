# ALL SAINTS EPISCOPAL CHURCH CHANCEL CHOIR

**Speaking:** Heflin, Howell (H000445) [D-AL]

```
[Congressional Record: February 9, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
               ALL SAINTS EPISCOPAL CHURCH CHANCEL CHOIR

  Mr. HEFLIN. Madam President, the All Saints Episcopal Church Chancel 
Choir of Homewood, AL, a suburb of Birmingham, performed a 25-minute a 
capella choral prelude last fall at the Washington National Cathedral 
of Saint Peter and Saint Paul. Their stirring performance came on the 
first Sunday of Advent, November 28, which was also Alabama Day at the 
cathedral. The choir's prelude came before the 11 a.m. Eucharist.
  I am proud of this dedicated group of singers, and am pleased to 
extend my congratulations to them for having had the opportunity to 
perform at the Washington National Cathedral. Members of the choir are:
  Pat King, Katy Smith, Cindi Eastwood, Mike Morris, Rick Vise, Fran 
Robins, Jane Pochran, Roseanne Croft, Jerry Smith, Gail Smith, Newell 
Baker, Carol Baker, Ron May, Mike Bradley, Mike Kellum, Kay Kellum, 
Stuart Richeson, Paula Richeson, Buddy Gaines, and Billy Hecker.
  Anita Dickerson, Virginia Beckworth, Susan Dean, Nan Wingo, Mary Lyle 
McGuire, Martha Miller, Carol Hudson, Judi Nelson, Jan Gentry, Cindy 
Haddock, Chris Kelly, Bill Braden, B.J. Braden, Terry Martin, Bill 
Funderburk, Elise Keddie, Cassandra Lewis, Paul Foreman, Lynn 
Hendricks, and Evan Thayer.
```
