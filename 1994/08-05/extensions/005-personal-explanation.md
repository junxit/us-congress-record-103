# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Goodling, William F. (G000291) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4217

```
[Congressional Record: August 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                        HON. WILLIAM F. GOODLING

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 5, 1994

  Mr. GOODLING. Mr. Speaker, I regret my absence for rollcall votes No. 
377 and No. 378, amending H.R. 4217, the Federal Crop Insurance Reform 
Act. I was attending the funeral services for a family member.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``nay'' and rollcall vote No. 
377 and ``aye'' on rollcall vote No. 378.
```
