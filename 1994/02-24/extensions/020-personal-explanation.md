# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Talent, Jim (T000024) [R-MO]

**Measures:** H.R. 811

```
[Congressional Record: February 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                          HON. JAMES M. TALENT

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 24, 1994

  Mr. TALENT. Mr. Speaker, it has come to my attention that, although I 
was present at the time of the vote on final passage of H.R. 811, the 
Independent Counsel Reauthorization Act of 1993, I failed to vote. The 
Record should show that, because this bill did not cover the Congress, 
I would have voted ``no'' on final passage.
```
