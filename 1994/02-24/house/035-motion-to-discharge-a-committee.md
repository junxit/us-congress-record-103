                    MOTION TO DISCHARGE A COMMITTEE

                                                   February 24, 1994.  
To the Clerk of the House of Representatives:
  Pursuant to clause 4, rule XXVII, I, Charles W. Stenholm, move to 
discharge the Committee on Rules from the consideration of the 
resolution (H. Res. 331) providing for the consideration of the joint 
resolution (H.J. Res. 103) proposing an amendment to the Constitution 
to provide for a balanced budget for the United States Government and 
for greater accountability in the enactment of tax legislation, which 
was referred to said committee January 25, 1994, in support of which
