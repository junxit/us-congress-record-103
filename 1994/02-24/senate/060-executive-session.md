                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. PELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nominations: 
Calendar Order No. 698, Calendar Order No. 699, Calendar Order No. 700, 
Calendar Order Number 701, Calendar Order No. 702, Calendar Order No. 
704, and Calendar Order No. 705.

confirmed en bloc; that any statements appear in the Record as if read; 
that, upon confirmation, the motions to reconsider be laid upon the 
table en bloc; and that the President be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action.
