# RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 20, 1994, AT 2 P.M.

```
             RECESS UNTIL MONDAY, JUNE 20, 1994, AT 2 P.M.

  So, Mr. President, if there is no further business to come before the 
Senate today, and I see no other Senator seeking recognition, I now ask 
unanimous consent that the Senate stand in recess as previously 
ordered.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 11:23 p.m., recessed until 
Monday, June, 20, 1994, at 2 p.m.

                          ____________________
```
