# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. FORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of Calendar order Nos. 440, 441, 
446, 448, and 449, en bloc; that the committee substitute amendments 
and committee amendments, where appropriate, be agreed to en bloc; that 
the several bills each be deemed read a third time and passed; that the 
motion to reconsider the passage of these items be laid upon the table, 
en bloc; that amendments to the title, where appropriate, be agreed to; 
that the consideration of each bill be included separately in the 
Record; and that statements with respect to the passage of each bill be 
printed in the Record, where appropriate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
