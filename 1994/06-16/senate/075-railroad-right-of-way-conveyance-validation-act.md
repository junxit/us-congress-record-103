# RAILROAD RIGHT-OF-WAY CONVEYANCE VALIDATION ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 1183

```
            RAILROAD RIGHT-OF-WAY CONVEYANCE VALIDATION ACT

  The bill (H.R. 1183) to validate conveyances of certain lands in the 
State of California that form part of the right-of-way granted by the 
United States to the Central Pacific Railway Company, was considered, 
ordered to a third reading, read the third time, and passed.
```
