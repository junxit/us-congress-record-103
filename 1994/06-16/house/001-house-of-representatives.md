House of Representatives

  The House met at 10 a.m.

                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend Dr. Donald H. Roberts, pastor, United Methodist Church, 
Arlington, VA, offered the following prayer:
  Let justice roll down like waters, and righteousness like an 
everflowing stream.--Amos 5:24.
  O God, in whom we trust, remind our hearts of that which You require 
of us: ``To do justly, and to love mercy, and to walk humbly,'' with 
our God.
  With Your divine presence alive within us, grant us, we pray, the 
wisdom and the courage to serve You and Your people honorably and 
faithfully and well, toward the end that Thy will be done. Amen.
