# UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 540

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 540

```
[Congressional Record: April 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                  UNANIMOUS-CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 540

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I thank all my colleagues. I have one more 
unanimous consent agreement to offer.
  I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate considers S. 540, the 
bankruptcy reform bill, that Senator McCain be recognized to offer the 
first floor amendment, providing that he is on the floor and ready with 
his amendment at that time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
