# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

**Measures:** H.R. 6, S. 2004

```
[Congressional Record: April 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
             MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING RECESS

  Under the provisions of the order of January 5, 1993, the Secretary 
of the Senate on April 15, 1994, during the recess of the Senate, 
received a message from the House of Representatives announcing that 
the House has passed the following bill in which it requests the 
concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 6. An act to extend for five years the authorizations 
     of appropriations for the programs under the Elementary and 
     Secondary Education Act of 1965, and for certain other 
     purposes.


                         enrolled bills signed

  The message also announced that Speaker has signed the following 
enrolled bill:

       S. 2004. An act to extend until July 1, 1998, the exemption 
     from ineligibility based on a high default rate for certain 
     institutions of higher education.
```
