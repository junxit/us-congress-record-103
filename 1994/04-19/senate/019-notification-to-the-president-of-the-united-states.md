# NOTIFICATION TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME], Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 203

```
[Congressional Record: April 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
           NOTIFICATION TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. MITCHELL. Madam President, I send a resolution to the desk and 
ask that it be stated and immediately considered.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 203) notifying the President of the 
     United States of the election of a Sergeant at Arms and 
     Doorkeeper of the Senate.

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection to the immediate 
consideration of the resolution?
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on agreeing to the resolution.
  The resolution (S. Res. 203) was agreed to, as follows:

       Resolved, That the President of the United States be 
     notified of the election of the Honorable Robert Laurent 
     Benoit as Sergeant at Arms and Doorkeeper of the Senate.

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I move to reconsider the vote.
  Mr. FORD. I move to lay that motion on the table.
  The motion to lay on the table was agreed to.
```
