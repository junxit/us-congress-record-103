# MEASURE INDEFINITELY POSTPONED--S. 1182

**Speaking:** Metzenbaum, Howard M. (M000678) [D-OH], Pell, Claiborne (P000193) [D-RI]

**Measures:** H.R. 233, S. 1182

```
[Congressional Record: May 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                MEASURE INDEFINITELY POSTPONED--S. 1182

  Mr. METZENBAUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Calendar No. 273, S. 1182, the Arms Control and Disarmament Act, be 
indefinitely postponed.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. METZENBAUM. Mr. President, I might say that the provision of that 
bill was included in H.R. 233, the State Department Authorization Act.
  Mr. President, I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. PELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
