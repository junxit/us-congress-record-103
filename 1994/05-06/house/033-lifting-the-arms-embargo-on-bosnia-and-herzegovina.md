# LIFTING THE ARMS EMBARGO ON BOSNIA AND HERZEGOVINA

**Speaking:** Baucus, Max (B000243) [D-MT], Metzenbaum, Howard M. (M000678) [D-OH]

**Measures:** S. 798, S. 2042

```
[Congressional Record: May 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
           LIFTING THE ARMS EMBARGO ON BOSNIA AND HERZEGOVINA

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, upon completion of 
all debate on the amendments that have just been handed to S. 798, the 
Senate will now proceed to S. 2042, the Bosnian arms embargo bill, for 
debate only. The clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2042) to remove the United States arms embargo 
     of the Government of Bosnia and Herzegovina.

  The Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. BAUCUS. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. METZENBAUM. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
   The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
