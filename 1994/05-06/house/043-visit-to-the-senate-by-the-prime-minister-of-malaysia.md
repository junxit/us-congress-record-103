# VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRIME MINISTER OF MALAYSIA

**Speaking:** Cohen, William S. (C000598) [R-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: May 6, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
         VISIT TO THE SENATE BY THE PRIME MINISTER OF MALAYSIA

  Mr. COHEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that we stand in 
recess for 1 minute. However, before doing so, I would like to take 
this occasion to announce that we have the privilege of having the 
Prime Minister of Malaysia, Mr. Mahathir, who is visiting our country 
and will be going to the White House shortly to visit President 
Clinton.
  We want to take this opportunity to welcome him to the U.S. Senate 
and say how much we appreciate our relationship with Malaysia. It is a 
strong one, both economically and militarily and, hopefully, we will 
build upon that relationship even more so in the future. So we welcome 
you to our Senate.
  [Applause.]
```
