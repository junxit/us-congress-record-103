**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

```
[Congressional Record: July 19, 1994]

                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. FORD. Now, Mr. President, if there be no further business to come
```
