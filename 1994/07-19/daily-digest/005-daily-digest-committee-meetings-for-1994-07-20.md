                         COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR

                        WEDNESDAY, JULY 20, 1994

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Appropriations, Subcommittee on Labor, Health and 
Human Services, and Education, business meeting, to mark up H.R. 
4606, making appropriations for the Departments of Labor, Health and 
Human Services, and Education, and related agencies, for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 1995, 9:30 a.m., SD-192.
  Full Committee, business meeting, to mark up H.R. 4606, making 
appropriations for the Departments of Labor, Health and Human 
Services, and Education, and related agencies, for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 1995, 2 p.m., S-128, Capitol.
  Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs, to hold hearings 
to review the Federal Reserve's semi-annual monetary policy report, 
10 a.m., SD-538.
  Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, business meeting, to 
consider pending calendar business, 9:30 a.m., SD-366.
  Committee on Environment and Public Works, to hold hearings to 
review the Administration's Floodplain Management Task Force report 
on the 1993 Midwest floods, and to examine the current flood 
situation in Georgia, 2 p.m., SD-406.
  Committee on Governmental Affairs, Subcommittee on Federal 
Services, Post Office, and Civil Service, to hold hearings to 
examine the Federal role in child support enforcement, 9:30 a.m., 
SD-342.


                                 House

  Committee on Agriculture, to consider H.R. 4217, Federal Crop 
Insurance Reform Act of 1994, 10 a.m., 1300 Longworth.
  Committee on Armed Services, Subcommittee on Military Forces and 
Personnel, to mark up legislation relating to the role of the 
Department of Defense in health-care reform, 4 p.m., 2212 Rayburn.
  Committee on Education and Labor, Subcommittee on Elementary, 
Secondary, and Vocational Educational, oversight hearing on school 
violence, 10 a.m., 2175 Rayburn.
  Committee on Foreign Affairs, Subcommittee on Western Hemisphere 
and the Subcommittee on Economic Policy, Trade and Environment, 
joint hearing on the Summit of the Americas, Part I: ``Prospects for 
Trade within the Hemisphere,'' 10:30 a.m., 2172 Rayburn.
  Committee on Natural Resources, to consider the following bills: 
H.R. 359, to improve the administration of the Women's Rights 
National Historical Park in the State of New York; H.R. 4448, to 
amend the Act establishing Lowell National Historical Park; H.R. 
3898, to establish the New Bedford Whaling National Historical Park 
in New Bedford, Massachusetts; H.R. 4158, to establish the Lower 
East Side Tenement Museum National Historic Site; H.R. 4228, to 
extend Federal recognition to the United Auburn Indian Community of 
the Auburn Rancheria of California; and H.R. 3600, Health Security 
Act, 9:45 a.m., 1324 Longworth.
  Committee on Public Works and Transportation, Subcommittee on 
Surface Transportation, hearing on Legislation to Preempt State 
Motor Carrier Regulations Pertaining to Rates, Routes and Services, 
10 a.m., 2167 Rayburn.
  Committee on Rules, to consider the following: H.R. 3838, Housing 
and Community Development Act of 1994; and H.R. 3870, Environmental 
Technologies Act of 1994, 1 p.m., H-313 Capitol.
  Committee on Science, Space, and Technology, to mark up the 
following bills: H.R. 4306, Risk Assessment Improvement Act of 1994; 
H.R. 4489, National Aeronautics and Space Administration 
Authorization Act, Fiscal Years 1995 and 1996; and H.R. 4008, 
National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration Authorization Act of 
1994, 10 a.m., 2318 Rayburn.
  Committee on Small Business, hearing on Key Issues Confronting 
Minor League Baseball, 10 a.m., 2359 Rayburn.
  Committee on Veterans' Affairs, Subcommittee on Oversight and 
Investigations, hearing on ``VA Health Care and Communication and 
Information Technologies,'' 8:30 a.m., 334 Cannon.
  Committee on Ways and Means, to continue consideration of draft 
Uruguay Round implementing proposals, 10 a.m., 1100 Longworth.


                             Joint Meetings

  Conferees, on H.R. 2243, to amend the Federal Trade Commission Act 
to extend the authorization of appropriations in such Act, 11:30 
a.m., S-146, Capitol.
