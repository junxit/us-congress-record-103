# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: July 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to the order of the House of February 11, 1994, 
and June 10, 1994, the Chair will now recognize Members from lists 
submitted by the majority and minority leaders for morning hour 
debates.
  The Chair will alternate recognition between the parties, with each 
party limited to 30 minutes, and each Member except the majority and 
minority leaders limited to 5 minutes.
  The Chair recognizes the gentleman from Colorado [Mr. Hefley] for 5 
minutes.
```
