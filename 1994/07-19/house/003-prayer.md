# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: July 19, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  As the days go on with all the needs that must be met, remind us, O 
gracious God, not only of the world of action and duty, but also to see 
more clearly the reality of the spiritual and the holy, the place of 
gratitude and thanksgiving, the realm of faith and hope and love. Guide 
us, O God, in the things of the spirit, that we will truly be the 
people You would have us be. In Your name, we pray. Amen.
```
