# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Crane, Philip M. (C000873) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2947, H.R. 4867

```
[Congressional Record: August 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______


                          HON. PHILIP M. CRANE

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, August 17, 1994

  Mr. CRANE. Mr. Speaker, yesterday I was unavoidably detained and 
therefore was unable to cast my vote on two of the three recorded 
votes. I failed to vote on rollcall No. 378 relating to H.R. 2947, 
legislation relating to the Black Revolutionary War Patriots 
Foundation, and rollcall No. 379 relating to H.R. 4867, the High Speed 
Rail Development Act. I had intended to cast my vote on the legislation 
in question; however, my flight from Chicago to Washington was 
unavoidably detained due to weather. Fortunately, the outcome of the 
votes on H.R. 2947 and H.R. 4867 were not decided by a single vote and 
my vote therefore would not have been determinative.
```
