# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: August 17, 1994]

                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there will now 
be a period for the transaction of morning business not to extend 
beyond the hour of 10 o'clock a.m., with Senators permitted to speak 
therein for not to exceed 5 minutes each.
```
