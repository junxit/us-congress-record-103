# THOMAS F. EAGLETON U.S. COURTHOUSE

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY], Daschle, Thomas A. (D000064) [D-SD]

**Measures:** H.R. 4790

```
[Congressional Record: August 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                   THOMAS F. EAGLETON U.S. COURTHOUSE

  A bill (H.R. 4790) to designate the U.S. courthouse under 
construction in St. Louis, MO, as the ``Thomas F. Eagleton United 
States Courthouse,'' was considered, ordered to a third reading, read 
the third time and passed.
  Mr. FORD. I thank the Chair, I thank my colleague and I suggest the 
absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DASCHLE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
