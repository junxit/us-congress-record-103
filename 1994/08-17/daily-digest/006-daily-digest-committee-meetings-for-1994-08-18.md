# Daily Digest/COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR 1994-08-18

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 215, S. 1329, S. 1697, H.R. 3800, H.R. 4624, H.R. 4683, H.R. 4779

```
[Congressional Record: August 17, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

                            AUGUST 18, 1994

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Armed Services, to resume hearings to examine the 
military implications of the Chemical Weapons Convention (Treaty 
Doc. 103-21), 9:30 a.m., SR-222.
  Committee on Foreign Relations, business meeting, to mark up S. 
1329, to provide an investigation of the whereabouts of the U.S. 
citizens and others who have been missing from Cyprus since 1974, 
and to consider H. Con. Res. 215, honoring James Norman Hall and 
recognizing his outstanding contributions to the U.S. and the South 
Pacific, and pending nominations, 10 a.m., SD-419.
  Committee on Governmental Affairs, business meeting, to mark up S. 
1697, to improve the ability of the Federal Government to prepare 
for and respond to major disasters, 10 a.m., SD-342.
  Committee on the Judiciary, to hold hearings on the nomination of 
Lois Jane Schiffer, of the District of Columbia, to be an Assistant 
Attorney General, Department of Justice, 9:30 a.m., SD-226.
  Full Committee, to hold hearings on the nominations of Nancy E. 
Gist, of Massachusetts, to be Director of the Bureau of Justice 
Assistance; Laurie O. Robinson, of the District of Columbia, to be 
Assistant Attorney General for the Office of Justice Programs; Jan 
M. Chaiken, of Massachusetts, to be Director of the Bureau of 
Justice Statistics; and Jeremy Travis, of New York, to be Director 
of the National Institute of Justice, all of the Department of 
Justice, 10 a.m., SD-628.

                                 NOTICE

  For a listing of Senate Committee Meetings scheduled ahead, see page 
E1759 in today's Record.


                                 House

  Committee on Energy and Commerce, to mark up the following bills: 
H.R. 4779, State and Local Government Interstate Waste Control Act; 
and H.R. 4683, Flow Control Act of 1994, 10 a.m., 2123 Rayburn.
  Committee on Ways and Means, to mark up H.R. 3800, Superfund 
Reform Act of 1994, 4 p.m., 1100 Longworth.


                             Joint Meetings

  Conferees, on H.R. 4624, making appropriations for the Departments 
of Veterans Affairs and Housing and Urban Development, and 
independent agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 1995, 
time and room to be announced.
```
