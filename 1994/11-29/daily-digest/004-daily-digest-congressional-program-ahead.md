# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** H.R. 5110

```
[Congressional Record: November 29, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of November 30 through December 2, 1994

                             Senate Chamber

  On Wednesday and Thursday, Senate will consider H.R. 5110, GATT 
implementing legislation, with a vote to occur on Thursday.
  On Friday, Senate will not be in session.


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on Foreign Relations: December 1, Subcommittee on East 
Asian and Pacific Affairs, to hold hearings to examine implications 
of the United States-North Korea nuclear agreement, 9 a.m., SD-419.
  Committee on Governmental Affairs: November 30, Subcommittee on 
Federal Services, Post Office, and Civil Service, to hold hearings 
to review labor management relations at the U.S. Postal Service, 
9:30 a.m., SD-342.


                            House Committees

  Committee on Education and Labor, November 30, Subcommittee on 
Labor-Management Relations and the Subcommittee on Regulation, 
Business Opportunities, and Technology of the Committee on Small 
Business, joint hearing on health care, 9 a.m., 2175 Rayburn.
```
