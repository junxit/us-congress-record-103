# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: March 23, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  O gracious God, from whom comes every good gift, we ask that Your 
Spirit be with all those who turn to You for Your blessing. Where there 
is illness, may Your healing power be present; where there is timidity, 
may resolve and courage be our power; where there is haughtiness, give 
us humility; and where there is any apprehension or anxiety, give us a 
faith that sees us through. O loving and wondrous God, be present with 
us and support us all the day long. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
