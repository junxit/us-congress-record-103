# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: July 20, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  We pray, O God, that our thoughts will be born of a good spirit, that 
our words will have the ring of truth, our deeds will show forth with 
courage, our promises will be made with integrity, our motivations will 
be cleansed by honor, our commitments will be fulfilled and true, and 
all our aspirations will be marked by strength of character. O gracious 
God, You have given to each person the marks of humanity and civility, 
so bless us and keep us in Your truth, now and evermore. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
