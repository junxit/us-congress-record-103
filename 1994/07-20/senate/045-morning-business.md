# MORNING BUSINESS

```
[Congressional Record: July 20, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Also under the previous order, 
there will now be a period for the transaction of morning business not 
to extend beyond the hour of 9:30 a.m., with Senators permitted to 
speak therein for not to exceed 5 minutes each.
  The Senator from Alabama [Mr. Heflin] is recognized to speak for up 
to 10 minutes.
  The Senator from Texas [Mr. Gramm] is recognized to speak for up to 
15 minutes.
```
