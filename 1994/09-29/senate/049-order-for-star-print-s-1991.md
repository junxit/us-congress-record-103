# ORDER FOR STAR PRINT--S. 1991

**Speaking:** Kohl, Herb (K000305) [D-WI], Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

**Measures:** S. 1991

```
[Congressional Record: September 29, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                     ORDER FOR STAR PRINT--S. 1991

  Mr. KOHL. Mr. President, I now send to the desk and ask unanimous 
consent that a star print be made of S. 1991, the Professional Boxing 
Safety Act, in order to correct an inadvertent omission when the Senate 
Commerce Committee reported this matter on September 28.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. KOHL. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The absence of a quorum having been suggested, 
the clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
