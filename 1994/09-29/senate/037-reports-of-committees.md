                         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

     Senate concerning District Council elections in Hong Kong on

     Senate concerning U.S. relations with Taiwan.
       By Mr. KENNEDY, from the Committee on Labor and Human

     and Mental Health Services Administration, and for other 
     purposes.
       By Mr. PELL, from the Committee on Foreign Relations, 
     without amendment:
       S. 2475. An original bill to authorize assistance to
