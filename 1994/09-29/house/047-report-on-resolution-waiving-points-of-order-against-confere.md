# REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT TO ACCOMPANY H.R. 6, IMPROVING AMERICA'S SCHOOLS ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.R. 6, H.Res. 556

```
[Congressional Record: September 29, 1994]

REPORT ON RESOLUTION WAIVING POINTS OF ORDER AGAINST CONFERENCE REPORT 
      TO ACCOMPANY H.R. 6, IMPROVING AMERICA'S SCHOOLS ACT OF 1994

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-767) on the resolution (H. Res. 556) waiving 
points of order against the conference report to accompany the bill 
(H.R. 6) to extend for 6 years the authorizations of appropriations for 
the programs under the Elementary and Secondary Education Act of 1965, 
for certain other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
