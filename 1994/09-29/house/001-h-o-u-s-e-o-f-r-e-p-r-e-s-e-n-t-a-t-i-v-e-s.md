             H O U S E  O F  R E P R E S E N T A T I V E S


Vol. 140


WASHINGTON, THURSDAY, SEPTEMBER 29, 1994

No. 139


House of Representatives

  The House met at 10 a.m.
  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  When we see the opportunities of the new day, O God, may we remember 
the beauty of Your creation in all the world; when we see people become 
whole and experience the fullness of life, may we give thanks for 
healing in body, mind, and spirit; when we see anger turn to kindness 
and estrangement turn to conciliation, may we express our gratitude to 
You, O God, for Your grace and Your mercy and Your peace. In Your name, 
we pray. Amen.
