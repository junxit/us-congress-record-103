# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Collins, Cardiss (C000634) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 4779

```
[Congressional Record: September 29, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mrs. COLLINS of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, yesterday, on the final vote 
for passage of H.R. 4779, the State and Local Government Waste Control 
Act, I was misrecorded as having voted ``aye'' for the bill. I ask to 
have this statement placed in the Congressional Record after rollcall 
vote 443 so that it is clear that I oppose H.R. 4779 and wish to be 
recorded as voting against the bill.

                          ____________________
```
