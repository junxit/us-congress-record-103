# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 1758, S. 1904, H.R. 2559, H.R. 3724, H.R. 4568, H.R. 4581, H.R. 4635

```
[Congressional Record: July 11, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest p. D784)
  S. 1904, to amend title 38, United States Code, to improve the 
organization and procedures of the Board of Veterans' Appeals. Signed 
July 1, 1994. (P.L. 103-271)
  H.R. 1758, to revise, codify, and enact without substantive change 
certain general and permanent laws, related to transportation, as 
subtitles II, III, and V-X of title 49, United States Code, 
``Transportation,'' and to make other technical improvements in the 
Code. Signed July 5, 1994. (P.L. 103-272)
  H.R. 2559, to designate the Federal building located at 601 East 12th 
Street in Kansas City, Missouri, as the ``Richard Bolling Federal 
Building and the United States Courthouse'' located at Ninth and Locust 
Streets, in Kansas City, Missouri, as the ``Charles Evans Whittaker 
United States Courthouse.'' Signed July 5, 1994. (P.L. 103-273)
  H.R. 3724, to designate the United States courthouse located in 
Bridgeport, Connecticut, as the ``Brien McMahon Federal Building.'' 
Signed July 5, 1994. (P.L. 103-274)
  H.R. 4568, making supplemental appropriations for the Department of 
Housing and Urban Development for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
1994. Signed July 5, 1994. (P.L. 103-275)
  H.R. 4581, to provide for the imposition of temporary fees in 
connection with the handling of complaints of violations of the 
Perishable Agriculture Commodities Act, 1930. Signed July 5, 1994. 
(P.L. 103-276)
  H.R. 4635, to extend the Export Administration Act of 1979. Signed 
July 5, 1994. (P.L. 103-277)
```
