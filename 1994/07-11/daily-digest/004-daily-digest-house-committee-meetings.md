# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
UNDERREPORTING FEDERAL INVOLVEMENT IN NEW TECHNOLOGIES DEVELOPED AT 
SCRIPPS RESEARCH INSTITUTE
Committee on Small Business: Subcommittee on Regulation, Business 
Opportunities, and Technology held a hearing on Underreporting Federal 
Involvement in New Technologies Developed at Scripps Research 
Institute. Testimony was heard from the following officials of the 
Department of Health and Human Services: Michael R. Hill, Assistant 
Inspector General; and Wendy Baldwin, Deputy Director, NIH; and public 
witnesses.

Joint Meetings
EMPLOYMENT-UNEMPLOYMENT
Joint Economic Committee: On Friday, July 8, Committee held hearings to 
examine the employment-unemployment situation for June, receiving 
testimony from Katharine G. Abraham, Commissioner, Bureau of Labor 
Statistics, Department of Labor.
  Hearings were recessed subject to call.
```
