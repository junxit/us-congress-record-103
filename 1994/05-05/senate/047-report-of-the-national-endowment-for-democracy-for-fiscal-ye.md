# REPORT OF THE NATIONAL ENDOWMENT FOR DEMOCRACY FOR FISCAL YEAR 1993-- MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 107

```
[Congressional Record: May 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
 REPORT OF THE NATIONAL ENDOWMENT FOR DEMOCRACY FOR FISCAL YEAR 1993--
                   MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT--PM 107

  The PRESIDING OFFICER laid before the Senate the following message 
from the President of the United States, together with an accompanying 
report; which was referred to the Committee on Foreign Relations.

To the Congress of the United States:
  Pursuant to the provisions of section 504(h) of Public Law 98-164, as 
amended (22 U.S.C. 4413(i)), I transmit herewith the 10th Annual Report 
of the National Endowment for Democracy, which covers fiscal year 1993.
                                                   William J. Clinton  
  The White House, May 5, 1994.

                          ____________________
```
