# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Long, Jill L. (L000420) [D-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4296

```
[Congressional Record: May 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Ms. LONG. Mr. Speaker, unfortunately, due to a death in my family, I 
was not able to cast my vote on H.R. 4296, the Assault Weapons Ban Act. 
I do, however, want to express my opposition to the bill. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``nay.''
```
