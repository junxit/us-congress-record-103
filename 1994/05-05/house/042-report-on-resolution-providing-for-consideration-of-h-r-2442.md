# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2442, ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1994

**Measures:** H.Res. 420, H.R. 2442

```
[Congressional Record: May 5, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2442, ECONOMIC 
                DEVELOPMENT REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 1994

  Mr. MOAKLEY, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 103-495) on the resolution (H. Res. 420) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2442) to reauthorize appropriations 
under the Public Works and Economic Development Act of 1965, as 
amended, to revise administrative provisions of the Act to improve the 
authority of the Secretary of Commerce to administer grant programs, 
and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
