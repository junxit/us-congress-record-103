# PRAYER

```
[Congressional Record: May 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Rev. James David Ford, D.D., offered the following 
prayer:
  Teach us always, O gracious God, to enjoy the glory of Your created 
world. In spite of all the challenges and difficulties that face people 
each day, may we pause to celebrate with awe and respect and amazement 
the miracle of life, the mystery and wonder of all creation, the 
magnificence and beauty of nature, the glory and grace and potential of 
every person. May our lives never become so crowded with the necessary 
details that we miss the opportunities to see more clearly the 
spiritual verities that are Your gift to us. This is our earnest 
prayer. Amen.
```
