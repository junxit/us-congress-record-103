# ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2019

```
[Congressional Record: May 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                          ORDERS FOR TOMORROW

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, on behalf of the majority leader, I ask 
unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its business today, it 
stand in recess until 9 a.m., Thursday, May 19; that following the 
prayer, the Journal of proceedings be deemed approved to date and the 
time for the two leaders reserved for their use later in the day; that 
there then be a period for morning business, not to extend beyond 10:30 
a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 5 minutes 
each; with the following Senators recognized for the time limits 
specified: Senator Lieberman for up to 5 minutes; Senators Kerry, 
Graham, and Bradley for up to 15 minutes each; that at 10:30 a.m., the 
Senate resume consideration of S. 2019 and vote on final passage, as 
provided for under the provisions of a previous consent agreement; 
further that following the rollcall vote at 10:30 a.m., that no other 
rollcall votes occur prior to 6:30 p.m., Thursday.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
