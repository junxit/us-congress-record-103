# TITLE AMENDMENT--S. 2087

**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2087

```
[Congressional Record: May 18, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                        TITLE AMENDMENT--S. 2087

  Mr. FORD. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of S. 2087, passed May 17, the title be amended to read as 
follows: ``An act to extend the time period for compliance with the 
Nutrition Labeling and Education Act of 1990 for certain food products 
packaged prior to August 8, 1994.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
