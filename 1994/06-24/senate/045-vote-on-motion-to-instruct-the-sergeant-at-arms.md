**Speaking:** Ford, Wendell H. (F000268) [D-KY], Simpson, Alan K. (S000429) [R-WY]

```

            VOTE ON MOTION TO INSTRUCT THE SERGEANT AT ARMS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now vote on the motion to instruct the Sergeant at Arms to 
request the presence of absent Senators.
  The yeas and nays have been ordered. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. FORD. I announce that the Senator from Arizona [Mr. DeConcini], 
the Senator from Ohio [Mr. Glenn], and the Senator from Hawaii [Mr. 
Inouye] are necessarily absent.
  Mr. SIMPSON. I announce that the Senator from Missouri [Mr. Bond], 
the Senator from Idaho [Mr. Craig], the Senator from Texas [Mr. Gramm], 
the Senator from Vermont [Mr. Jeffords], the Senator from Oklahoma [Mr. 
Nickles], and the Senator from Wyoming [Mr. Wallop] are necessarily 
absent.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Are there any other Senators in the 
Chamber desiring to vote?
  The result was announced--yeas 80, nays 11, as follows:
```
