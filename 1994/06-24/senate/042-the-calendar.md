# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Mitchell, George J. (M000811) [D-ME]

```
[Congressional Record: June 24, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. MITCHELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed, en bloc, to the immediate consideration of Calendar Nos. 485, 
486, 487, and 488; that the joint resolutions be read three times, and 
passed, and the motions to reconsider laid upon the table, en bloc; 
that the preambles be agreed to, en bloc; further, that any statements 
relating to these calendar items appear at the appropriate place in the 
Record, and, that the consideration of these items appear individually 
in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
