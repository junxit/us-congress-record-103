# SENATE RESOLUTION 178--INFORMING THE HOUSE THAT A QUORUM OF THE SENATE IS ASSEMBLED

**Measures:** S.Res. 178

```
[Congressional Record: January 25, 1994]
From the Congressional Record Online via GPO Access [wais.access.gpo.gov]

 
SENATE RESOLUTION 178--INFORMING THE HOUSE THAT A QUORUM OF THE SENATE 
                              IS ASSEMBLED

  Mr. MITCHELL (for himself and Mr. Dole) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:


                              S. Res. 178

       Resolved, That the Secretary inform the House of 
     Representatives that a quorum of the Senate is assembled and 
     that the Senate is ready to proceed to business.
```
